# Progressive Understanding Architecture

## Purpose and status

This document defines the cross-capability cognitive architecture through which
Management OS constructs operational understanding with the owner.

## Canonical principle

> Management OS constructs operational understanding progressively with the
> owner. It introduces each reasoning concept into the primary experience only
> when current Evidence, context, or owner intent makes that concept meaningful,
> while preserving direct access, uncertainty, history, and non-linear
> Investigation.

Progressive Understanding governs the relationship among three separate
questions:

1. Does a contribution exist authoritatively?
2. Is it meaningful in the current reasoning condition?
3. Does it deserve primary attention now?

Existence, cognitive readiness, and attention are never interchangeable. A
capability or record may remain directly inspectable without receiving primary
presentation. Presentation never creates source truth.

## Progressive Construction and disclosure

**Progressive Construction** is the governed emergence of the current operational
picture as Evidence, relationships, contradictions, explanations, Decisions,
impacts, Outcomes, and learning become meaningful.

**Progressive Disclosure** controls when already existing detail is shown.

For example, identifying a contradiction after Evidence sources conflict is
construction; opening the complete provenance of those sources is disclosure.
Construction changes the Board projection through source-owned contributions and
versioned reasoning. It never fabricates a record, silently changes authority, or
delays a material risk.

## Cognitive readiness

A concept is cognitively ready for primary presentation when the owner can
understand:

- what it means in this Matter;
- why it matters now;
- what Evidence, context, or reasoning supports it;
- what contribution, Evidence-acquisition action, or judgment is possible;
- what remains unresolved.

Watson must not introduce a question merely because a field is empty or a nominal
stage suggests it. The owner need not already possess the answer. The owner needs
enough orientation to understand the question's purpose and either answer it,
identify an Evidence source, or choose an appropriate acquisition action.

Material safety, operational risk, contradiction, uncertainty, source failure,
authority, and consequence override ordinary sequencing when delay would impair
judgment or action.

## Cognitive conditions

Operational understanding may naturally develop through conditions such as:

```text
Reported condition
  -> orientation and current operational condition
  -> Evidence needs and observed facts
  -> relationships, contradictions, and uncertainty
  -> possible and competing explanations
  -> decision basis and owner Decision
  -> operational impact and approved adaptations
  -> execution and observed Outcome
  -> learning
```

These are revisitable reasoning conditions, not Matter states, workflow stages,
completion gates, capability locks, or a promise that every Investigation follows
one order. Conditions may overlap, be skipped, or return after new Evidence.
Artifact count, answered-question count, and AI activity never establish progress
or readiness.

## Living Situation Board

The Living Situation Board combines:

- a stable and predictable spatial frame;
- a progressively constructed operational picture;
- one dominant cognitive focus;
- complete source, reasoning, history, and capability inspectability.

Initially, the primary picture may contain only the reported condition, preserved
owner wording, material urgency, and one orientation need. Evidence,
relationships, contradictions, Hypotheses, Decision Readiness, operational impact,
execution, Outcome, and learning enter primary presentation only as they become
meaningful. Previously constructed understanding remains traceable when emphasis
changes.

When operational topology becomes relevant, an Operational System Map may grow
inside this same projection contract. It begins with the smallest useful
Matter-relative boundary and introduces processes, roles, systems, controls,
flows, dependencies, and feedback only as they become cognitively meaningful.
Material unknown or disputed topology remains visible.

## Cross-capability application

- **Operational Situation Board** projects the living current picture without
  becoming a populated template.
- **Guided Investigation** gives one cognitively justified interaction dominant
  attention.
- **Operational Inquiry** selects an answerable or acquireable question whose
  purpose the owner can understand now.
- **Natural Capture** preserves owner wording and introduces only the minimum
  interpretation needed for the current contribution and handoff.
- **Decision Readiness** appears when Evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, and
  authority make owner judgment meaningful.
- **Operational Impact Review** appears only after an authoritative Decision,
  except that earlier risk exploration remains non-authoritative Decision work.
- **Execution, Outcome, and learning** enter the picture from their source-owned
  records and applicable reasoning conditions.
- **Operational Transition** progressively introduces intended condition,
  approved adaptations, execution Evidence, observed behavior, and stabilization
  only as those contributions become meaningful.

## Operational Language

Canonical documentation, contracts, audit, and specialist inspection use precise
**Architectural Language**. Ordinary owner interaction uses cognitively natural
**Operational Language** governed by the Product Language Guide.

Operational wording may simplify expression but never semantic consequence. A
request for approval cannot be softened into a generic next step; uncertainty
cannot be restated as fact; Watson proposal and owner Decision remain distinct.
Architectural terminology remains retrievable where precision or audit requires
it.

## Safeguards

1. Cognitive conditions never become lifecycle states or universal scores.
2. Capability access never depends on nominal progression.
3. Material risk, uncertainty, contradiction, source state, and authority remain
   visible when relevant.
4. Guidance changes are explainable, announced when material, and reversible.
5. Direct expert, structured, accessible, and degraded operation remain complete.
6. Progressive Construction changes projection and reasoning, not source truth.
7. Conversation supports the operational picture but never replaces the Board,
   source records, or reasoning history.
8. Owner redirect, defer, reject, alternative inspection, and strategy change
   remain available.

## Validation

Progressive Understanding succeeds when representative owners:

- orient to the current condition and next meaningful judgment without learning
  the interface architecture;
- can explain why the current question or concept appears now;
- can inspect Evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, authority, and history;
- do not mistake visual emergence for mandatory workflow completion;
- can bypass guidance without losing capability or context;
- retain the same essential understanding in degraded operation.

This architecture adds no entity, lifecycle state, scoring system, capability
gate, AI runtime, persistence, migration, interface, or autonomous authority.
