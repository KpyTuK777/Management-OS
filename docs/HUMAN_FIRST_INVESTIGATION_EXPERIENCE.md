## Canonical principle

> Management OS supports human operational reasoning before exposing system
> structure.

The owner begins with a natural account. Watson asks the smallest useful question,
summarizes material input, and invites correction. The Situation Board becomes
prominent only when visualization materially helps the owner hold shared
understanding.

Human-first does not mean chat-first. Conversation is optional, direct capability
access remains available, and material risk, contradiction, authority, privacy,
source failure, or consequential approval may interrupt the ordinary rhythm.

## First interaction

After a symptom is reported, primary presentation contains:

1. preserved owner wording;
2. one short Watson response;
3. one useful natural question;
4. one clear place for a free-form answer.

The answer may address one question, several questions, or supply multiple
observations. The interface never forces field-by-field completion merely to
populate architecture.

## Watson intervention contract

Each primary intervention has one purpose: ask, summarize, request correction,
identify missing information, explain necessary relevance, recommend one next
step, or surface a material concern.

The normal structure is:

- **What I understood:** one short, revisable summary;
- **What I need next:** one clear question;
- **Why this matters:** shown only when useful or necessary for safety.

Questions must be understandable on first reading, make the useful answer shape
clear, be capable of changing understanding or action, and remain smaller than
the problem they address.

Watson does not silently convert owner wording into authoritative fact. After
material input, the owner can confirm, correct, or add detail.

## Board timing and distinctions

The Board is the progressively constructed externalization of shared
understanding, not a complete framework displayed at the beginning. Detailed
construction remains governed by the
[Operational Situation Board Architecture](OPERATIONAL_SITUATION_BOARD_ARCHITECTURE.md).

Primary language and visual treatment distinguish reported symptom, owner or
witness observation, sourced Evidence with provenance, unverified assumption,
testable Hypothesis, and revisable Evidence-supported established understanding.
Future concepts do not appear as empty, locked, disabled, or placeholder regions.

## Access and safeguards

Conversation never gates Evidence, timeline, Hypotheses, Operating Model,
Decision Laboratory, history, or specialist records. Bulk contribution,
non-linear Investigation, redirection, deferral, rejection, and degraded operation
without Watson remain available.

Urgent risk, material contradiction, authorization, privacy, source failure,
Evidence invalidation, and consequential approval remain direct and may interrupt
the one-question rhythm.

## Validation

Using “Profit has fallen sharply during the last two months,” a first-time owner
must be able to identify without explanation what they reported, what Watson
understands and asks, why the question is useful, where to answer, what entered
the operational picture, what remains an assumption, and the next useful action.
Failure of any item blocks further capability expansion.
