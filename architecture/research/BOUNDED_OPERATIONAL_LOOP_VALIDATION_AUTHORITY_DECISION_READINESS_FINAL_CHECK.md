## 2. Readiness Standard

The input set is ready for authority consideration only when:

1. the Decision identity is stable and singular;
2. neutral decision support exists;
3. qualitative selection criteria and non-selection conditions exist;
4. authority questions expose required human judgment and missing Evidence;
5. an attributable record structure matches this exact Decision identity;
6. the structure preserves all non-effects and requires complete human inputs;
   and
7. no input silently selects a hypothesis, scenario, or authority effect.

Human answers do not need to exist before consideration can begin. However, the
record structure in which those answers and the resulting Decision would be
attributed must exist and must not concern a different Decision.
