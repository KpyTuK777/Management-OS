## 1. Purpose

This artifact defines a possible structure for a future attributable authority
record concerning whether bounded operational loop validation preparation may
begin. It identifies the decision identity, permitted dispositions, required
evidence, authority boundary, preservation conditions, and minimum record
content needed to prevent ambiguity.

It is not the future record itself. It does not make, recommend, approve, defer,
request evidence for, or decline the decision. It does not identify or select a
scenario, authorize preparation or validation, create a prototype, establish
Engineering Architecture or implementation authority, create M9, or create a
backlog task.

The word **authority** in the title describes the subject of the proposed future
record. This supporting structure possesses no authority.

## 5. Decision Identity

### 5.1 Decision under consideration

The future record would answer only:

> Should a bounded candidate-scenario identification and qualitative
> suitability-assessment preparation activity be authorized, deferred, held
> pending specifically requested additional decision evidence, or declined?

The preparation subject would be limited to producing evidence and decision
support for a later human authority consideration. It would not test operational
value or perform a Change.

### 5.2 Why this decision is distinct

The decision is distinct from scenario selection because no real candidate has
yet been chosen and preparation exists to make later consideration possible.

The decision is distinct from validation authorization because validation would
require a separately selected scenario and a fixed case-specific value claim,
Evidence boundary, Outcome, comparison, attribution posture, human and source
authority conditions, ethical and operational constraints, and explicit
execution exclusions.

The decision is distinct from validation execution because authorization alone
does not perform Investigation, make an operational Decision, execute a
source-owned Change, observe Outcome, or establish Learning.

The decision is distinct from Engineering Architecture and implementation
because preparation considers existing evidence and scenario suitability only.
It creates no technical realization need or authority.

### 5.3 Maximum decision effect

The future record could at most:

- authorize a bounded preparation activity under explicit constraints;
- preserve the current state through deferral;
- request named decision-relevant evidence within a bounded non-preparation
  scope; or
- decline the proposed preparation transition.

It could not create an automatic next step under any disposition.

## 6. Permitted Dispositions

A future record would select exactly one disposition. This research structure
selects none.

### 6.1 Authorized preparation

```text
Authorized preparation
```

**Meaning:** existing human authority permits only the bounded candidate-
identification and qualitative suitability-assessment preparation activity
specified in the record.

**Required qualifiers:**

- fixed preparation purpose and scope;
- accountable human owner of the preparation conclusion;
- existing-source and access boundary;
- qualitative, non-ranked assessment;
- explicit exclusions and termination conditions;
- no-candidate, rejection, and indeterminate results permitted;
- maximum output limited to decision support; and
- mandatory return to separate human authority before selection or validation.

**Does not mean:** scenario selected, validation approved, operational Change
authorized, Outcome predicted, prototype or implementation approved, or
successor work opened.

### 6.2 Deferred

```text
Deferred
```

**Meaning:** no preparation is authorized or declined; the decision remains open
for later human reconsideration.

**Required qualifiers:**

- reason for deferral;
- material uncertainty left unresolved;
- whether any reconsideration trigger exists, without creating a task or date
  automatically; and
- confirmation that deferral is neither approval nor rejection.

**Does not mean:** passive authorization, hidden preparation, negative finding,
or automatic future review.

### 6.3 Additional evidence requested

```text
Additional evidence requested
```

**Meaning:** the human authority identifies one or more bounded evidence
questions needed to decide among the dispositions. Preparation remains
unauthorized.

**Required qualifiers:**

- exact decision uncertainty;
- exact evidence requested;
- identified existing sources and applicable access authority;
- maximum permitted inference;
- explicit prohibition on candidate suitability assessment, selection,
  validation, prototype, and implementation;
- conditions for Insufficient or Indeterminate evidence; and
- return point for human decision.

**Does not mean:** open-ended research, candidate discovery, preparation,
instrumentation, data creation, implementation, or backlog authority.

### 6.4 Declined

```text
Declined
```

**Meaning:** the proposed transition from research to bounded preparation is not
authorized through this decision.

**Required qualifiers:**

- basis and scope of decline;
- whether decline concerns current evidence, current conditions, or the proposed
  preparation form;
- confirmation that no value hypothesis is thereby falsified; and
- confirmation that no alternative successor direction is authorized.

**Does not mean:** Management OS lacks value, candidate classes are invalid,
Engineering Architecture is authorized instead, or future research is
prohibited.

## 7. Disposition Integrity Rules

A future record would need to preserve these integrity rules:

1. Exactly one disposition is recorded.
2. The human authority and record identity are attributable.
3. The fixed evidence basis is identified by exact revision where available.
4. No disposition is inferred from silence, missing fields, status labels, or
   repository placement.
5. `Authorized preparation` is valid only with complete scope and boundary
   qualifiers.
6. `Additional evidence requested` identifies bounded questions and cannot
   create open-ended work.
7. `Deferred` creates no authority or automatic trigger.
8. `Declined` applies only to the named decision and cannot imply another
   direction.
9. A recommendation, assessor conclusion, score, model output, or majority view
   cannot substitute for the human disposition.
10. Any material scope change requires a new attributable human decision rather
    than silent amendment.

These are proposed clarity conditions for the future record, not a new
governance protocol.

## 9. Evidence Sufficiency Boundary

Evidence is sufficient for the preparation decision only when it supports the
chosen disposition and all material qualifiers. It does not need to establish
scenario suitability or value, because those are outside this decision.

### Sufficient for authorized preparation

The human would need enough evidence to determine that preparation can remain:

- bounded in purpose and scope;
- human-owned and non-authoritative in output;
- respectful of existing source and access authority;
- implementation-independent;
- qualitative and non-ranked;
- open to contrary, no-candidate, rejection, and indeterminate results;
- able to stop without selection or validation; and
- separate from all later decisions.

### Insufficient for authorized preparation

Authorization evidence is insufficient when:

- ownership or maximum scope is ambiguous;
- access or participation authority is assumed;
- preparation requires prototype, instrumentation, data model, or implementation;
- a selected or preferred candidate is already embedded without assessment;
- ranking or automated scoring would decide suitability;
- no-candidate or Indeterminate is not acceptable;
- preparation completion is expected to trigger validation;
- ethical, operational, privacy, or burden constraints are hidden; or
- exclusions and termination conditions are incomplete.

Insufficient authorization evidence does not determine which alternative
disposition should be chosen. That remains human judgment.

## 10. Authority Boundary

### 10.1 What the future record may decide

The future record may decide only whether bounded validation preparation is:

- authorized within explicitly recorded conditions;
- deferred;
- held pending bounded additional evidence; or
- declined.

For `Authorized preparation`, it may define:

- preparation purpose;
- evidence and operational boundary;
- accountable preparation owner;
- permitted candidate-identification and assessment activities;
- explicit exclusions;
- termination conditions;
- maximum output; and
- return-to-authority requirement.

For the other dispositions, it may define only the qualifiers necessary to make
that disposition clear.

### 10.2 What the future record may not decide

The record may not:

- select or rank a candidate class or real scenario;
- approve or execute validation;
- choose an operational Decision or Change;
- grant source access or mutation authority beyond existing applicable authority;
- predict or record a validation Outcome;
- claim that Management OS creates value or caused an Outcome;
- create a product-readiness or implementation-readiness claim;
- authorize Engineering Architecture, prototype, implementation, delivery,
  staffing, schedule, priority, or release;
- create a canonical scenario, semantic object, capability, taxonomy,
  assessment framework, lifecycle, or protocol;
- amend governance or establish a new authority;
- resolve M8.4 Gaps, accept or treat Risks, adopt Recommendations, or select M8.5
  alternatives;
- assign an M8.6 readiness class;
- modify canonical M7 or M8.1–M8.7;
- create M9, a successor milestone, stage, transition, or backlog task; or
- combine preparation, selection, validation approval, and execution into one
  decision.

## 11. Human Authority Requirements

A future record would need to identify an existing accountable human authority
under canonical governance. It must not create a new role or authority merely to
make the record effective.

The human authority should attest that:

- the decision and evidence were independently considered;
- no score, AI output, recommendation, or research artifact selected the
  disposition;
- supporting, contrary, missing, and conflicting Evidence remained visible;
- the chosen disposition is bounded by its recorded meaning;
- human and source ownership remain preserved;
- ethical and operational constraints were considered;
- no hidden scenario selection or implementation commitment is present; and
- later decisions remain separate.

This research structure does not supply the authority identity or attestation.

## 13. Disposition-Specific Minimum Content

### If `Authorized preparation`

The record would need:

- authorized purpose and scope;
- accountable preparation owner;
- candidate-discovery and source boundary;
- access and participation conditions already valid under applicable authority;
- qualitative assessment method without score or rank;
- permitted and prohibited activities;
- termination conditions;
- maximum output;
- explicit no-selection and no-validation boundary; and
- required return to separate human authority.

### If `Deferred`

The record would need:

- reason for deferral;
- material unresolved consideration;
- explicit no-authority effect; and
- no automatic trigger, task, or schedule.

### If `Additional evidence requested`

The record would need:

- exact decision question;
- bounded existing evidence requested;
- source and access authority;
- permitted inference;
- prohibition on hidden preparation and implementation;
- Insufficient and Indeterminate outcomes; and
- return point for human decision.

### If `Declined`

The record would need:

- bounded basis for decline;
- scope of the decline;
- confirmation that value is not thereby falsified;
- confirmation that no alternative direction is authorized; and
- confirmation that no successor or work follows.
