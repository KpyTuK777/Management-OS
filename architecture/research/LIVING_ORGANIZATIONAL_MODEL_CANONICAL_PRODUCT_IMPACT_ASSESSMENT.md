# Living Organizational Model canonical and Product impact assessment

## Assessment state

Non-canonical research assessment. It prepares a human Decision and creates no adoption, implementation, roadmap or backlog authority.

## Existing repository support

The fixed repository already supplies most lower-level semantics needed for the proposed upper composition:

| Existing concept | Reused responsibility |
|---|---|
| Artifact | Typed, attributable operational object without collapsing all objects into generic content. |
| Relationship | Bounded, explicit links whose appearance does not establish causality or truth. |
| Matter | Persistent envelope for related operational concern and continuity across Investigation episodes. |
| Current Situation / Situation Board | Progressive, inspectable current understanding, including uncertainty and contradiction. |
| Investigation | Bounded inquiry episode; material, Evidence, Working Set, hypotheses and judgment boundaries remain distinct. |
| Decision / Intervention / Change | Accountable human judgment and source-owned real-world action remain separate. |
| Monitoring / Outcome / Stabilization | Observation after change without automatic success or causal claims. |
| Closure / Reopening | Bounded disposition and later resumption without erasing history. |
| Operational Memory / Knowledge | Preserved provenance, interpretation, Decision, Change, Outcome and learning. |
| Operational Operating Model / System | Processes, roles, systems, dependencies and intended operating condition. |
| P1–P6 | Existing responsibility views: experience/contribution, context/model, Investigation, Decision/change/outcome, learning/memory, assistance/adaptation. |

These concepts were present but not composed into a persistent organization-level starting layer. The gap is not evidence for a seventh product layer. P1 remains the primary owner of coherent experience, P2 of operational context/model, P3 Investigation, P4 Decision/change/outcome, P5 learning/memory and P6 bounded assistance.

## Genuine responsibility gap

The repository lacks one explicit product composition and durable context identity: a progressively refinable, organization-scoped operational model that remains present before, during and after individual Matters, Investigations and Improvements. Existing Operating Model and Operational System sources support much of its content, but do not currently define this organization-level product workspace, its construction experience, or its feedback contract with completed operational cycles.

Disposition: **new canonical concept candidate plus new Product composition**, not merely a new Matter type. The candidate concept is `Living Organizational Model`: the current, historically grounded, explicitly incomplete organizational context assembled from governed references and attributable observations. It does not become a universal source of truth or replace the semantic owners of people, systems, processes, SOPs, Evidence, Decisions or Memory.

## Proposed upper-layer model

```text
Living Organizational Model
  ↕ continuous understanding and observation
  ├─ Investigation — “I need to understand.”
  └─ Improvement — “I understand enough to consider deliberate change.”
          ↓
     accountable human Decision
          ↓
     source-owned Implementation / Intervention
          ↓
     Monitoring → Stabilization or further inquiry
          ↓
     Closure disposition and Operational Memory
          ↓
     reviewed update to Living Organizational Model
          ↓
     continued operational control
```

This is a graph of responsibilities, not a mandatory linear workflow. Monitoring may reveal uncertainty; Improvement may open Investigation; Investigation may create an optional Improvement; stabilization may fail or remain indeterminate; closure may later reopen.

## Investigation and Improvement

They should be sibling work modes sharing organizational context but not lifecycle semantics.

- Investigation begins from an unclear condition and protects the possibility that no negative root cause exists.
- Improvement begins from an opportunity or intent and protects the possibility that the current system works, change is optional, benefit is uncertain and new risks may exceed value.
- Organizational context inherited by either mode remains context; it is not automatically material or accepted Evidence.
- A transition creates a new linked mode with an attributable reason and preserved source context; it never silently converts one record into the other.

## Complete-cycle reconciliation

Decision remains human; Implementation/Intervention remains source-owned; Monitoring records observations rather than proving causality; Stabilization is an observed condition, not UI completion; Closure records disposition without erasing uncertainty; Operational Memory preserves the historical chain. Updating the Living Organizational Model requires a reviewed projection from the governed sources and cannot rewrite historical snapshots.

## Person-as-Source assessment

Artifact, Relationship, provenance, Investigation and Operational Memory supply most primitives, but the repository does not currently fix a relationship that means “this exact person provided this information in this historical organizational capacity.” A bounded relationship candidate is required: `Information Contribution`.

It differs from:

- authorship: who created a document;
- ownership: who controls an artifact or system;
- responsibility: who is accountable for work;
- subject: who or what the information concerns;
- recorder: who entered the information into Management OS.

The relationship must store immutable contribution-time person, role and department references/snapshots, time, form, directness and limitations, while linking to the current identities. Later role changes must not mutate history. Later support, contradiction or supersession must be separate attributable relationships or statuses, never a computed reliability score.

## Required semantic changes

Canonical adoption would minimally require:

1. define Living Organizational Model responsibility, incompleteness and source boundaries;
2. define Improvement as a distinct operational mode and its transition contracts;
3. define organization-context inheritance and feedback after Stabilization/Closure;
4. define Information Contribution and historical organizational-capacity integrity;
5. clarify privacy, sensitive observations and human-judgment constraints.

No change is required to Investigation internals, Evidence acceptance, Working Set meaning, Decision authority, source-owned Change, Monitoring caution, Operational Memory provenance, P1–P6 or Workbench Laws.

## Impact disposition

- Canonical: candidate additions/reconciliation required; not adopted.
- Product: material new top-level composition and sibling Improvement mode.
- Interaction: Organization Constructor, Organization Map, Department/Person Workbenches and context-aware entry contracts require definition.
- Engineering: identity, temporal snapshots, relationship ownership, privacy and projection contracts require assessment; no topology selected.
- Implementation: not ready and not authorized; current Investigation MVP remains a valid slice.
