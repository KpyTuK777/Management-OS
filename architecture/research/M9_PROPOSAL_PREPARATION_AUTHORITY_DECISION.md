## 5. Disposition-Specific Minimum Requirements

Every future record must preserve the exact Decision identity, all fixed
evidence identities, all mandatory human-owned fields, explicit non-effects,

### A — Authorized preparation

- preserve empirical product-value learning as the proposal focus;
- limit proposal content to possible intent, scope, boundaries, Decision
  identities, and non-effects;

- attest that preparation is not proposal approval, M9 opening, hypothesis or

  roadmap, or backlog authority.

### B — Deferred

The human-owned record must:

- state that neither authorization nor refusal is presently issued;
- give the human-owned reason for deferral;
- state any reconsideration condition, or explicitly record that none is set;
  and
- confirm that time or condition occurrence cannot automatically authorize
  preparation.

### C — Additional evidence requested

The human-owned record must:

- identify the exact additional evidence and why it is material;
- state the acceptable source, fixed-identity expectation, and scope;
- identify any separate authority needed to obtain it; and
- confirm that the request itself authorizes no evidence collection,
  validation, experiment, implementation, or external action and cannot
  automatically select another disposition.

### D — Declined

The human-owned record must:

- state that preparation is not authorized under the current identity and
  evidence basis;
- give the human-owned reason for declining;
- state any reconsideration boundary, or explicitly record that none is set;
  and
- confirm that decline does not erase research history or decide a materially
  different future question.

Mixed, conditional, implicit, or multiple disposition selections are invalid
under this shell and require direct human clarification.
