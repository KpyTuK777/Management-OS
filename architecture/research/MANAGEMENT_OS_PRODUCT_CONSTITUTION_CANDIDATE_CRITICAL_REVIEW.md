## 4. Product Identity Fidelity

**Result: Preserved.**

The two-sentence identity defines Management OS as “the operational manager's ecosystem” and continues its work through operational reality, Evidence, human Decision, source-owned Change, observed Outcome, and governed Learning. The longer identity gives facts, observations, hypotheses, reports, notes, voice notes, correspondence, Decisions, Changes, Outcomes, and experience an appropriate governed place while allowing that place to be an internal record or governed source reference.

The candidate therefore preserves both ecosystem breadth and relational continuity. It does not reduce the product to accumulation: Sections 1–4 emphasize preserved context and transitions, and Section 10 distinguishes the product from storage-oriented or capability-specific categories without denying that they may contribute.

Decision is not terminal. The loop continues through bounded Change, Outcome observation and verification, reflection, Learning, and improved future capability. Failed expectations are named in Section 1; Section 5 distinguishes desired and observed results; and Section 7 exposes indeterminate Outcomes. Together these preserve expected-versus-observed difference as potential experience rather than success labeling.

Section 4 rejects one mandatory Root Cause and describes multiple related and differently scoped problems within an operational system. It does not turn that rejection into a required method.

## 7. Human Authority and AI Boundary

**Result: Preserved.**

Human Decision is explicit and accountable. Approval and consent cannot be inferred. AI output cannot become Evidence or fact, and AI cannot become owner, accountable decision-maker, source, approval, transition, causal, success, or governance authority.

The allowed AI actions—organize, retrieve, compare, explain, challenge, identify missing context, prepare alternatives, surface uncertainty, and support reflection—are assistance verbs. They neither establish Outcome nor verify causality or fidelity autonomously. The candidate also states that AI fluency, confidence, or completeness cannot substitute for Evidence, consent, attribution, or human judgment.

The candidate does not claim that AI can make context complete, identify a correct Decision, produce a successful Change, or certify Learning.

The broad-place proposition and ownership proposition are held together rather than traded off. ## 9. Product Promises, Outcome, and Learning

**Result: Preserved.**

Section 3 expressly defines promises as support commitments rather than guarantees. Section 8 excludes complete truth, optimal Decisions, successful Change, correct causal attribution, unbiased judgment, sufficient authority, reliable external systems, and implementation readiness from architectural coherence. It also denies that coherent research proves real-world product validation.

Outcome remains an observed consequence rather than a status label. The candidate distinguishes activity, completion, desired result, observed result, actual Change, Outcome, and causal attribution. It preserves contrary Evidence, uncertainty, and indeterminate Outcomes, and does not collapse positive or negative Outcomes into retrospective judgment of the Decision.

Learning does not arise automatically from Outcome. The product-understanding loop places Outcome observation and verification before reflection and governed Learning; Section 4 says Outcome is not Learning by itself; and Section 7 requires preservation of the episode, contrary evidence, and uncertainty. Sections 5 and 9 preserve correction, human judgment, and bounded AI participation.

## 13. Advisory Observations

### Advisory PC-01 — The shortest transition invariant omits the authorization layers

**Exact evidence:** Section 4 says, “Recommendation is not Decision, Decision is not Change, Change is not Outcome.”

**Observation:** In context, Sections 3, 5, and 7 preserve Decision authority, source ownership, intended Change, and actual Change. There is no semantic collapse. Quoted alone, however, the compact invariant does not expose the more precise boundaries `Decision ≠ Change authorization` and `Change authorization ≠ Actual Change`.

**Consequence:** Out-of-context reuse could compress distinct Decision and mutation authorities into a single Decision-to-Change edge.

**Classification:** Advisory only. The complete candidate preserves the boundary; no correction is required by this review.

### Advisory PC-02 — “Source-owned and bounded Change” depends on surrounding distinctions

**Exact evidence:** The identity and loop use “source-owned Change” and “source-owned and bounded Change,” while Section 7 separately distinguishes intended from actual Change.

**Observation:** The phrase correctly protects source ownership but does not itself say whether the Change is intended, authorized, executed, or observed.

**Consequence:** A reader extracting only the two-sentence identity or loop could read “Change” more broadly than the rest of the candidate permits.

**Classification:** Advisory only. Sections 3, 5, and 7 provide the necessary distinctions, so no blocking ambiguity exists in the artifact as a whole.

### Advisory PC-03 — The Learning commitment is safer with its neighboring loop language

**Exact evidence:** Section 7 says, “Every Learning claim should preserve the historical episode, contrary evidence, and uncertainty.” Section 4 separately places “reflection and governed Learning” after Outcome observation and verification.

**Observation:** The commitment sentence alone does not repeat applicable Evidence, human review, or Learning authority. Those constraints are supplied by Sections 4, 5, 6, and 9.

**Consequence:** Isolated quotation could make preservation appear sufficient for justified Learning when the research requires evidence, reflection, and bounded human judgment.

**Classification:** Advisory only. The candidate as a whole does not make Learning automatic or AI-owned.

### Advisory PC-04 — The category descriptions are intentionally high-level generalizations

**Exact evidence:** Section 10 describes what task/project management, notes/documents, BI, workflow automation, knowledge bases, and AI assistants “primarily” do.

**Observation:** Individual products may span several of these roles. The qualifying word “primarily,” the allowance that these categories may contribute capabilities, and the explicit non-competitive disclaimer appropriately limit the generalization.

**Consequence:** Reuse without those qualifications could sound like an unsupported market claim.

**Classification:** Advisory only. The actual section is neutral and adequately bounded.
