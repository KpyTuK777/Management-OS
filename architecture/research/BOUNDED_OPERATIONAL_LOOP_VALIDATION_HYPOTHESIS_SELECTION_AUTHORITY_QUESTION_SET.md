## 1. Purpose and Evidence Basis

The purpose is to help a real accountable human determine whether the first
bounded validation-hypothesis selection question is sufficiently understood and,
only if so, make a separate attributable Decision.

The question set uses:

- `BOUNDED_OPERATIONAL_LOOP_VALIDATION_HYPOTHESIS_SELECTION_DECISION_SUPPORT.md`;
- `BOUNDED_OPERATIONAL_LOOP_VALIDATION_HYPOTHESIS_SELECTION_CRITERIA_REFINEMENT.md`;
- `POST_M8_OPERATIONAL_LOOP_DECISION_SUPPORT_SUMMARY.md`; and
- `BOUNDED_OPERATIONAL_LOOP_PREPARATION_AUTHORITY_DECISION.md`.

The sources remain unchanged. Canonical M7 and M8 remain controlling. Questions
do not create authority, evidence, facts, answers, requirements, or work.

## 2. How the Question Set May Be Used

The questions are grouped to expose distinct evidence and authority propositions.
They are not a checklist whose completion automatically creates readiness or a
Decision.

A future human may answer:

- with attributable Evidence;
- with an explicit human judgment and rationale;
- `unknown`;
- `not applicable`, with rationale;
- `additional evidence required`; or
- `cannot be responsibly determined`.

An unanswered or indeterminate question must remain visible. AI must not fill it
with a likely answer, inferred authority, generated rationale, or confidence
score.

The question order is not ranking. No question carries an automatic weight, and
strength in one area cannot compensate for absence of a non-substitutable
condition such as accountable ownership, falsifiability, observable Outcome, or
responsible exposure.
