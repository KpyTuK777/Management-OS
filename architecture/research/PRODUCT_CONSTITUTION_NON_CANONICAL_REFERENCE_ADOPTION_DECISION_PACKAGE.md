## 3. Proposed Permissible Uses

If the role were separately adopted, the following uses could remain non-decisional:

1. **Orient product discussion:** provide a compact starting picture before participants consult authoritative sources.
2. **Examine whole-loop alignment:** ask whether a concept considers context, Evidence, understanding, Decision, Change, Outcome, and Learning without using the answer as a verdict.
3. **Surface product-philosophy tension:** identify a possible inconsistency, omission, or uncertainty for investigation by the appropriate human and source owner.
4. **Explain the product to contributors:** supplement onboarding or discussion with explicit non-canonical classification.
5. **Prompt canonical consultation:** direct readers to Product Vision, Systems, canonical M8, or source-owned architecture for authoritative purpose, responsibility, semantic, or architectural detail.

Each use would support inquiry or explanation only. None could approve, reject, prioritize, require, authorize, validate, or establish conformance.
