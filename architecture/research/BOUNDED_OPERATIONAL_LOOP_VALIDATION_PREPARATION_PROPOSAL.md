# Bounded Operational Loop Validation Preparation Proposal

## Proposal Status

```text
Proposal for separate human authority consideration only
Implementation-independent, non-authoritative, and non-normative
No preparation authorization, scenario selection, validation authorization or execution, milestone, backlog, or work authorization
```

## 1. Purpose

This proposal describes what would be necessary to move from completed
architectural research to a separately authorized bounded validation-preparation
activity. It organizes preconditions, required evidence, authority decisions,
scope, anticipated materials, risks, and exclusions for future human
consideration.

It does not open or perform preparation. It does not identify, assess, rank, or
select a real operational scenario. It does not authorize validation, create a
prototype, define Engineering Architecture or implementation, create a backlog
task, or establish M9 or another milestone.

## 2. Research Basis

The proposal is based on seven completed non-authoritative artifacts:

1. **Bounded Operational Value and Evidence Proposition — Architectural
   Research**;
2. **Bounded Operational Loop Selection and Validation Criteria — Architectural
   Research**;
3. **Operational Loop Validation Protocol — Architectural Research**;
4. **Bounded Operational Loop Validation Scenario — Architectural Research**;
5. **Bounded Operational Loop Scenario Assessment Framework — Architectural
   Research**;
6. **Candidate Operational Loop Classes — Research Analysis**; and
7. **Candidate Operational Loop Consideration Brief**.

The research establishes a coherent basis for considering preparation:

- a bounded value hypothesis centered on management capacity and observed
  operational consequence;
- a complete-loop trace from Operational Reality through verified Outcome and
  Learning;
- candidate selection and rejection considerations;
- Evidence sufficiency and semantic boundaries;
- positive, negative, null, interrupted, and indeterminate result posture;
- attribution, bounded-contribution, bias, and validity limits;
- qualitative scenario-assessment dimensions without scoring or ranking; and
- candidate-class and human-consideration material.

The research does not supply a real scenario, case Evidence, access, consent,
authority, Outcome, comparison, preparation plan, or validation approval.

## 3. Preserved Architectural Boundaries

Any future preparation consideration would remain bounded by canonical M7 and
M8.1–M8.7. This proposal does not change them.

The following remain fixed:

- Outcome is the final consequence test for a claimed operational effect.
- Learning follows attributable experience and verified Outcome.
- Observation, Evidence, Interpretation, hypothesis, Modeled / Scenario Output,
  Recommendation, Decision, Change record, Outcome, and Learning remain distinct
  semantic kinds.
- Decision remains accountable human judgment; Management OS and AI do not
  decide.
- Facts, records, and mutation remain source-owned.
- A bounded contribution claim remains distinct from unique causal attribution.
- One Root Cause is not required.
- Operational Diagnosis remains research framing for a possible composition of
  existing responsibilities and is not a canonical object.
- Research, suitability, recommendation, selection, authorization, preparation,
  execution, Outcome verification, and later transition remain distinct acts.

## 4. Proposed Meaning of Validation Preparation

Validation preparation would be a bounded evidence activity whose purpose is to
make a later human authority decision possible. It would determine whether one
real candidate scenario can be described and evidenced sufficiently for an
authority to decide whether a separate bounded validation should be considered.

Preparation would not test the Management OS value proposition. It would test
only whether the proposition can be fixed responsibly and investigated
credibly for a real candidate.

The intended preparation question would be:

> Is there at least one real operational candidate with bounded ownership,
> attributable and contestable Evidence, genuine human Decision, a plausibly
> reversible source-owned Change, observable Outcome, credible comparison,
> manageable attribution limits, and Learning potential that may be presented
> to a separate human authority for a validation decision?

An acceptable preparation conclusion could be that no candidate is suitable or
that available Evidence is indeterminate.

## 5. Preconditions

Preparation should not be considered until all preconditions below can be
addressed under existing authority without inventing missing canonical meaning.

### 5.1 Research-completeness precondition

- The seven research artifacts remain fixed, attributable, and available as the
  non-authoritative basis.
- Their boundaries and differences are understood: value proposition, loop
  criteria, protocol principles, scenario criteria, assessment framework,
  class analysis, and consideration brief.
- No research statement is treated as a canonical requirement, governance rule,
  scenario selection, or validation approval.
- Known tensions or contradictions among the research artifacts, if found, are
  made explicit before reliance.

### 5.2 Purpose precondition

The preparation purpose must be fixed narrowly enough to prevent drift:

- identify and describe real candidate scenarios;
- collect existing suitability Evidence rather than value results;
- assess candidates qualitatively against disclosed dimensions;
- expose rejection conditions, uncertainty, burden, harm, bias, and attribution
  limits;
- support a later human decision; and
- stop before selection or validation unless a distinct authority decision
  explicitly permits the next action.

The purpose must not be phrased as proving value, ensuring a candidate proceeds,
building a prototype, or discovering implementation requirements.

### 5.3 Authority precondition

Applicable existing human authority under canonical M7 would need to make an
explicit bounded decision on whether preparation may begin. The decision would
need to identify:

- the preparation purpose and maximum permitted conclusion;
- the evidence and operational boundary that may be examined;
- the accountable human for the preparation conclusion;
- the sources and participants whose existing authority must be respected;
- explicit exclusions and termination conditions; and
- the return point for a later, distinct human consideration.

This proposal does not identify a new authority, amend governance, or make that
decision.

### 5.4 Operational-access precondition

Before preparation, there must be a credible basis to believe candidate
information can be considered lawfully and responsibly under existing
authority, including:

- access to source-owned operational facts and records where applicable;
- preservation of provenance, semantic meaning, correction, and limitations;
- ability to retain contrary, missing, null, negative, and adverse material;
- visibility of affected people and authority boundaries;
- proportionate handling of privacy, confidentiality, sensitivity, and burden;
  and
- freedom to conclude that access or evidence is insufficient.

This does not authorize access or define data handling. Those conditions would
need to be established separately under applicable authority.

### 5.5 Candidate-plurality precondition

Where reasonably possible, preparation should be able to examine more than one
candidate. Considering only one candidate chosen for convenience or expected
success risks turning assessment into confirmation.

Plurality does not require ranking or selection. It permits human comparison of
material differences and preserves the possibility that no candidate is
suitable.

### 5.6 Human-authority precondition

- Candidate identification may be informed by people and source Evidence.
- Scenario assessment remains human, explicit, evidence-backed, and
  challengeable.
- AI may organize or summarize stated Evidence but cannot score, rank, select,
  approve, or infer suitability from confidence.
- Management OS cannot become the Decision owner or source of truth because it
  is the subject of future inquiry.
- Operational Decision and source-mutation authority remain outside preparation.

### 5.7 Non-implementation precondition

Preparation must be possible using existing research and attributable existing
operational evidence. If a candidate can be made assessable only by designing a
prototype, instrument, interface, service, data model, workflow, or technical
realization, preparation would exceed this proposal's boundary.

The inability to proceed without implementation may be recorded as a limitation.
It does not authorize implementation.

## 6. Data Needed Before Preparation

The following information should be available or credibly obtainable before a
preparation authority decision. These are information needs, not a canonical
schema or collection plan.

### 6.1 Research identities and boundaries

- exact repository identities of the seven research artifacts;
- their non-authoritative status and exclusions;
- canonical M7 and M8 constraints relied upon; and
- any known interpretive disagreement or missing research basis.

### 6.2 Candidate-source landscape

- operational areas in which recurrent bounded deviations may exist;
- accountable operational owners willing and authorized to provide context;
- source systems or records that may contain pre-Decision and post-Change
  Evidence;
- known access, sensitivity, privacy, correction, and provenance limitations;
- likely candidate classes without assuming class suitability; and
- reasons candidates may be visible or absent.

### 6.3 Candidate-level preliminary facts

For each possible candidate, enough information should exist to consider:

- real operational condition, timing, scope, recurrence, and material
  consequence;
- accountable human Decision owner;
- owners of relevant facts, records, and prospective Change;
- plausible alternatives and genuine uncertainty;
- pre-existing Evidence and known missing or contrary material;
- possible bounded and responsibly reversible Change surface;
- possible intended condition, Outcome source, verification owner, and horizon;
- baseline, trajectory, comparable instances, or intended range;
- external factors, concurrent interventions, burden, and harm; and
- plausible later comparable situation for Learning.

These facts support preparation suitability only. They are not value Evidence
and do not make the candidate selected.

### 6.4 Decision-request facts

- what authority decision is being requested now;
- what decisions are explicitly deferred;
- what may stop or invalidate preparation;
- who may decline participation or access;
- what maximum conclusion preparation may return; and
- how records will remain distinct from canonical and operational source
  authority.

## 7. Authority Decisions Required

Three future decisions must remain separate.

### 7.1 Decision A — authorize or decline preparation

The first possible decision would determine only whether a bounded
candidate-identification and suitability-assessment activity may begin. It
would fix preparation scope, evidence boundary, human accountability,
exclusions, and return condition.

It would not select a scenario or authorize validation.

### 7.2 Decision B — select, reject, defer, or request more Evidence

After preparation, a separate human authority could consider candidate evidence
and may:

- select one candidate for possible validation consideration;
- reject one or all candidates;
- defer because Evidence is indeterminate;
- request more bounded Evidence without creating implementation; or
- conclude that no candidate is currently suitable.

This decision remains human. Suitability evidence and an assessor's
recommendation may inform it but cannot make or authorize it.

Selection would not approve validation execution.

### 7.3 Decision C — authorize or decline bounded validation

Only after a candidate were separately selected could another explicit decision
consider whether bounded validation may be prepared further or executed under a
fixed claim, Evidence boundary, Outcome, comparison, attribution posture,
authority conditions, and exclusions.

That decision is outside this proposal. It must not be bundled with Decisions A
or B.

### 7.4 Later decisions remain external

No positive preparation or validation outcome would itself authorize
Engineering Architecture, implementation, product readiness, delivery, or a
successor milestone. Each would require its own applicable authority basis.

## 8. Proposed Validation-Preparation Scope

If separately authorized later, preparation could include only the bounded work
described here.

### 8.1 Included scope

- confirm the exact research and canonical constraint basis;
- identify more than one real candidate where possible without selecting one;
- describe each candidate's operational reality, scope, ownership, recurrence,
  and materiality;
- inventory existing source Evidence and material limitations;
- identify genuine human Decision and possible source-owned Change boundaries;
- assess Outcome observability, comparison feasibility, attribution threats,
  Learning potential, burden, harm, and confounding;
- apply the qualitative scenario-assessment dimensions without score or rank;
- record supporting, contrary, missing, conflicting, and inaccessible Evidence;
- apply explicit non-substitutable rejection conditions;
- compare candidates narratively without declaring a winner;
- prepare a decision-support package for separate human consideration; and
- record a bounded recommendation about whether each candidate is sufficiently
  evidenced for authority consideration.

### 8.2 Excluded scope

- selecting or approving a scenario;
- defining or executing validation;
- choosing an operational Change;
- influencing or making the operational Decision;
- collecting Outcome results from a Change performed for validation;
- creating new semantic kinds, canonical objects, capabilities, or authority;
- creating a lifecycle, governance process, protocol, review gate, or readiness
  class;
- resolving M8.4 Gaps, accepting or treating Risks, adopting Recommendations,
  or selecting M8.5 alternatives;
- designing a prototype, UI, API, schema, data model, service, runtime,
  infrastructure, instrumentation, or deployment;
- defining Engineering Architecture, implementation, delivery, priority,
  staffing, schedule, or roadmap;
- creating M9, a milestone, stage, order, or backlog item; and
- making product-wide value, causal, readiness, or implementation claims.

### 8.3 Preparation termination conditions

Preparation should be able to stop with no selection recommendation when:

- no real material candidate exists;
- Decision or source ownership is ambiguous;
- material Evidence is inaccessible or semantically invalid;
- Outcome cannot be observed and owned for verification;
- no credible comparison or attribution path exists;
- exposure is safety-critical, disproportionate, or irreversible;
- implementation would be required merely to assess suitability;
- participants, sources, or authority conditions cannot be respected;
- assessment becomes ranking, selection, or pressure to proceed; or
- supporting and contrary Evidence remain insufficient for bounded judgment.

Stopping is a valid preparation result, not failure requiring scope expansion.

## 9. Scenario Decision Boundary

### Assessment is not selection

Assessment describes what Evidence supports, contradicts, or leaves unknown for
one fixed candidate. It cannot choose that candidate.

### Comparison is not ranking

Narrative comparison exposes material differences. It cannot assign aggregate
scores, weights, priority, or a winner.

### Recommendation is not authorization

An assessor may recommend that a candidate be presented for human
consideration, rejected, or held indeterminate. The recommendation cannot
authorize selection, validation, access, participation, Change, or execution.

### Suitability is not validation approval

Suitability means the available Evidence suggests a credible bounded value
inquiry could be formulated. It does not show that the value hypothesis is true,
that Outcome will improve, or that validation is approved.

### Selection remains human

Only the applicable accountable human authority may select, reject, defer, or
request further Evidence. AI, Management OS, score, ranking, assessor, research
artifact, participant enthusiasm, data abundance, or expected success cannot
make that Decision.

### Validation approval remains separate

Selection identifies a candidate for possible consideration. It does not start
validation or authorize an operational Change.

## 10. Required Materials Before Preparation Starts

The following materials would be needed before a preparation activity could
begin. This proposal does not create them.

### 10.1 Authority record

A repository-visible, attributable decision under existing governance that:

- authorizes preparation only;
- fixes purpose, scope, accountable human, evidence boundary, exclusions, and
  termination conditions;
- distinguishes preparation from selection and validation; and
- creates no successor, implementation, or delivery authority.

### 10.2 Fixed research-basis register

A bounded list of the seven research artifacts with exact identities, status,
scope, exclusions, and relationship to canonical M7 and M8. This would establish
attribution, not canonical authority.

### 10.3 Preparation boundary statement

A concise statement of:

- requested decision;
- candidate classes initially in view without preference;
- operational and source-access boundaries;
- maximum permitted conclusion;
- prohibited claims and activities; and
- required return to human authority.

### 10.4 Source and participation conditions

Evidence that applicable owners permit the bounded consideration of relevant
existing information and that participation, confidentiality, correction,
privacy, burden, and withdrawal or refusal conditions are addressed under
existing applicable authority.

This paper does not define those conditions or create consent and data-handling
mechanics.

### 10.5 Candidate discovery material

Existing non-solution-led information about possible recurrent operational
conditions, materiality, ownership, Evidence sources, Outcome visibility,
comparison, and known exclusions. It should permit more than one candidate to
be considered where possible.

### 10.6 Assessment structure

An attributable copy or bounded use statement for the research assessment
dimensions, qualitative expressions, non-substitutable boundaries, and
decision-boundary language. It must not convert the research framework into a
canonical protocol or automatic score.

### 10.7 Decision-support output outline

An outline for presenting:

- fixed candidate descriptions;
- supporting, contrary, missing, and conflicting Evidence;
- dimension-level reasoning;
- rejection and attribution conditions;
- burden, harm, bias, and uncertainty;
- narrative comparison without rank;
- maximum permitted inference; and
- recommendation for human consideration only.

This is a proposed information shape, not a new required artifact type or data
model.

## 11. Possible Preparation Outputs

If preparation were authorized and completed, it could return one bounded
package containing:

- attributable candidate descriptions;
- evidence-source and limitation summaries;
- qualitative suitability assessments;
- explicit rejection findings where supported;
- cross-candidate narrative comparison without ranking;
- attribution-feasibility and bias analysis;
- unresolved conditions and indeterminate dimensions;
- a statement that no candidate is suitable, if supported; and
- a recommendation about which candidates, if any, may be presented for a
  separate human selection decision.

The package would not contain a validation result, selected scenario,
authorization, implementation specification, prototype, or product readiness
claim.

## 12. Risks and Research Responses

### 12.1 Premature implementation

**Risk:** candidate Evidence gaps trigger prototype, instrumentation, UI, data,
or runtime design, silently converting preparation into implementation planning.

**Boundary response:** use existing attributable Evidence; record unavailable
Evidence and stop when implementation is required. Missing evidence is not
implementation authority.

### 12.2 False causal claims

**Risk:** a recurrent condition and possible Outcome are narrated as proof that
Management OS would cause improvement.

**Boundary response:** preparation assesses attribution feasibility only. It
produces no Outcome evidence. Preserve alternative explanations and distinguish
association, plausible contribution, material contribution, and causality.

### 12.3 Insufficient Evidence

**Risk:** convenient records, participant confidence, activity volume, or AI
summaries substitute for attributable and semantically valid Evidence.

**Boundary response:** retain missing, conflicting, contrary, null, negative,
and adverse material. Use Indeterminate rather than favorable inference. Reject
a candidate when non-substitutable Evidence cannot exist.

### 12.4 Scenario bias

**Risk:** a candidate is favored because it is visible, data-rich, supported by
an enthusiastic owner, easy to implement, already improving, or likely to
succeed.

**Boundary response:** consider multiple candidates where possible, record why
each entered consideration, apply the same qualitative dimensions, preserve
rejection conditions, and prohibit scores and rank.

### 12.5 Hidden selection through recommendation

**Risk:** an assessment conclusion or preferred narrative becomes de facto
selection.

**Boundary response:** state Assessment ≠ Selection and Recommendation ≠
Authorization in every decision-support output. Return the decision explicitly
to human authority.

### 12.6 Authority drift

**Risk:** assessor, AI, Management OS, or preparation activity influences the
operational Decision or obtains source authority.

**Boundary response:** preparation observes candidate suitability only; no
operational Decision or Change occurs for preparation. Human and source
ownership remain explicit.

### 12.7 Semantic collapse

**Risk:** Observation, Evidence, Interpretation, model output, Recommendation,
Decision, Change, and Outcome are summarized as one apparent proof chain.

**Boundary response:** preserve kind-specific source, scope, time, ownership,
uncertainty, and prohibited inference. No modeled or prepared output becomes
Evidence or Outcome.

### 12.8 Pressure to proceed

**Risk:** completion of research or preparation is treated as requiring
selection and validation.

**Boundary response:** no-candidate, rejection, and indeterminate conclusions
remain legitimate. Preparation completion creates no next-step entitlement.

## 13. Explicit Exclusions

This proposal does not create or authorize:

- a validation result or value conclusion;
- a product-readiness or implementation-readiness claim;
- Engineering Architecture or engineering authorization;
- selection, ranking, scoring, or winner recommendation;
- validation approval, preparation, or execution;
- an operational Decision or source-owned Change;
- a prototype, experiment, instrumentation, interface, service, API, schema,
  data model, event, storage, runtime, infrastructure, UI, or deployment;
- implementation planning, delivery planning, product priority, staffing,
  schedule, or release commitment;
- a new canonical object, semantic kind, capability, lifecycle, protocol,
  governance authority, review gate, or readiness class;
- mandatory Root Cause or canonical Operational Diagnosis;
- resolution, acceptance, treatment, adoption, or selection of existing M8
  assessment or evolution objects;
- M9, a milestone, stage, order, transition, or backlog task; or
- amendment of canonical M7 or M8.1–M8.7.

## 14. Human Authority Consideration Questions

Before deciding whether preparation should be authorized, the applicable human
authority should answer:

1. Is the requested activity limited to candidate identification and
   suitability evidence?
2. Is the maximum conclusion limited to support for a later human decision?
3. Can the activity stop without selecting a candidate?
4. Are multiple candidates reasonably available, or is single-candidate bias
   understood and bounded?
5. Are operational owners, source authorities, affected participants, and
   access limits visible?
6. Can existing Evidence support preparation without prototype or
   implementation?
7. Can contrary, missing, adverse, and indeterminate evidence remain visible?
8. Can assessment remain qualitative, human, non-ranked, and non-authorizing?
9. Are Decision A, Decision B, and Decision C kept separate?
10. Can the authority reject all candidates or decline preparation?
11. Are premature implementation, causal overclaim, insufficient Evidence,
    scenario bias, and authority drift bounded explicitly?
12. Does the decision preserve Outcome as consequence test and all semantic and
    human-authority boundaries?

If these questions cannot be answered responsibly, the proposal does not
support preparation authorization.

## 15. Proposal Recommendation

The completed research is sufficient for an applicable human authority to
consider whether a bounded **candidate-scenario identification and suitability-
assessment preparation activity** should be separately authorized.

The research is not sufficient to select a scenario or authorize validation
execution. A preparation decision would be justified only if:

- its purpose, scope, evidence boundary, accountable human, exclusions, and
  termination conditions are fixed;
- operational and source-access conditions can be respected under existing
  authority;
- candidate Evidence can be examined without implementation;
- assessment remains qualitative and non-ranked;
- no-candidate and indeterminate results remain legitimate;
- scenario selection returns to a separate human authority decision; and
- any validation authorization remains a later distinct decision.

This recommendation supports consideration of a decision, not the decision
itself. It does not request or create a milestone, order, backlog task, or work
authorization.

## 16. Non-Decision and Non-Authorization

This proposal creates no preparation approval, scenario selection, validation
authorization or execution, canonical object, lifecycle, governance authority,
implementation requirement, prototype, Engineering Architecture, product
readiness claim, milestone, M9, stage, order, transition, backlog task, product
roadmap, or delivery commitment.

Canonical M7 and M8.1–M8.7 remain unchanged. M8 remains Completed / Closed.
Outcome remains the final consequence test; semantic kinds remain distinct;
human Decision and source ownership remain controlling; bounded contribution
remains distinct from causality; Root Cause remains non-mandatory; and
Operational Diagnosis remains research framing only.
