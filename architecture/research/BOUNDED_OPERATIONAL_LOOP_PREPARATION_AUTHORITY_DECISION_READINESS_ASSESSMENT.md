## 5. Boundary Preservation Verification

### Recommendation is not authorization

**Preserved.** Research findings, proposal language, assessor output, and any
future suitability recommendation cannot create authority. The decision shell
requires an attributable human disposition and complete applicable conditions.

### Suitability is not validation approval

**Preserved.** Preparation may at most produce candidate suitability decision
support. A suitable candidate would still require separate human selection and
later separate validation consideration.

### Preparation is not validation

**Preserved.** Preparation is limited to identifying candidates and assessing
existing suitability Evidence. It cannot perform an operational Investigation
for value, make the operational Decision, choose or execute Change, observe a
validation Outcome, or establish Learning.

### Outcome is not causal proof

**Preserved.** Outcome remains a future consequence target requiring
attributable Observation or source-owned Evidence and owned verification.
Favorable Outcome would not prove unique Management OS causality or complete
correctness of prior Interpretation, Recommendation, or Decision.

### Human Decision authority

**Preserved.** An existing accountable human authority must choose any future
disposition. AI, Management OS, assessor, score, ranking, confidence, data
volume, or research artifact cannot select or infer the option.

### Canonical and repository boundary

**Preserved.** The materials state that canonical M7 and M8.1–M8.7 remain
unchanged, M8 remains Completed / Closed, and no M9, successor, backlog,
Engineering Architecture, implementation, validation result, or product-
readiness claim follows.

## 6. Option Completeness

All four possible human dispositions are defined distinctly and without a
recommended choice:

| Option | Decision meaning | Automatic next step prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Authorized preparation` | Permit only the bounded preparation activity under complete recorded conditions | Scenario selection, validation, Change, implementation, or successor work |
| `Deferred` | Leave the decision unresolved without authorizing or declining preparation | Automatic reconsideration, task, or passive approval |
| `Additional evidence requested` | Request named decision-relevant existing Evidence within a bounded question | Hidden preparation, open-ended research, tooling, or implementation |
| `Declined` | Do not authorize this preparation transition | Value falsification, alternative-direction approval, or successor authority |

The option set is sufficient for the current decision question. It permits the
human to act without forcing authorization and preserves uncertainty explicitly.
