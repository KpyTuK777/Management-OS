## 3. Current Understanding

### 3.1 What research has established

Within the limits of architectural research, the completed work supports the
following understanding:

1. Canonical M8 provides a coherent constraint baseline for examining a complete
   operational loop.
2. The loop can be described compositionally using existing responsibilities and
   semantic kinds without creating a new lifecycle, governance process, or
   canonical object.
3. Evidence, Interpretation, modeled output, Recommendation, Decision, actual
   Change, Outcome, and Learning must remain distinguishable.
4. Human Decision authority cannot be supplied by AI, readiness, analysis,
   confidence, or a system artifact.
5. Decision authority and authority to alter a source-owned operational domain
   may be different.
6. Actual Change, rather than intended or reported Change, is the relevant input
   to consequence evaluation.
7. Outcome verification is the final bounded consequence test; it does not prove
   exclusive causality or retrospectively certify the preceding Decision.
8. Learning may arise after desired, undesired, mixed, null, unexpected, or
   indeterminate Outcome and remains contextual, correctable, and
   non-authorizing.
9. Operational Diagnosis remains optional research framing for composed
   understanding. The research has not established a need for a new Diagnosis
   object or a mandatory Root Cause model.
10. Analytical readiness and an attributable human Decision have remained
    demonstrably separate. Readiness does not cross the authority boundary.

The independent critical review found no canonical-boundary breach,
evidence-overstatement, or semantic collapse in the synthesis. It recorded two
non-blocking reading sensitivities: the phrase “Confirmed Architectural
Principles” must retain its qualifier, and compact arrow notation must retain its
non-lifecycle caveat.

### 3.2 Research-supported hypotheses

The following propositions are supported for consideration but remain unproven:

- preserving Evidence, meaning, authority, Change fidelity, Outcome verification,
  and Learning continuity may improve a bounded management Outcome;
- existing capabilities may compose into useful system-level understanding
  without a new Operational Diagnosis object;
- making an authority absence visible may improve operational clarity without
  creating authority;
- higher Decision-to-Change fidelity may improve the interpretability of later
  Outcome;
- bounded evidence may support a contribution claim without supporting exclusive
  causality;
- attributable, correctable Learning may improve later human judgment or action;
  and
- explicit semantic boundaries may reduce false certainty, authority leakage,

These are validation hypotheses. They are not product claims.

## 4. Management OS Operational Loop Model

The research synthesis uses the compact model:

```text
Reality
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Change
  ↓
Outcome
  ↓
Learning
```

This is a synthesis model. It is not a lifecycle, workflow, state machine,
governance process, validation protocol, object model, or guaranteed sequence.
Each arrow represents a transition boundary where meaning may be preserved,
qualified, interrupted, or lost.

### Reality → Evidence

Source-owned Observation, provenance, timing, context, contradiction, and
limitations must remain visible. Available Evidence does not exhaust Reality,
and missing Evidence does not automatically prove absence.

### Evidence → Understanding

Interpretation, hypotheses, possible contributing factors, and modeled
alternatives may organize Evidence but cannot replace it. Confidence,
presentation, repetition, or similarity does not promote modeled output into
Fact, Evidence, Prediction, or authority.

### Understanding → Decision

Understanding may support accountable judgment. It does not make the Decision.
Recommendation, suitability, readiness, or AI output cannot substitute for an
attributable human authority.

### Decision → Change

Decision intent and meaning must be distinguished from Change interpretation,
authorization, execution, and Observation. Actual Change requires source-owned
Evidence. Decision authority does not automatically include source-mutation
authority.

### Change → Outcome

Completed activity does not establish consequence. Outcome verification requires
an observed operational condition evaluated against bounded intent, actual
Change, time horizon, context, counterevidence, and relevant external factors.

### Outcome → Learning

Outcome Observation, Interpretation, Attribution, and Learning remain separate.
Learning must preserve context, uncertainty, contrary evidence, and correction.
It cannot become a success declaration or automatic authority for a future
Decision.

## 5. Decision Context

### 5.1 Possible decision for later consideration

A properly identified and accountable human authority could later consider only
the following bounded question:

> Should preparation for a possible bounded operational-loop validation be
> authorized within explicit scope, evidence, access, ethical, resource, and
> authority constraints?

This summary does not ask that question of itself and does not answer it.

### 5.2 What that possible decision would concern

If taken by a real authority, the Decision could determine whether bounded
preparation may begin. Preparation could concern collecting suitability evidence,
confirming ownership and access, bounding a candidate context, documenting
baseline and Outcome observability, and identifying what a later validation
decision would need.

Those examples describe the existing decision context. They do not authorize the
activities, define a preparation plan, select a scenario, or create required
artifacts through this summary.

### 5.3 What that possible decision would not decide

An authorization of preparation would not by itself:

- select a final scenario;
- authorize validation execution;
- validate product value;
- establish causal contribution;
- approve Engineering Architecture or implementation;
- create a milestone, M9, stage, or backlog;
- modify canonical architecture or governance; or
- authorize source mutation or operational Change.

### 5.4 Why preparation is separate from validation execution

Preparation determines whether a credible, bounded validation proposition can be
formed and whether necessary ownership, access, Evidence, comparison, Outcome,
and safety conditions could exist. Validation execution would expose real
operations to an approved inquiry and could create observations and Outcome
evidence. The authority, risks, scope, and effects are therefore different.

Preparation ≠ Validation

Validation readiness ≠ Validation approval

Suitability ≠ Selection

### 5.5 Why readiness is not authorization

Readiness describes the sufficiency of information for consideration. Only an
identified accountable human authority can make an attributable Decision and
create its permitted effect. A complete research package cannot infer the
authority identity, supply missing rationale, choose a disposition, or sign on
behalf of a person.

The completed authority-boundary learning demonstrates the valid terminal state:

```text
Decision materials ready
        ≠
Decision made
        ≠
Authority effect created
```

If authority identity, rationale, scope, or disposition is absent, the truthful
state remains no Decision and no authority effect.

### 6.2 Semantic boundaries

The following conditions remain mandatory for any later consideration:

```text
Evidence ≠ Interpretation
Modeled / Scenario Output ≠ Evidence or Fact
Recommendation ≠ Decision
Decision ≠ Change
Change ≠ Outcome
Outcome ≠ causal proof
Learning ≠ success declaration
```

### 6.3 Authority boundaries

- Decision remains attributable human judgment.
- AI has no Decision, governance, validation, source-mutation, or verification
  authority.
- Analytical readiness cannot create authorization.
- Source owners retain authority over their sources and operational Changes.
- Missing authority input may be observed and investigated but is not
  automatically governance failure.
- Preparation authority, validation authority, implementation authority, and
  operational Change authority are distinct.

### 6.4 Evidence discipline

- Every material claim requires claim-specific Evidence of sufficient quality.
- Observation, provenance, time, context, limitations, uncertainty,
  contradiction, and counterevidence remain visible.
- Activity metrics do not establish value or Outcome.
- Intended or modeled Change does not establish actual Change.
- One favorable Outcome does not prove causal contribution or product value.
- An indeterminate result remains legitimate when Evidence cannot support a
  stronger proposition.
- Learning must remain traceable to experience and open to correction.

## 7. Remaining Uncertainties

### 7.1 Real-world value

The principal post-M8 uncertainty is whether preserving the complete loop changes
a material management Outcome rather than merely producing more information,
analysis, records, or activity.

### 7.2 Evidence availability

It is unknown whether a real context can provide adequate baseline Evidence,
source access, contrary observations, Decision trace, actual-Change Evidence,
Outcome observation, comparison information, and later Learning trace without
excessive burden or distortion.

### 7.3 Scenario suitability

No scenario is selected. A candidate would still require bounded ownership,
material human judgment, reversible Change, available Evidence, observable
Outcome, comparison feasibility, acceptable risk, and useful Learning potential.
Suitability evidence would support consideration but not authorize selection.

### 7.4 Outcome observability

It remains unknown whether the intended operational condition can be observed at
a credible time horizon, distinguished from activity, and assessed without proxy
substitution or premature closure.

### 7.5 Attribution limits

Context change, concurrent interventions, incomplete Change, selection effects,
novelty, measurement distortion, and external events may prevent a credible
contribution conclusion. Outcome must remain indeterminate when those limits
cannot be bounded.

### 7.6 Authority and access

The decision-support materials cannot identify or invent the responsible real
authority, provide that authority’s rationale, secure source-owner consent,
commit resources, or resolve ethical and operational constraints. These remain
human inputs.

## 8. Possible Future Paths

The paths below are neutral options. Their order is not ranking, preference, or
recommendation.

### Path A — Proceed to bounded preparation

**Possible effect:** an accountable authority permits only the preparation scope
it explicitly bounds.

**What remains unchanged:** no scenario is automatically selected; no validation
is executed; no value claim, engineering authority, implementation authority,
milestone, or backlog is created.

**Material risks:** preparation may drift into hidden validation or prototype
work; weak Evidence may be rationalized; authority or source access may be
assumed; sunk effort may bias later selection.

### Path B — Request additional evidence

**Possible effect:** specified evidence gaps become the subject of a later,
separately bounded inquiry.

**What remains unchanged:** preparation and validation remain unauthorized; no
scenario is selected.

**Material risks:** the request may be too broad, impossible to satisfy without
authorization, or become indefinite research without reducing the decisive
uncertainty.

### Path C — Defer

**Possible effect:** consideration is postponed until stated return conditions or
changed circumstances exist.

**What remains unchanged:** research remains available; canonical architecture,
authority boundaries, and the absence of validation remain unchanged.

**Material risks:** evidence may become stale, operational access may change, and
the unresolved value uncertainty persists.

### Path D — Decline

**Possible effect:** preparation is not authorized within the considered scope.

**What remains unchanged:** declining preparation does not invalidate canonical
M8, the research, or the possibility of a differently bounded future question.

**Material risks:** real-world value remains untested, and future reconsideration
would require a new attributable authority context.

### Cross-path boundary

The summary supplies no preferred option, score, ranking, or automatic trigger.
The options are mutually distinguishable human dispositions, not system states.

## 10. Decision-Support Summary

The post-M8 research arc has produced a coherent conceptual account of the
operational loop and its semantic, evidence, authority, handoff, Outcome, and
Learning boundaries. Independent critical review found that the synthesis stays
within its evidence and does not change canonical M8.

an observable, bounded management contribution. No research artifact proves that
claim. Scenario suitability, Evidence availability, Outcome observability,
attribution, real-world product adequacy, and acceptable burden remain unknown.

more evidence, defer, or decline. This summary provides no recommendation among

remain separate from scenario selection, validation execution, Engineering
