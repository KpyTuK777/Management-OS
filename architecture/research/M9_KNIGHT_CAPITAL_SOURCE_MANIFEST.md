## Cataloguing Discipline

- Only sources with an identifiable public owner, stable public location, and
  stated publication or filing date are catalogued.
- Regulatory and government records, company-originated records, and secondary
  reporting remain separate source classes.
- Hosting by SEC EDGAR makes a company filing publicly attributable; it does not
  convert company-originated statements into SEC findings.
- A press release about an order is not treated as the order itself.
- A later rulemaking or policy discussion is not treated as an incident finding.
- Potential relevance names only the kinds of material a source may contain. It
  does not assert accuracy, completeness, consistency, or evidentiary weight.
- Known limitations describe provenance and scope constraints. They do not assess
  the truth of the source's contents.
- No statement from one source is merged with a statement from another source in
  this manifest.

  not independent findings, even when preserved on EDGAR.

  but may reflect incomplete early reporting and unstable third-party archives.
