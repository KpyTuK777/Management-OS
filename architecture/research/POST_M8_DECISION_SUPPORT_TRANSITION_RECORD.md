## 5. Authority Boundary

### 5.1 Readiness ≠ Decision

Readiness describes whether information is sufficiently organized for an
accountable person to consider a bounded question. Decision requires that person
to exercise applicable authority, choose an explicit disposition, own a
rationale, and attest that the Decision was made.

```text
Materials ready
      ≠
Question considered by authority
      ≠
Decision made
      ≠
Authority effect created
```

Analytical completeness cannot bridge any of these boundaries automatically.

### 5.2 Decision support ≠ Recommendation

Decision support may preserve evidence, options, consequences, uncertainties,
and constraints. It does not choose a preferred option. The available paths—
proceed to bounded preparation, request additional evidence, defer, or decline—
remain unranked and unselected.

A support artifact that made the choice would cease to be neutral support and
would improperly simulate human judgment.

### 5.3 Recommendation ≠ Authorization

Even if another research artifact contains a reasoned recommendation, the
recommendation has no authority effect. Persuasiveness, confidence, repetition,
review readiness, or agreement cannot transform advice into authorization.

Authorization requires a real authority acting within an applicable authority
basis and recording an attributable Decision.

### 5.4 AI and research artifacts cannot substitute human authority

AI and research artifacts may:

- assemble and trace evidence;
- make uncertainty and contradiction visible;
- preserve options and non-effects;
- identify missing authority inputs;
- test structural completeness; and
- leave a truthful non-decision state.

They cannot:

- identify a real accountable person by inference;
- establish that person’s authority basis;
- select a disposition;
- invent or own the person’s rationale;
- use the current date as evidence of a Decision;
- attest on behalf of a human;
- grant access, resources, consent, or source-mutation authority; or
- create preparation, validation, engineering, or implementation authority.
