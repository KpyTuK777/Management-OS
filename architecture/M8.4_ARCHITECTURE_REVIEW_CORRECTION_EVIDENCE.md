## Semantic Resolution Verification

### Observation

- Purpose is explanatory observation only.
- It must derive from repository-visible evidence while remaining distinct from
  that evidence.
- It cannot become a Finding or substitute for evidentiary support.
- It cannot promote evidence or imply severity.
- It cannot imply a Recommendation or Decision.
- It cannot accept or treat Risk, resolve Gap, or authorize work.
- It may only explain existing Findings, Gaps, Risks, or their relationships and
  may inform a bounded Conclusion without changing any object.

### Conclusion

- Purpose is a bounded M8.4 assessment outcome only.
- It must derive from Findings, Gaps, Risks, and supporting Observations.
- It may summarize only what those attributable objects support.
- It cannot approve or correct.
- It cannot accept Risk or resolve Gap.
- It cannot authorize implementation, governance change, backlog work, or stage
  transition.
- It cannot become Architecture Approval or Canonical authority.
- It remains distinct from Recommendation and Decision and cannot modify a
  source assessment object.
