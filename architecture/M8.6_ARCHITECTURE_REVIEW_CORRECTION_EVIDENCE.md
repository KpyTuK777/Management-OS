## Determinism Verification

- One subject, scope, purpose, and evidence boundary are fixed before
  classification; ambiguity produces R0 rather than subjective selection.
- The procedure is ordered and stops at the first satisfied rule.
- R0 excludes every positive class.
- R2 precedes R3, R4, and R1 and is used only where claim support actually
  depends on an explicit unresolved architectural condition.
- R3 and R4 are distinguished by mutually exclusive named purposes.
- R1 is reached only when none of R0, R2, R3, or R4 applies.
- Each assessed purpose receives its own conclusion, preventing one scope from
  receiving multiple purpose-dependent classes.
- R3 supports submission as review input only and does not imply an M8.7 result.
- R4 supports a bounded architectural basis only and authorizes neither
  Engineering Architecture nor implementation.

## Governance-Separation Verification
