## Semantic Resolution Verification

- A model may explore alternatives and possible consequences.
- Modeled or scenario output remains a model and a distinct semantic kind.
- It is not Evidence, Observation, Fact, Prediction, Recommendation, or
  Decision.
- Reuse and similarity do not turn modeled output into Evidence.
- Confidence and presentation do not change its semantic kind or authority.
- Recommendation inherits no authority from modeled output.
- Decision remains explicit accountable human judgment.
- Automation cannot promote modeled output into authority.
- Observed outcome requires attributable observation or source-owned Evidence
  and owned verification judgment, not a scenario result.
