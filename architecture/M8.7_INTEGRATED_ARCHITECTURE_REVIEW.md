### 5.1 Trace invariants

1. Evidence remains distinct from interpretation, hypothesis, modeled or
   scenario output, confidence, Recommendation, Decision, outcome, and learning.
2. A model may explore alternatives and possible consequences, but a scenario
   remains a model. Reuse does not turn it into Evidence; high confidence does
   not change its semantic kind; and presentation, confidence, reuse, or
   similarity cannot promote it to Fact, Observation, Prediction,
   Recommendation, Decision, or authority.
3. A Recommendation informs but cannot become or authorize a Decision and
   inherits no authority from modeled or scenario output.
4. Automation may assist capture, organization, retrieval, analysis, simulation,
   and recommendation only within recorded authority and uncertainty bounds.
5. Human authority owns accountable judgment and Decision.
6. Source ownership and provenance survive ingestion, composition, handoff,
   correction, outcome observation, and learning.
7. Feedback may inform future interpretation or evolution but cannot rewrite
   historical evidence or canonical authority.
