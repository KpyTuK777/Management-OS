# 2. Why M9 May Be Needed

M8 is Completed / Closed. Its canonical corpus supplies a coherent integrated
architectural constraint baseline while preserving residual gaps, risks,
maturity limits, and empirical unknowns.

The post-M8 research program is **Analytically Complete Within Current Scope**.
It has clarified the operational loop, semantic non-substitution, source
ownership, human authority, Outcome, attribution, Learning, and Product Value
Hypotheses. Its completion synthesis concludes that additional broad
document-centered analysis is unlikely to reduce the dominant uncertainty
materially.

The dominant remaining uncertainty supported by that research is empirical
product value: whether Management OS creates an observable, useful contribution
in real operational contexts while preserving its architectural and authority
boundaries.

These conditions explain why a bounded successor milestone may deserve human
consideration. They do not establish that M9 is necessary, timely, approved, or
the only legitimate next direction. Deferral, correction, refinement, or
decline remain possible.

# 3. Proposed Milestone Purpose

The proposed purpose is:

> To produce bounded, inspectable empirical learning about whether and how
> Management OS creates observable value for operational managers while
> preserving Evidence, human authority, source ownership, Change, Outcome, and
> Learning boundaries.

“Produce learning” means make a bounded change in what is supportably known,
including through negative, mixed, null, contrary, adverse, unexpected, or
indeterminate findings. It does not mean obtain a positive result.

# 4. Primary Uncertainty

The proposal addresses:

> Whether Management OS creates an observable, useful contribution in real
> operational contexts.

This uncertainty includes whether relevant context and provenance remain
recoverable; reasoning remains inspectable without displacing human judgment;
Decision meaning survives into source-owned actual Change; material Outcomes
can be observed; attribution can remain bounded; and Learning supports later
work without becoming hidden authority.

The uncertainty is not reducible to:

- user satisfaction;
- feature usage;
- implementation progress;
- shipping;
- task or stage completion;
- artifact volume; or
- activity counts.

Those observations may supply context but cannot independently establish
useful product contribution.

# 5. Proposed Units of Learning

Candidate units that a future separately authorized design might consider
include:

- one bounded operational case;
- one preserved Decision episode;
- one source-owned actual Change;
- one observed Outcome;
- one structured re-engagement with a completed case; and
- one Evidence-backed Learning result.

Each candidate has a different identity. A case can bound context, a Decision
episode can expose reasoning continuity, actual Change can test handoff fidelity,
Outcome can expose consequence, re-engagement can examine reflective value, and
Learning can record a bounded change in understanding.

These are proposal-level candidates only. They are not selected protocol
elements, stages, metrics, minimum sample sizes, experiments, or acceptance
criteria. Their presence creates no authority to access Evidence or conduct
empirical work.

# 6. Relationship to H1–H5

The proposal carries forward the five Product Value Hypotheses in their existing
unvalidated identities:

- **H1 — Operational Context Preservation:** Management OS may reduce context
  loss and improve reconstruction relative to fragmented operational tooling.
- **H2 — Better Decision Support:** preserved relationships may support more
  evidence-grounded, uncertainty-visible, inspectable reasoning without
  guaranteeing better Decisions.
- **H3 — Experience Amplification:** structured re-engagement with preserved
  completed cases may yield richer bounded reflection than ordinary recollection
  or one-time review.
- **H4 — Organizational Operational Memory:** Management OS may help authorized
  actors recover the evolution of managerial understanding rather than only
  disconnected records.
- **H5 — Manager Capability Development:** repeated evidence-grounded case
  reflection may support development of managerial judgment without proving
  causal acceleration or performance improvement.

The following boundaries are mandatory:

- no hypothesis is selected;
- no ranking, preference, priority, or sequence exists;
- no hypothesis is proven or validated;
- this proposal does not require all five hypotheses to be examined;
- Evidence for one hypothesis would not validate another; and
- future human authority must separately select any hypothesis for bounded
  work.

# 12. Outcome and Learning Boundary

The proposal preserves these non-substitution rules:

```text
Activity ≠ Outcome
Completion ≠ Outcome
Positive Outcome ≠ causal proof
Negative Outcome ≠ automatic proof of a bad Decision
```

An indeterminate Outcome is valid and must not be forced into success or
failure. Outcome observation requires the relevant precondition, actual Change,
time horizon, source-owned Evidence, concurrent factors, and limitations to
remain visible.

Learning requires preserved history, Evidence, Decision context, actual Change,
Outcome, attribution limits, contradiction, and reflection. It remains
contextual, correctable, and non-authorizing. It cannot rewrite what was known
at Decision time or become current Evidence automatically.

Modeled alternatives remain modeled content. They do not become Facts,
Evidence, predictions, Decisions, actual Changes, or observed Outcomes.

# 16. Major Risks

| Risk | Possible distortion | Required future protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmation bias | Evidence is sought or interpreted to preserve the desired product story. | Preserve falsification, contrary Evidence, and negative dispositions. |
| Scenario selection bias | A convenient or favorable context is treated as representative. | Make selection rationale, exclusions, and transfer limits explicit. |
| Novelty effect | Initial engagement is mistaken for durable value. | Preserve time horizon and alternative explanations. |
| Weak Evidence | Incomplete, stale, inaccessible, self-reported, or low-provenance material supports a strong claim. | Assess relevance, provenance, quality, contradiction, and sufficiency separately. |
| Unobservable Outcomes | Activity or completion substitutes for a material consequence. | Establish Outcome identity and observability before interpretation. |
| Causal overclaim | Correlation or one sequence becomes proof of exclusive contribution. | Preserve concurrent factors and bounded attribution. |
| Activity mistaken for value | Documents, meetings, tasks, or cases count as progress. | Tie progress to attributable uncertainty reduction. |
| Implementation drift | Building becomes the real milestone while Learning becomes secondary. | Keep implementation separately authorized and subordinate. |
| AI authority drift | AI synthesis becomes Evidence, verdict, Decision, or causal authority. | Preserve provenance and accountable human judgment. |
| Research recursion | More documents repeat boundaries without real Evidence. | Stop broad analysis when it cannot reduce the empirical uncertainty. |
| Architecture overfit | One case reshapes general architecture without adequate transfer Evidence. | Bound architecture feedback and require separate review. |
| One-case product proof | One positive case is generalized into validated product value. | Preserve case scope, replication uncertainty, and non-effects. |
| Outcome bias | Later result rewrites the quality of the earlier Decision. | Preserve historical Evidence, rationale, and uncertainty. |
| Evidence burden | Capture or observation creates disproportionate cognitive, privacy, or operational cost. | Assess burden, ethics, access, and harm before activity. |
