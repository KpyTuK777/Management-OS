# Living Organization Product and Interaction definition

## Definition state

Non-canonical proposal for review. No implementation authority.

## Product lifecycle

The persistent home is the Living Organizational Model. It supports continuous observation and progressive refinement. Investigation and Improvement are sibling bounded work modes. Both may lead to an accountable Decision, source-owned implementation, Monitoring, Stabilization, Closure/continuation, Operational Memory and a reviewed model update.

Useful work may begin with one organization identity, one department and explicit unknowns. Completeness is never a prerequisite.

## Organization Constructor

The Constructor is a progressive modelling environment, not a setup wizard or administration database.

Primary sequence:

1. establish minimal organization identity and purpose;
2. add a department or known operational area;
3. add a known leader, role or person;
4. record missing or uncertain information alongside known information;
5. connect a process, SOP, system, KPI, artifact or interaction;
6. inspect formal and actual relationships separately;
7. enrich or correct the model over time while preserving history.

Every entry carries source class, confidence/knowledge state, effective time and privacy boundary where applicable. The constructor must offer human-first creation in context, search and progressive disclosure; technical IDs, provenance nodes and graph terminology remain hidden by default.

## Organization Map

Purpose: orientation, navigation and relationship inspection across departments and operational dependencies.

The calm spatial map may project reporting structure, operational dependencies, information flows, key systems/processes, unresolved areas and active Investigation/Improvement contexts. Users choose layers and inspect one relationship at a time. Reporting and operational relationships use distinct labels and visual grammar. Density is reduced through semantic zoom, filtering and focus—not by hiding material uncertainty.

The map is not an org chart, dashboard, free-form mind map, universal graph, detective board or causal visualization. Position, proximity, line weight and activity cannot imply importance, authority, truth or causality without explicit semantics.

## Department Workbench

Each department is an operational workspace composed from governed references:

- identity: name, purpose, status, organizational place and concise summary;
- leadership: manager, formal authority, operational responsibility and Decision boundaries;
- team: people, roles, supported skills/experience, responsibilities, capacity and carefully classified observations;
- systems: CRM, ERP, tools, spreadsheets, communications, ownership, purpose, usage, limitations, integrations and dependencies;
- SOP/rules: owner, scope, version, linked process/roles, actual-usage evidence, missing/outdated state;
- KPI/measures: purpose, owner, behavior affected, source, cadence, uncertainty and known distortion;
- processes: Input → Process → Output → Result, with trigger, owner, participants, systems, SOPs, decisions, handoffs, expectations, exceptions, constraints, risks and feedback where known;
- interactions: source/recipient departments, purpose, transferred information/artifact, roles, cadence, SOP, system, expected result, friction and uncertainty;
- knowledge/history: Decisions, Investigations, Improvements, changes, Monitoring outcomes, lessons, archived states and Operational Memory.

Conflicting descriptions remain inspectable parallel accounts until human judgment resolves or preserves them.

## Person and Role Workbench

Separate factual identity/role history from statements, manager observations, subjective impressions, confirmed events, unresolved claims and sensitive personal data. Access follows purpose and least disclosure. No hidden scoring or inference.

The contribution chronology may show reports, process explanations, observations, responsibility clarifications, exceptions and participation in Investigation or Improvement. Each entry links to the original material and historical role/department snapshot. “Later supported” or “later contradicted” describes the information relationship, never the person.

## Person-as-Source interaction

The primary form asks “Who provided this information?” and allows search or Organization → Department → Person browsing. After selection it confirms the person, contribution-time role and department. Progressive optional details include information date, communication form, direct/indirect knowledge and limitation.

The Investigation Inspector owns inspection of a specific contribution in its inquiry context. Person Workbench owns chronological contributions about that person identity; Department Workbench owns department-level participation and unresolved accounts. All are projections of one relationship, not duplicated records.

## Improvement contour

Candidate sequence:

Opportunity/Intent → Current State → Desired State → Scope → Affected Elements → Dependencies → Risks → Proposed Change → Implementation Plan → Human Decision → Implementation → Monitoring → Stabilization → model update → Operational Memory.

Required invariants:

- current operation may be healthy;
- change may be optional;
- expected value and causal mechanism may remain uncertain;
- risks and damage to working behavior remain visible;
- proposal is not Decision; Decision is not execution authority;
- implementation status is not Outcome; Monitoring is not causal proof;
- stabilization may be partial, failed or indeterminate.

## Investigation reconciliation

Investigation remains internally intact and may open from organization, department, person/role, process, system, SOP, interaction, symptom, material or report. Its context envelope records origin and relevant model references. Those references are not automatically Investigation material or accepted Evidence.

## Transition contracts

- Improvement → Investigation: create linked Investigation when a material unknown blocks responsible change; preserve Improvement intent and scope; do not label the unknown a defect.
- Investigation → Improvement: create linked optional opportunity when sufficient understanding supports considering deliberate change; preserve findings and uncertainty; do not imply correction is required.
- Both transitions require an attributable human action/reason and retain distinct identities, states and closure.

## Watson future context

Future bounded assistance may surface missing relationships, inconsistent ownership, conflicting SOPs, duplication, dependencies, unclear authority, missing model areas and questions. Person-as-Source may reveal conflicting accounts, knowledge concentration or absent roles. Watson may suggest questions and expose traceable patterns; it may not infer dishonesty, competence, motive, reliability, performance, diagnosis, causality or Decisions.

## Human Experience and visual boundary

Use the Design Bible: calm, premium, structured, spatial, tactile and inspectable; maximize useful viewport, minimize explanatory text, use progressive disclosure, preserve honest incompleteness and clear interaction feedback. No visual metaphor is canonized by this proposal.
