# 6. Maximum Bounded Effect

A valid, complete, human-attested A–H disposition may at most:

- record one fixed selected published candidate;
- record one bounded historical episode;
- authorize preparation of a source manifest;
- authorize chronology and Evidence-gap assessment; and
- authorize preparation of walkthrough-readiness consideration.

The selection does not establish that sources, chronology, Evidence, Decision
rationale, Actual Change, Outcome attribution, or Replay controls are sufficient.

I or J records no selected candidate and creates no A–H preparation effect. No
disposition creates automatic transition authority.

# 7. Explicit Non-Effects

- expand backlog without a separate transition;
- modify roadmap, milestone, stage, canonical M7/M8, source ownership, or Product
