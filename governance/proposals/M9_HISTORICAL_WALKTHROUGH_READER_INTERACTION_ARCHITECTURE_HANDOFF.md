## 2. Handoff Responsibility

Interaction Architecture preserves product meaning while making interaction
obligations precise enough for later technical allocation. Primary semantic
ownership remains P1 Owner Experience and Contribution, with dependencies on
P2–P6 and the cross-cutting trust plane.

## 3. Interaction Contracts

| Contract | Required behavior | Prohibited collapse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation | Establish case, scope, phase position, source limits, hindsight, Replay absence, and H3 state before detail | Repository completion cannot imply human participation |
| Progressive disclosure | Reveal identity and boundaries first, claim/source depth on deliberate inspection | Simplicity cannot hide provenance or uncertainty |
| Current Situation | Project governed phase-relevant understanding with a return point | Presentation cannot become source truth |
| Claim inspection | Preserve class, source, attribution, locator, publication time, knowledge/hindsight status, confidence, contradiction, and gap | Presence cannot imply acceptance or completeness |
| Source traceability | Resolve every substantive claim to fixed source identity and locator | Summary cannot displace source authority |
| Uncertainty | Keep Missing Evidence and confidence boundaries adjacent to constrained meaning | Unknown cannot become absence or certainty |
| Working Set | Allow attributable collect, remove, order, and revisit behavior | Membership cannot promote Evidence or change claim state |
| Reflection | Require direct traversal, identity, response or explicit deferral, and provenance | AI cannot infer response; reflection cannot validate H3 |
| Bounded exit | Preserve response/deferral and stop before Replay or unsupported conclusion | Exit cannot imply Root Cause or causal proof |

## 4. State and Transition Semantics

```text
UNORIENTED → ORIENTED
ORIENTED ↔ PHASE FOCUSED
PHASE FOCUSED ↔ CLAIM INSPECTION ↔ SOURCE INSPECTION
ORIENTED / PHASE FOCUSED ↔ WORKING SET MAINTENANCE
SUFFICIENT DIRECT TRAVERSAL → REFLECTION AVAILABLE
REFLECTION AVAILABLE → RESPONSE RECORDED | DEFERRAL RECORDED
RESPONSE RECORDED | DEFERRAL RECORDED → BOUNDED EXIT
```

Transitions express user-perceivable meaning, not routes, events, state machines,
storage records, or implementation workflow. Replay has no transition.

## 5. Human, AI, and System Boundaries

- Human owns attention, Working Set choices, judgment, response, deferral, and attestation.
- AI may orient, retrieve, summarize within fixed claims, expose uncertainty, and assist navigation.
- The future technical system may preserve identity, state, ordering, traceability,
  and failure visibility but cannot manufacture human authority or historical truth.

## 6. Failure and Degraded Contracts

- unavailable source: retain identity and disclose access limitation;
- broken claim/source link: do not present the claim as supported;
- ambiguous support: stop at confidence boundary;
- missing attributable identity: block final response or retain non-attributable draft;
- conflicting repository evidence: preserve conflict and suspend affected synthesis;
- Replay request: expose authorization boundary and stop.

## 7. Accessibility Contract

Every interaction obligation must remain achievable through keyboard-complete
navigation, meaningful focus progression, non-color-only state distinctions,
readable hierarchy, and textual equivalents for material visual meaning. The
contract does not choose visual composition.

## 8. Engineering Handoff Requirements

Engineering Architecture must demonstrate traceability from every technical
responsibility to these contracts; explicit ownership of case identity, source
identity, phase focus, Working Set, reflection provenance, and degraded state;
preservation of historical immutability and Replay exclusion; and no technical
mechanism that silently changes semantic or authority state.
