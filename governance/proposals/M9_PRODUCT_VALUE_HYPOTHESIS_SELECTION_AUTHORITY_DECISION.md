## 6. Maximum bounded effect

A valid, complete, human-attested A–E disposition may at most:

- record one fixed selected Product Value Hypothesis;
- authorize preparation of case or scenario suitability consideration for that
  hypothesis;
- authorize preparation of Evidence-access and Outcome-observability questions;
  and
- permit refinement of the bounded empirical question through a separate
  transition.

F or G records no hypothesis selection and creates no selected-hypothesis
preparation authority. No disposition creates automatic transition authority.
