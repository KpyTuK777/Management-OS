## InformationContribution

I adopt InformationContribution as a specialized Operating Model Relationship Assertion.

InformationContribution remains distinct from:

- source-content ownership;
- authorship;
- responsibility;
- subject-of-information;
- recorder identity;
- approval;
- Decision;
- Evidence;
- credibility;
- trustworthiness.

Material content remains externally owned. Investigation or Improvement context remains externally owned. Evidence status remains externally owned. The relationship preserves references rather than copying externally owned content.

## Temporal authority

I adopt the reviewed distinction among:

- effective time;
- recording time;
- observation and communication time;
- source version and retrieval time;
- decision-time snapshot time;
- correction time;
- dispute time;
- supersession time;
- withdrawal time;
- redaction time;
- deletion time.

Current-state derivation must respect authority before recency.

The reviewed precedence remains:

1. authoritative source;
2. accountable-human-approved Operating Model assertion where no higher source exists;
3. qualified declarations and observations;
4. explicit derived or inferred projections.

A newer lower-authority assertion must not silently override a higher-authority source.

## Historical integrity

Current organizational state must not destroy historical meaning.

Role changes, department changes, relationship changes, corrections, and supersession must preserve the applicable historical context.

Correction, supersession, dispute, withdrawal, redaction, deletion, retirement, merge, and split remain semantically distinct.

Deletion integrity markers remain authorization-bound and non-reconstructive. Deleted identifiers are not reused or silently reassigned. Ordinary projections cannot leak deleted or restricted existence.

## Reviewed-update authority

- Source-backed changes require the native source owner’s authority and action before Operating Model reconciliation.
- Operating-Model-owned changes require approval from an identifiable human holding the applicable semantic/update authority.
- Record custody or synchronization may apply only an already authorized result.
- Investigation, Improvement, Impact Review, Monitoring, Stabilization, Operational Memory, Watson, integrations, and projections may propose or reference updates but cannot approve or apply them.
- An operational Decision does not automatically mutate the Operating Model.

## Projection invariants

All Product projections must preserve:

- one governed identity;
- one mutation owner;
- authorization and filtering before projection;
- qualified current versus historical state;
- no context-to-Evidence promotion;
- no context-to-Decision promotion;
- source references instead of copied source-owned content;
- correction, redaction, and deletion propagation;
- separation of UI/layout state from organizational truth;
- existing Investigation, Improvement, Decision, and other work-capability ownership;
- filtered and non-authoritative future AI consumption.

## Human rationale

The adopted Living Organizational Model, Investigation, Improvement, People & Privacy, and Interaction Architecture require a single canonical organizational identity and relationship authority before Engineering Architecture can be completed safely.

The reviewed clarification resolves that gap without creating a competing organizational model or transferring authority from existing source owners.

It provides the minimum canonical semantics required for consistent Organization Map, Workbench, Investigation, Improvement, Operational Memory, and future Watson projections.
