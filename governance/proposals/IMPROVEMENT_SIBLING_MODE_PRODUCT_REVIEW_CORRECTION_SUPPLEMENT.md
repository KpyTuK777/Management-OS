## C1 — pre-Decision candidate implementation approach

In the Product completeness map, the pre-Decision label `Implementation Plan` means a **candidate implementation approach for human Decision support**, not the canonical post-Decision Execution Plan.

Before Decision, Improvement may coordinate non-authoritative projections of:

- feasibility and material assumptions;
- possible sequencing and dependencies;
- candidate accountable and source owners for confirmation;
- resource, timing, reversibility, rollback, recovery, containment, and stop considerations;
- proposed guardrails, monitoring conditions, and validation needs;
- implementation alternatives, including a bounded experiment or no implementation.

This work creates no approved adaptation, assignment, task, commitment, schedule reservation, source mutation, execution record, or authorization to act. Candidate owners are not assigned merely by being named.

After an authoritative executable Decision, the existing canonical sequence remains controlling:

```text
authoritative executable Decision
  -> proportionate Operational Impact Review or attributable skip
  -> owner-approved adaptations
  -> source-owned Execution Planning
  -> source-owned execution
  -> Outcome, Monitoring, and Stabilization Assessment
```

Operational Impact Review owns impact findings and adaptation dispositions. Execution Planning owns the approved delivery plan. Each source capability validates and performs its own mutation. Improvement may project status and preserve links across that sequence but owns none of those records.

## C2 — mandatory Matter linkage without a parallel envelope

For a bounded operational situation, Improvement is a Product-mode work context linked to the applicable canonical Operational Matter. Matter remains the single persistent control-plane identity and owns macro-state, lifecycle history, lineage, authoritative Decision references, and Outcome references.

An Improvement workspace may have a distinct mode/work-context identity so its intent, revisions, alternatives, transitions, and closure remain inspectable. That identity is a linked contribution identity, not a second Matter, lifecycle envelope, canonical aggregate, or organizational model.

Starting from an organization, department, process, system, SOP, role, interaction, recurring meeting, report, or identified opportunity does not bypass Matter authority:

- if an applicable Matter exists, Improvement links to it;
- if no applicable Matter exists, Matter creation may be proposed and recorded only through the existing Matter authority in `Reported`;
- Improvement cannot infer a Matter state, create a later state directly, or change macro-state from workspace progress;
- duplicate-Matter avoidance, relationship typing, reopening, and lineage follow the canonical Matter contract.

The Living Organizational Model supplies the entry context and projection. The Operational Operating Model supplies governed organizational references. Neither the Improvement work identity nor the entry reference becomes a parallel source of truth.

## C3 — material inquiry trigger and safe concurrency

Improvement must open a linked Investigation whenever deliberate-change work exposes an uncertainty that materially requires inquiry, including insufficient understanding, contradiction, an unknown dependency, an unexplained behavior, or a premise requiring Evidence. The uncertainty need not block every part of Improvement work.

The accountable human may explicitly disposition the Improvement as:

- paused pending Investigation;
- continuing only in unaffected areas;
- narrowed to remove the unresolved dependency;
- retained as an option without progress;
- stopped or closed without change.

If work continues concurrently:

- the unresolved item remains visibly linked to the Investigation;
- assumptions remain assumptions and cannot become facts, Evidence, confirmed impact, or approved adaptations through reuse;
- proposed scope cannot silently expand around the unknown;
- an Investigation update does not automatically change the Improvement, Decision, or Matter state;
- resumption, revision, narrowing, or stopping remains an attributable human action.

Investigation retains its own identity, inquiry, Evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and disposition. Improvement retains its separate deliberate-change intent and optionality. Neither mode converts into or absorbs the other.

## Corrected lifecycle reading

The complete proposal is therefore read as:

```text
Opportunity / Intent and governed current context
  -> progressive, revisable change reasoning
  -> candidate implementation approach for Decision support
  -> accountable human Decision
  -> existing post-Decision Impact Review / planning / source-owned execution
  -> Monitoring and proportionate Stabilization Assessment
  -> immutable, inspectable Operational Memory contribution
  -> separately reviewed Operating Model update where justified
  -> continued operational control
```

At every point, the applicable Matter preserves canonical lifecycle continuity. A linked Investigation may be created whenever material inquiry is needed. No sequence is a mandatory wizard, and no favorable result or causal mechanism is presumed.
