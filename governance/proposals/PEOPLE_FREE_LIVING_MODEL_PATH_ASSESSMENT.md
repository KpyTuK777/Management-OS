# People-free Living Organizational Model path assessment

## Assessment identity

- Assessed People & Privacy proposal: commit `533bfe177d532cade7923fb2db385f3d1d95c4d5`, blob `7f7cb42193ab9b83771edcf4d2affbc1811686e5`
- Governing Living Model Decision: `9a8a6adad800c71f772984e6f36d6c6e345b3622`
- Assessment purpose: determine which useful Living Organizational Model scope can remain independent of named-person and people-sensitive capability
- Implementation authority: none

## Disposition

**A useful people-free Product path is semantically viable and should remain independently authorizable.**

People/privacy policy must not block the whole Organization Constructor or Living Organizational Model. The canonical Operational Operating Model already supports organizational structure, roles, responsibilities, processes, systems, SOPs, measures, flows, controls, dependencies, and interactions without requiring named individuals or sensitive observations.

This assessment establishes Product separability only. It does not authorize Interaction Architecture, Engineering Architecture, implementation, or a repository transition.

## People-free scope

A future separately authorized people-free slice may represent:

- organization identity, purpose, and bounded scope;
- departments or operational units;
- roles without named occupants;
- formal responsibility and decision-authority definitions attached to roles;
- processes, process steps, handoffs, inputs, outputs, and dependencies;
- systems and integrations;
- SOPs, controls, standards, and ownership expressed as roles or departments;
- KPIs/measures, sources, cadence, limitations, and monitoring expectations;
- interdepartmental interactions and information/artifact flows;
- recurring meetings represented as operational interactions, without attendee identity;
- reports and artifacts with department/role ownership;
- known, unknown, disputed, stale, and missing organizational context;
- Investigation and Improvement entry from non-person organizational elements;
- reviewed Operating Model updates and non-person Operational Memory projections.

Where operational responsibility must be shown, the Product may use a role or department reference. “Unassigned,” “unknown,” or “requires confirmation” is preferable to inventing or requiring a named person.

## Excluded from the people-free path

- named individual identities and memberships;
- Person or named-person Workbench;
- Person-as-Source / InformationContribution tied to identity;
- named-person contribution, Investigation, Improvement, or Decision history;
- manager observations and all subjective people information;
- named workload, capacity, skills, experience, knowledge-holder, or availability records;
- sensitive personal data;
- named reporting relationships;
- person-level Watson context or inference;
- identity-based search, filtering, exports, analytics, or Memory retrieval.

Free-text source attribution in an existing authorized Investigation may remain governed by its current boundary; this assessment neither expands nor converts it into Person-as-Source.

## Safe substitution rules

| People-sensitive need | People-free representation |
| --- | --- |
| “Who owns this process?” | Owning role or department; unknown if not established. |
| “Who approves this Decision?” | Decision-authority role definition; no current holder required. |
| “Who uses this system?” | Participating roles/departments. |
| “Who knows this informal process?” | Unresolved knowledge-holder question or role-level dependency; no inferred person. |
| “Who attends this meeting?” | Participating roles/departments and meeting purpose. |
| “Who supplied this statement?” | Existing context-bound free-text attribution only where already authorized, or protected/unknown source; no durable person relationship. |
| “Who is overloaded?” | Process/role demand or capacity uncertainty, not named-person workload. |

These substitutions must remain honest. Role-based representation cannot imply that a current person occupies the role, performs the responsibility, has the skill, or supplied information.

## Product value retained

The people-free slice still supports:

- organizational orientation and progressive construction;
- department, process, system, SOP, control, measure, and interaction mapping;
- formal-versus-unknown responsibility visibility at role level;
- Investigation context and entry without turning context into Evidence;
- adopted Improvement context and entry for deliberate organizational change;
- dependency and handoff inspection;
- reviewed model evolution after Monitoring and Stabilization;
- non-person historical operational learning.

This is enough to materialize meaningful structural and operational value without collecting named-person or sensitive information.

## Dependency boundary

The people-free path still requires its own future Product-slice, Interaction, Engineering, and implementation authority. It is not automatically authorized by the Living Model Decision or this assessment.

Later People & Privacy adoption may extend the same source-governed model with named-person projections and InformationContribution. It must not require migration from a parallel people-free organizational model. Role, department, process, system, SOP, interaction, and artifact identities remain the shared foundation.

## Risk controls

- Do not hide named-person data behind role labels while retaining it in search, logs, exports, or Watson context.
- Do not infer person identity from email, document metadata, system telemetry, meeting data, or free text.
- Do not treat role vacancy, uncertainty, or missing attribution as a defect or performance signal.
- Do not make named people mandatory to complete organization construction.
- Do not use placeholder people that could later be mistaken for real identity.
- Do not present role-level workload or knowledge concentration as a named-person conclusion.

## Authority effect

This assessment supports independent future consideration of a people-free Living Organizational Model slice. It does not adopt or implement that slice, bypass People & Privacy authority for named data, alter canonical sources, authorize Organization Constructor work, modify ROADMAP/BACKLOG, or create Interaction, Engineering, AI, integration, deployment, Replay, H3, Root Cause, or causal-proof authority.
