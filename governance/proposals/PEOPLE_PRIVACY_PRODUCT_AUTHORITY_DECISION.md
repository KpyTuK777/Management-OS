## Adopted Product principles

People information may be stored only where there is a defensible operational purpose.

Management OS must distinguish:

- organizational facts;
- attributable statements;
- manager observations;
- system-derived records;
- Investigation material and Evidence relationships;
- sensitive people information.

A statement made by a person is not automatically an established fact.

A manager observation is not automatically an objective characteristic of a person.

Sensitive or subjective information must not silently propagate across Product surfaces.

## Person-as-Source / InformationContribution

I adopt InformationContribution as the bounded Product relationship for representing information supplied by a known person.

It must preserve, where applicable:

- exact person identity;
- role at contribution time;
- department at contribution time;
- material or information supplied;
- communication time;
- recording time;
- relevant context;
- knowledge basis;
- uncertainty;
- later correction, dispute, support, contradiction, supersession, or withdrawal where explicitly established.

InformationContribution remains distinct from:

- authorship;
- ownership;
- responsibility;
- subject-of-information;
- recorder identity;
- approval;
- Decision;
- Evidence status;
- credibility;
- trustworthiness.

## Historical integrity

Current role or department changes must not rewrite contribution-time organizational context.

Corrections must preserve attributable history according to the adopted correction and retention rules.

## Person Workbench

The Person Workbench is an operational context surface.

It must not become:

- a dossier;
- a general HR file;
- a reputation profile;
- a performance-scoring surface;
- a behavioral profile;
- an unrestricted manager-notes archive.

## Subjective-information disposition

The reviewed dispositions are adopted. In particular:

- satisfaction remains excluded pending a separate policy Decision;
- telemetry-derived engagement is prohibited;
- strengths are not core Person facts;
- development areas are excluded from the Living Model core;
- workload/capacity should prefer role/process-level representation;
- manager observations remain exceptional, attributable, time-bound, disputable, and purpose-specific.

## Anti-surveillance and anti-scoring boundary

Management OS must not introduce:

- covert monitoring;
- productivity surveillance;
- private-content mining;
- behavioral profiling;
- speculative people inference;
- trust scoring;
- credibility scoring;
- reliability scoring;
- competence scoring;
- performance scoring derived from information contributions;
- automated promotion recommendations;
- automated termination recommendations;
- automated employment judgment.

## Watson boundary

Future Watson access to people-related context must remain source-linked, minimized, and non-judgmental.

Watson may eventually surface traceable organizational patterns and questions. It must not infer or judge:

- honesty;
- competence;
- loyalty;
- motive;
- personality;
- mental state;
- trustworthiness;
- employee value;
- performance;
- promotion suitability;
- termination suitability.

No Watson execution is authorized by this Decision.

## People-free separability

The people-free Living Organizational Model path remains valid and independently materializable using:

- departments;
- unnamed roles;
- processes;
- systems;
- SOPs;
- KPIs;
- interactions;
- meetings by role or department;
- reports;
- artifacts;
- known, unknown, and disputed organizational context.

This Decision does not authorize materialization or implementation of that path.

## Human rationale

The Living Organizational Model requires people context to represent how an organization actually functions, but this creates material risks if subjective, sensitive, or historical information is treated casually.

The reviewed People & Privacy model provides the necessary Product boundaries to support operational understanding while preserving attribution, historical integrity, data minimization, correction, restricted visibility, and human dignity.

It also allows Person-as-Source to become useful organizational memory without turning contributions into credibility, trust, or performance scores.
