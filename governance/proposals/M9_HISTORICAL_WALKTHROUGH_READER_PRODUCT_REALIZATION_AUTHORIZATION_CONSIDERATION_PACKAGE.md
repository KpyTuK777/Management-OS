Exactly one disposition may be selected by the accountable human. The ordering
below carries no preference or recommendation.

### Disposition A — Authorize bounded Product Realization preparation for Historical Walkthrough Reader

**Authority effect**

- grants bounded authority to prepare the selected slice at the product-
  realization level within its fixed definition;
- permits refinement of user-facing interaction requirements and a separately
  reviewable product-realization package; and
- permits preparation of later Engineering Architecture authority consideration
  if the bounded product work demonstrates that it is required.

**Permitted work**

- preserve and elaborate the fixed user, job, entry, exit, interaction sequence,
  required capabilities, canonical-concept mapping, exclusions, and success boundary;
- prepare interaction requirements and review criteria without screen design or
  technical allocation;
- identify unresolved product questions and technical questions that require
  later separate authority;
- prepare independent review artifacts; and
- prepare a separate Engineering Architecture consideration only after the
  product boundary is sufficiently fixed.

**Prohibited work**

- Engineering Architecture;
- screens, wireframes, mockups, component inventories, data schemas, or APIs;
- prototype, coding, tests, deployment, delivery, or user-interaction execution;
- metrics, KPIs, or experiments;
- Replay, H3 validation, Root Cause, causal proof, or canonical change.

**Control-surface implication and future transition**

A recorded Disposition A would still not modify repository operational state.
A separate bounded transition would be required before preparation begins. The
minimum transition should modify only `architecture/ROADMAP.md`,
`architecture/BACKLOG.md`, and one transition record: reconcile the completed
walkthrough state, preserve M9/M9.1 unless separately changed by human authority,
close the obsolete active walkthrough-execution item, and create exactly one
active item for bounded Historical Walkthrough Reader Product Realization
preparation. It must not open Engineering Architecture or implementation.

**Risks and non-effects**

- product requirements may overfit the Knight Capital case;
- elaboration may drift into technical design unless the boundary is reviewed;
- authority does not prove usability, implementation readiness, or H3; and
- Alternatives B and C remain available later.

**Prohibited work**
