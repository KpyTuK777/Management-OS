**Decision:** No Decision

**Disposition:** NONE

This artifact prepares a structure for a possible future attributable human
Decision. It does not record that Decision, select a Product Value Hypothesis,
select a case or scenario, or authorize empirical work.

## 1. Decision identity

The only Decision identity supported by this structure is:

> **Which Product Value Hypothesis, if any, should be selected as the first
> bounded subject for empirical-preparation consideration within M9?**

Selection would identify only one uncertainty to examine next. It remains
distinct from hypothesis validation, case or scenario selection, Evidence
access, validation authorization, operational Decision or Change, Engineering
Architecture, implementation, delivery, and product-value proof.

No Decision under this identity may silently decide any other identity.

Select **H5 — Manager Capability Development** as the first bounded subject for

the singular Decision identity.

## 4. Required human-owned inputs

A future attributable Decision must contain:

- accountable authority identity;
- authority basis;
- selected disposition;
- human-owned rationale;
- fixed hypothesis identity where applicable;
- explicit non-effects;
- decision date; and
- attributable human attestation.

AI must not infer, populate, select, complete, simulate, or attest any of these
fields. Repository authorship, prior conversation, apparent preference,
analytical completeness, or silence cannot substitute for attributable human
input.

## 5. Disposition-specific completeness

For A–E, the record must include exactly one selected disposition and its exact
corresponding fixed hypothesis identity. A mismatch, abbreviation that changes
meaning, multiple hypotheses, or absent hypothesis identity leaves the Decision
invalid under this structure.

For F, the human-owned rationale must identify what additional decision
evidence is needed without creating an automatic reconsideration or deadline.

For G, the rationale must apply to the current Decision identity and fixed input
basis without implying rejection or falsification of H1–H5.

Every disposition requires the accountable authority identity, authority basis,
explicit non-effects, Decision date, and attributable human attestation.

## 6. Maximum effect of dispositions A–E

A valid future A–E disposition may at most:

- record one fixed selected Product Value Hypothesis;
- authorize preparation of case or scenario suitability consideration for that
  hypothesis;
- authorize preparation of Evidence-access and Outcome-observability questions;
  and
- permit refinement of the bounded empirical question through a separate
  transition.

The separate-transition requirement means the selection record itself does not
mutate roadmap, backlog, stage, validation, Evidence-access, Engineering
Architecture, or implementation state.

## 7. Explicit non-effects

No disposition under this Decision identity may:

- select a case or scenario;
- treat synthetic material as Evidence;
- authorize Evidence access;
- approve validation design;
- authorize validation execution;
- authorize operational Change;
- authorize Engineering Architecture;
- authorize prototype, implementation, or delivery;
- define KPIs, metrics, or experiments;
- establish product value;
- validate, prove, prioritize, or rank a hypothesis;
- select a second hypothesis; or
- change canonical architecture or source ownership.

F and G record no hypothesis selection and create no preparation authority for a
selected hypothesis. None of the dispositions creates an automatic next step.
