# 3. Candidate Inquiry Shapes

The five shapes below are assessed symmetrically. Their order communicates no
preference, rank, default, or recommendation.

## Shape A — Full managerial Decision walkthrough

This shape would attempt to reconstruct actor-specific information, an attributable
Decision owner, rationale, selected alternative, Change, Outcome, original Learning,
and Replay.

Current Evidence supports bounded operational occurrences, later regulatory
findings, certain organizational actions, and immediate aggregate Outcomes. It does
not materially support the central human Decision identity, actor-specific
information, rationale, original Learning, or a credible counterfactual Outcome.

Shape A must not be made viable through inferred ownership, plausible rationale,
assumed alert recognition, or retrospective assignment of foresight.

## Shape B — Bounded operational-control walkthrough

This shape would focus on preparation and deployment, actual production state,
control visibility, incident emergence, alerts and detection, response actions,
containment uncertainty, immediate Outcome, and later official findings.

The fixed record offers qualified support for these operational elements, especially
through the later SEC findings. It does not support one coherent accountable
Decision narrative. Any Shape B preparation would therefore have to preserve
differences among action, observation, Decision, rationale, intended Change, Actual
Change, response, and verified containment.

## Shape C — Source-limited chronology walkthrough

This shape would focus on what the public record establishes, what became known
retrospectively, where Decision and rationale remain absent, how hindsight changes
interpretation, and what Additional Learning might arise from disciplined
preservation of uncertainty.

It is reconstruction-focused rather than Decision-focused. Its historical claims
could remain closely coupled to source authority and publication time. It could not
turn reconstruction discipline into verified organizational Learning or infer a
historical Decision merely to create a Replay pivot.

## Shape D — Defer pending additional sources

This shape would pause walkthrough preparation until additional authoritative public
material is found or access conditions change. The existing preparation artifacts
would remain intact, and current source insufficiencies would remain unresolved.

Deferral would not predict that additional material exists, create authority to
search or ingest sources, or create an automatic return date.

## Shape E — Decline Knight Capital walkthrough under the current evidence identity

This shape would preserve all preparation artifacts while ending this case under
the current fixed source basis. It would not invalidate the candidate selection,
source manifest, chronology, Evidence-gap assessment, or research already recorded.

Decline under this identity would not reject Knight Capital permanently or select a
different case.

# 6. Decision and Replay Boundary

Because attributable operational Decision and rationale are materially absent, a
future walkthrough must not:

- fabricate a Decision owner;
- attribute one coherent rationale to the organization;
- assume alert recognition;
- assign foresight using later regulatory findings; or
- present alternative reasoning as what historical actors should necessarily have
  done.

Possible Replay, if separately authorized in the future, may be bounded to:

- alternative control checks;
- alternative escalation questions;
- alternative deployment-verification reasoning; and
- alternative uncertainty handling.

It must not model a confident alternative historical Outcome.

```text
Response action ≠ attributable Decision
Attributable Decision ≠ known rationale
Known later failure ≠ Evidence of what should have been known then
Alternative reasoning ≠ historical reasoning
Replay uncertainty ≠ permission to fill uncertainty
```

This package neither prepares nor authorizes Replay.

# 7. Original Learning Boundary

Original organizational Learning is materially absent. Therefore:

- a walkthrough cannot compare verified original Learning against later Additional
  Learning as if both were equally observable;
- regulatory findings may be treated as later public findings, not internalized
  organizational Learning; and
- later remediation statements must remain distinct from verified sustained
  adoption.

A future H3 inquiry could consider, without assuming a positive result, whether
re-engagement produces:

- additional analyst or learner insight;
- improved recognition of uncertainty;
- better distinction among Decision, Change, and Outcome; or
- identification of missing knowledge.

These are permissible subjects for later consideration, not claims of capability
development, Product Value, validation, or realized Additional Learning. The
absence of original Learning makes comparison structurally asymmetric and must
remain visible.

# 8. Walkthrough-Limiting Versus Replay-Limiting Gaps

## Walkthrough-limiting gaps

These threaten the accuracy or boundedness of historical reconstruction itself:

- detailed technical reconstruction relies mainly on retrospective findings;
- raw code, configuration, logs, order data, and internal communications are absent;
- deployment, detection, response, stopping, and trade-out timing is incomplete;
- actor-specific awareness cannot be reconstructed;
- operational Decision owner and rationale are materially absent;
- Change fidelity and verified containment are incomplete;
- multidimensional and long-term Outcome remains fragmented; and
- original organizational Learning is materially absent.

## Replay-limiting gaps

These may permit some qualified historical reconstruction while preventing credible
alternative reasoning or Outcome comparison:

- no reliable actor-specific information state exists for the principal windows;
- no fixed accountable operational Decision and rationale exists;
- recognized-warning state cannot be assumed from message generation;
- available alternatives and response hypotheses are unknown;
- exact Actual Change and containment states are uncertain;
- Outcome aggregates do not support confident counterfactual estimation;
- original Learning is unavailable for symmetric comparison; and
- detailed 2013 findings create substantial hindsight-contamination risk.

A bounded walkthrough could remain useful when it stays within materially supported
or explicitly qualified historical claims and treats the unknowns as unknowns.
That does not make Replay available. A particular inquiry may therefore be
historically reconstructable yet unsuitable for a particular Replay question.
