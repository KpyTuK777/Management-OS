## Decision object

The accountable human is deciding whether to adopt the reviewed interaction behavior for:

- primary organization entry;
- Organization Constructor;
- Organization Map;
- Department Workbench;
- Role and Person Workbenches;
- Person-as-Source / InformationContribution;
- processes, systems, SOPs, KPIs, reports, meetings, and interdepartmental interactions;

- Operational Memory;
- future Watson placement;

Adoption would determine how the Product should behave before Engineering and implementation are considered. It would not select how the behavior is built.

## Interaction principles under Option A

Option A would adopt these obligations:

- organization entry begins progressive understanding rather than Investigation or a setup wizard;
- useful work starts with minimal information and remains honest in incomplete states;
- one primary workspace and one natural next action dominate each context;
- selection opens focused inspection without losing organizational orientation;
- navigation to dedicated detail is explicit and preserves a return path;
- human language leads, while technical/source/version metadata remains inspectable but subordinate;
- visual density follows operational meaning and current focus, not schema size;
- hover, keyboard focus, active/pressed, selected, and non-color state feedback are required;
- current state remains primary; history is progressively inspectable;
- narrow screens preserve meaning, authority, safe correction, and honest degradation rather than miniaturizing desktop.

## First use and Constructor under Option A

First use asks only for organization identity, then opens a sparse useful workspace with a dominant **Create/Add first department** action and secondary paths for another known organizational element or explicit unknown.

Constructor work happens in context:

```text
orient
  -> add the smallest meaningful element/relationship
  -> record minimum known information and source state
  -> place/connect it explicitly
  -> inspect
  -> refine, continue, or leave
```

Unknown, missing, disputed, stale, and not applicable remain distinct. Roles may exist without named people. Hierarchy and reporting changes use semantic previews; presentational arrangement cannot mutate organizational meaning. The Constructor is not ERP/CRM administration, a database editor, giant form, or mandatory wizard.

## Organization Map under Option A

The Map is a navigable structural overview, not an org chart decoration or universal graph.

- Department anchors and organization scope remain stable.
- One primary relationship lens controls emphasis.
- The selected object may retain a bounded, typed set of material cross-lens dependencies.
- Semantic scale moves from organization to department to focused-object density.
- Search-to-focus, hierarchy, breadcrumbs, layer filtering, and an unknown/unplaced area support large organizations.
- Selection and navigation remain distinct.
- Relationship lines require type, direction, label, and accessible meaning; they do not imply causality.
- Restricted existence never leaks through connectors, gaps, counts, or “hidden” placeholders.

## Workbenches under Option A

### Department

An operational workspace centered on current purpose, structure, roles/responsibilities, processes, systems/SOPs/measures, reports/meetings, interactions, unresolved/conflicting context, current Investigation/Improvement, and progressively disclosed history. It is not a profile page or equal-card schema dashboard.

### Role

Shows organizational expectations, responsibility, decision boundaries, process/system/SOP/interaction relationships, occupancy history, and unresolved assignment questions.

### Person

Shows only permitted, purpose-filtered organizational context, current and historical capacity, operational relationships, and narrow source-linked contribution/participation. It excludes dossier, HR, subjective/sensitive, communications/activity, inferred-trait, scoring, ranking, and unrelated-history behavior.

## Process and operational-object interactions under Option A

Process begins with `Input → Process → Output → Result`. Trigger, owner, participants, systems, SOPs, decisions, handoffs, time, exceptions, constraints, risks, and feedback appear progressively.

- Map shows presence and major dependencies.
- Department shows local summaries and handoffs.
- Process detail holds the progressive operational model.
- Inspector holds focused relationship, state, source, and history detail.

Systems, SOPs, and KPIs/measures share summary-to-detail behavior but retain separate meanings. Management OS represents operational use and relationships; it does not administer ERP/CRM, edit source SOPs without source authority, or turn KPIs into causal/person judgments.

Interdepartmental interactions use one directional source relationship with multiple authorized projections across Map, Department, Process, Investigation, and Improvement.

## Person-as-Source under Option A

The primary question is:

> **Хто надав цю інформацію?**

The user may browse `Organization → Department → Person` or search. The interaction confirms person, contribution-time role/department, communication time, and the source relationship. Direct/indirect knowledge, uncertainty, subject, and correction/dispute details appear progressively.

Historical capacity defaults only from one unambiguous authoritative effective interval. Otherwise it remains **to confirm**, with explicit historical selection or unknown; current role is never a silent fallback.

Completion creates only the permitted source relationship—not Evidence, fact, approval, responsibility, credibility, or trust. Technical relationship identifiers and provenance locators remain in Inspector.

## Investigation and Improvement entry under Option A

Investigation uses:

> **Дослідити** — зрозуміти, що відбувається.

Improvement uses:

> **Запланувати вдосконалення** — спланувати свідому зміну.

They are visually and linguistically distinct. Entry previews use only already authorized, purpose-compatible context; named-person context requires explicit inclusion. Hidden context, counts, and omissions do not leak.

Inherited context does not automatically become Evidence, Working Set, Hypothesis, fact, failure, benefit, Decision, or execution authority. Investigation remains internally unchanged. Improvement preserves healthy-state possibility and optional change.

## Memory and Watson under Option A

Operational Memory is a current-first, progressively disclosed history of prior Investigations, Improvements, Decisions, changes, Monitoring, Stabilization, and organizational-state versions. Historical comparison is explicitly labeled and cannot become current truth, Evidence, Cause, or repeat authority.

Future Watson is compact, contextual, quiet, and absent when it has nothing useful to add. It may ask one bounded question or reference source-linked organizational context after separate AI authority. It cannot dominate the Workbench, decide, mutate, judge people, reveal restricted context, infer Cause, or block manual work. Option A authorizes no Watson execution.

## Responsive and empty-state model under Option A

- Large desktop: primary Map/Workbench with coexisting focused Inspector where useful.
- Laptop: primary workspace remains dominant; secondary/history/Working Set collapse first.
- Tablet: one primary context, focused drawers/sheets, bounded construction, complex rearrangement may hand off.
- 390 px mobile: orientation, search, summary, inspection, history, safe correction/dispute initiation, and contextual entry remain; universal Map arrangement and complex adjudication may defer honestly.

Mobile correction/dispute content and status cannot be silently discarded or made dependent on immediate desktop access.

Empty states use short truthful labels and one useful action. Authorized restriction may be named narrowly; unauthorized existence produces no placeholder, count, connector, search suggestion, History event, or Watson reference.
