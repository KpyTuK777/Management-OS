## 2. User-Facing Interaction Gap

The repository can execute and audit a bounded historical walkthrough, but the
accountable human cannot yet traverse that walkthrough as a Management OS user.
The present surfaces are Markdown artifacts, Git identities, source citations,
and long claim records. They do not provide a coherent user interaction that lets
the human progressively orient, inspect Evidence and uncertainty, maintain a
Working Set, recognize Decision boundaries, and provide attributable reflection.

Therefore:

```text
Repository walkthrough completion ≠ user-performed walkthrough
Method conformance ≠ usable product interaction
Human non-participation ≠ negative product assessment
Insufficient assessment basis ≠ invalidation of H3
```

## 3. Minimum Interaction Requirements

### Required in the first bounded slice

- a clear case or situation entry point and bounded exit;
- progressive context rather than an undifferentiated document corpus;
- navigable phase or situation orientation;
- direct inspection of Evidence, source identity, attribution, and claim linkage;
- visible uncertainty, Evidence gaps, hindsight boundaries, and authority limits;
- a persistent, inspectable Working Set distinct from accepted Evidence;
- explicit Decision boundaries without invented historical Decision ownership;
- attributable human-response and reflection capture;
- a readable visual and interaction hierarchy that does not require Git or
  repository expertise;
- preservation of Shape B, citation discipline, prohibited inferences, and the
  historical/Replay boundary.

### Valuable but deferrable

- full-text search and advanced filters;
- cross-case comparison;
- saved views, annotations, and collaborative review;
- export and presentation modes;
- adaptive personalization;
- rich automation or AI assistance beyond bounded orientation and traceability;
- advanced accessibility refinements beyond a usable accessible baseline.

### Outside the first bounded slice

- Replay or counterfactual execution;
- live operational-system integration;
- autonomous operational action;
- multi-case portfolio management;
- organization-wide collaboration and permissions;
- canonical-methodology changes;
- Engineering Architecture, production deployment, or complete MVP delivery;
- metrics, KPIs, experiments, or an H3 validation conclusion.

## 4. Neutral Product-Slice Alternatives

No alternative is selected or recommended. Effort labels are relative discovery
estimates only; they are not delivery estimates or implementation plans.

### Alternative A — Historical Walkthrough Reader

- **Primary user:** accountable owner or manager reviewing one completed,
  source-bound historical case.
- **User job:** orient to the case, progressively inspect the fixed walkthrough,
  understand claim/source/uncertainty boundaries, and provide attributable
  reflection.
- **Entry and exit:** enter through the fixed Knight Capital case identity; exit
  after an attributable response or explicit deferral.
- **Minimum sequence:** case orientation → phase navigation → claim and source
  inspection → uncertainty and boundary inspection → Working Set → human response.
- **Canonical concepts exercised:** Operational Case, Current Situation, Evidence,
  Working Set, progressive disclosure, traceability, uncertainty, and reflection.
- **What it could teach:** whether the completed Shape B material becomes
  understandable and usable when presented as a product interaction; whether a
  directly participating human then has a valid basis for bounded H3 reflection.
- **What it cannot establish:** usefulness for live management work, general H3
  validity, a complete management cycle, Root Cause, causal proof, or the value of Replay.
- **Prerequisites:** fixed walkthrough artifacts and existing canonical experience
  semantics; a future bounded Product Realization Decision and separate technical
  authority before any realization work.
- **Relative effort and risk:** lowest relative scope; primary risk is overfitting
  the interaction to one retrospective case.
- **MVP relationship:** narrow precursor or vertical reading slice, not an MVP.
- **H3 assessment potential:** `PARTIALLY READY` conceptually; valid assessment
  remains impossible until an attributable human actually uses an authorized
  interaction.

### Alternative B — Guided Investigation Slice

- **Primary user:** owner or manager working through a bounded new operational
  situation.
- **User job:** establish Current Situation, inspect Evidence, form a Working Set,
  expose uncertainty, and reach a human Decision boundary.
- **Entry and exit:** enter with one bounded situation and source set; exit at an
  attributable Decision, deferral, or escalation boundary.
- **Minimum sequence:** situation entry → progressive orientation → Evidence
  inspection → Working Set formation → uncertainty review → human judgment boundary.
- **Canonical concepts exercised:** Guided Investigation, Current Situation,
  Operational Workbench, Evidence, Working Set, Decision, and human authority.
- **What it could teach:** whether core management reasoning support is usable
  outside repository execution and whether structured re-engagement supports H3.
- **What it cannot establish:** end-to-end management-cycle completeness,
  operational Outcome improvement, general H3 validity, or implementation readiness.
- **Prerequisites:** a selected bounded situation, permissible Evidence access,
  participant and authority boundaries, plus separate Product Realization and
  technical authority.
- **Relative effort and risk:** moderate relative scope; risks include ambiguous
  live Evidence, participant authority, privacy, and premature expansion into a
  complete product workflow.
- **MVP relationship:** stronger vertical product slice and possible precursor to
  an MVP, but not the complete management cycle.
- **H3 assessment potential:** `PARTIALLY READY` conceptually; a valid conclusion
  would still require attributable human use under a separately authorized method.

### Alternative C — Bounded Practice-Case Workbench

- **Primary user:** owner or manager practicing structured judgment on a prepared,
  non-live case.
- **User job:** orient to progressively disclosed material, maintain a Working Set,
  make attributable judgments, and reflect on the experience.
- **Entry and exit:** enter through a fixed practice-case brief; exit after a
  bounded judgment and reflection sequence.
- **Minimum sequence:** case entry → progressive disclosure → Evidence and
  uncertainty inspection → Working Set → human judgment → reflection.
- **Canonical concepts exercised:** Operational Workbench, Guided Investigation,
  Evidence, uncertainty, Working Set, Decision boundaries, and reflection.
- **What it could teach:** whether repeated, direct interaction supports attention,
  memory, and judgment preparation without requiring live operational access.
- **What it cannot establish:** historical truth, live-work transfer, improved
  operational Outcomes, general H3 validity, Root Cause, causal proof, or Replay value.
- **Prerequisites:** a separately governed practice case, explicit separation from
  historical claims and Replay, and separate Product Realization and technical authority.
- **Relative effort and risk:** highest of the three relative scopes; risks include
  simulation bias, confusing practice material with Evidence, and accidental
  creation of unauthorized Replay semantics.
- **MVP relationship:** a learning-oriented precursor, not the real-work MVP.
- **H3 assessment potential:** `PARTIALLY READY` conceptually, provided future
  authority defines a valid human-use boundary; no Replay is authorized here.
