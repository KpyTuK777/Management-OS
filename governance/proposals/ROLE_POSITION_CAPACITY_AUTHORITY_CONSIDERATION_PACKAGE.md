## Why a human Decision is indispensable

Existing authority defines Role, Person, role occupancy, department membership, and InformationContribution, but it does not define Position/Slot identity or planned capacity. Implementation cannot choose between a temporal Role capacity assertion, a governed Position/Slot, or deferral without creating canonical meaning. If A or B is selected, source/custody must then be selected on its separate axis.

## Decision options

- **A — temporal planned-capacity assertion:** no Position identity; preserve direct Person → Role occupancy.
- **B — governed Position/Slot identity:** explicit slot lifecycle; reconcile direct role occupancy as authoritative or derived.
- **D — defer capacity representation:** continue without planned-capacity, Position/Slot, or vacancy claims while preserving adopted Person → Role occupancy semantics and its current runtime non-activation.
- **Return for revision:** identify the exact unresolved clause; no implementation follows.

Source/custody is a separate axis after A or B: Operating-Model-owned under accountable-human authority, source-backed, or unavailable pending an authoritative source. It is not a peer representation option.

## Recommended decision framing

The Decision should answer:

> How shall Management OS canonically represent planned Role position capacity—temporal Role-in-Department assertion, governed Position/Slot identity, or intentional deferral—while preserving Role/Person separation, the adopted role-occupancy family, truthful vacancy meaning, and historical InformationContribution context?

If A or B is selected, it must also state:

- whether capacity means planned, approved, funded, or observed;
- authoritative source/update owner;
- effective-time scope.

Privacy filtering already precedes projection and counts. Detailed aggregate release, protected absence, migration, and successor Interaction/Engineering/implementation preparation remain separate follow-on work after representation selection.

## Recommended disposition

Neutral human consideration. Repository evidence does not authorize choosing A or B autonomously. Option A is the smaller model when only aggregate planning is required; Option B is necessary only if individual position identity/lifecycle is a real Product requirement. Neither may be inferred from the Northstar example alone. Deferral affects only capacity/Position/vacancy meaning and does not withdraw adopted occupancy semantics.
