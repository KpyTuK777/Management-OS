## Human-owned direction

Management OS should become the persistent operational environment in which an operational manager progressively understands, models, observes, investigates, changes, improves, and controls an organization. The product should begin conceptually from a progressively refinable Living Organizational Model, not from Investigation.

Investigation remains intact as a bounded mode for understanding an unclear symptom, deviation, contradiction, or suspected problem. A distinct sibling mode, Improvement / Вдосконалення, should support deliberate system development when the current organization may be functioning adequately.

The proposed upper loop is:

Living Organizational Model → continuous understanding and observation → Investigation or Improvement → human Decision → source-owned Implementation / Intervention → Monitoring → Stabilization → Operational Memory → updated Living Organizational Model → continued operational control.

## Required organizational scope

The candidate model must be capable, where supported, of representing organization identity and purpose; departments, teams, roles and people; formal and actual reporting, responsibility, authority and working relationships; information flows, handoffs and dependencies; processes, SOPs and KPIs; CRM, ERP and other systems; documents, reports, meetings and artifacts; constraints, uncertainties, history and relationships.

It must preserve distinctions between formal and actual structure, documented and real practice, assigned and actual responsibility, system-of-record data and manager observation, confirmed and provisional understanding, and current and historical state. Incompleteness is an honest state, not a setup error.

## Person-as-Source discovery

Where a relevant person exists in the organizational model, Investigation material should be able to relate its source through Organization → Department → Person rather than retain only free text. The relationship must preserve the exact person, department and role at contribution time, material or observation, Investigation, contribution time, communication form, directness and explicitly established uncertainty or later status.

This relationship is not authorship, ownership, responsibility, subject-of-information, truth, credibility, competence, motive or performance. A Person Workbench may show an inspectable chronology of knowledge contributions, but must never become a surveillance, trust, reputation or employee-scoring surface.

## Explicit boundaries

This direction does not:

- amend canonical sources;
- create a seventh P1–P6 layer;
- authorize Organization Constructor, Improvement, Person Workbench or source-selector implementation;
- change Investigation semantics or make inherited organizational context Evidence;
- authorize AI reasoning, diagnosis, judgment, execution, integrations, deployment, H3, Replay or causal proof;
- modify ROADMAP.md, BACKLOG.md or active implementation authority.
