## Decision object

The accountable human is deciding only whether to adopt this Product meaning:

> Improvement is a bounded, progressive sibling work mode inside the Living Organizational Model for considering and coordinating an optional deliberate change when sufficient understanding exists to design change responsibly.

The adopted distinction would be:

- **Investigation:** “I need to understand what is happening and why.”
- **Improvement:** “I understand enough to consider a deliberate change.”

Improvement does not imply failure, incident, defect, bad performance, Root Cause, compulsory correction, proven benefit, approval, or success. The current system may be healthy. Change may remain optional and may not proceed.

## Investigation boundary and transition contract under Option A

Investigation remains internally unchanged and retains inquiry, Evidence, reasoning, uncertainty reduction, and Investigation-specific process understanding.

- **Improvement → Investigation:** create a distinct linked Investigation when insufficient understanding, contradiction, unknown dependency, unexplained behavior, or another material uncertainty requires inquiry. Improvement may pause, continue only in unaffected areas, narrow, remain optional, or stop. Assumptions cannot be laundered into facts or Evidence.
- **Investigation → Improvement:** create a distinct linked Improvement when sufficient understanding supports optional deliberate-change consideration rather than compulsory correction of an established failure.

Both transitions require an attributable human act and reason. They preserve separate identities, versions, owners, histories, uncertainty, source links, and closure. Neither mode silently converts into or absorbs the other.

The applicable Operational Matter retains canonical identity, macro-state, lineage, Decision, and Outcome references. Opportunity-based entry cannot bypass Matter authority.
