## 2. Readiness method

The assessment applies eleven qualitative, non-compensatory checks required by
the preparation request. A check passes only when the structural preparation
preserves its own scope and applicable semantic and authority boundaries.

The only possible readiness dispositions are:

- **Ready for authority consideration** — the preparation inputs are present,
  structurally complete, neutral, and bounded enough to receive direct human-
  owned Decision inputs; or
- **Not ready due to missing preparation inputs** — one or more structural
  inputs required for legitimate consideration are absent or materially
  deficient.

Readiness does not mean a hypothesis should be selected. Intentionally empty
human-owned fields are expected at this point and are not structural
deficiencies. They prevent the shell from becoming a Decision until an
accountable human directly completes them.
