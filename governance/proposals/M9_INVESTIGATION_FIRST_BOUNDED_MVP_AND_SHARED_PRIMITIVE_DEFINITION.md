# M9 Investigation-first Bounded MVP and Shared Primitive Definition

## Definition identity

- Basis: attributable Investigation-first direction and `M9_CANONICAL_PRODUCT_MATERIALIZATION_INVENTORY.md`
- State: PREPARED FOR REVIEW
- Implementation authority: NOT CREATED
- Scope: one broad, honest, runnable Investigation experience using already fixed meaning

## Primary user outcome

An accountable manager can open one operational Investigation, understand the Current Situation, collect and inspect bounded local material, form a Working Set, expose unknowns and contradictions, develop provisional hypotheses, preserve source traceability, and reach an explicit boundary where attributable human judgment or new authority is required.

The MVP is not a complete operational lifecycle, external integration platform, autonomous analysis system, or production deployment.

## Product boundary

### Included as working behavior

1. Enter a coherent Management OS shell and open one Investigation.
2. See Investigation identity, bounded purpose, status, and Current Situation.
3. Distinguish symptoms, context, supported material, uncertainty, and missing Evidence.
4. Add bounded local sample material and inspect type, source, attribution, provenance, and status.
5. Add and remove material from a Working Set without changing its evidentiary status.
6. Represent unknowns and contradictions without forced resolution.
7. Create bounded provisional hypotheses and connect supporting or contradicting Evidence.
8. Inspect focused items and relationships without losing Investigation context.
9. Preserve explicit distinctions among event time, record/publication time, uncertain time, and absent time wherever the bounded data supports them.
10. Show the boundary among analysis, system proposal, human acceptance, and attributable Decision.

### Included only as partial or read-only behavior

- local Current Situation revision demonstration with visible prior revision;
- bounded local/manual material representation, not external acquisition;
- one-session or browser-local context persistence;
- fixed supported Relationship types;
- static or read-only timeline illustration until its interaction contract is complete;
- responsive and accessibility baseline subject to defined browser verification;
- honest empty, loading, degraded, unavailable, and error states;
- disabled or explanatory AI and external-integration affordances.

### Explicitly excluded

- actual external integrations or source acquisition;
- autonomous AI inquiry, fact generation, decision, or mutation;
- authoritative Decision recording;
- Intervention, Change, Monitoring, Stabilization, Closure, or Reopening execution;
- complete cross-device or production persistence;
- unrestricted relationship ontology;
- full timeline authoring;
- Historical Replay or counterfactual exploration;
- H3 validation, Root Cause, causal proof, metrics, KPIs, experiments, deployment, or production infrastructure.

## Experience composition

### Global product context

The shell identifies Management OS and keeps Investigation as the primary MVP destination. The Historical Walkthrough Reader remains discoverable as a supporting practice and evidence-discipline module, not the home or central user-value path.

### Investigation context

The persistent Investigation envelope shows Matter/Investigation identity, status, Current Situation version, active Working Set, current focus, and return path. Presentation never implies that context is complete organizational truth.

### Primary workbench

The workbench composes:

- Current Situation;
- material and Evidence view;
- Working Set;
- unknowns and contradictions;
- hypotheses and bounded Relationships;
- timeline illustration where supported;
- focused-item Inspector;
- visible human-judgment boundary.

Progressive disclosure follows the Wave 1.5 discipline:

> understand → inspect → verify provenance → inspect full metadata

Technical identities support verification and do not dominate first comprehension.

## Historical Walkthrough Reader disposition

The Reader should:

- remain a supporting learning and evidence-discipline module;
- pause before full Wave 2 unless separately authorized;
- retain completed structural and Design Bible work;
- retain Wave 1.5 preparation without bypass or overwrite;
- contribute only semantically proven shared primitives;
- never become the canonical source for Investigation-specific meaning;
- be reconciled after the Investigation stages so shared shell, inspection, traceability, responsive, and accessibility behavior do not drift.

Reader historical artifacts remain read-only. Reader implementation files may be changed under a future positive authority only where the reviewed plan expressly permits shared-primitive reconciliation or a separately authorized Reader correction.

## Visual and language boundary

The fixed Design Bible governs atmosphere: professional operational-analysis workspace, executive/strategy environment, calm structure, material honesty, editorial hierarchy, warm paper, dark wood, restrained brass, and no theatrical detective-board imagery.

Primary language is concise, comprehensible Ukrainian. Canonical, technical, governance, and source identities remain inspectable. Plain language may not delete uncertainty or alter fixed meaning.

## Acceptance boundary

The bounded MVP is definition-ready when a reviewer can trace every included behavior to the inventory and every partial/excluded behavior remains visibly partial, unavailable, or outside scope. This definition does not make the MVP implementation-ready by itself; the reviewed materialization plan and attributable implementation authority remain mandatory.
