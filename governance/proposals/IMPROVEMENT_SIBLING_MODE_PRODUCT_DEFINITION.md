## Product definition

**Improvement is a bounded, progressive work mode for considering and coordinating an optional deliberate change when the accountable human understands enough of the current situation to design change responsibly.**

Its human question is:

> I understand enough to consider a deliberate change.

Improvement may begin because the organization wants to automate, prepare to scale, replace a CRM or ERP, redesign an SOP, redistribute responsibility, simplify an interaction, reduce manual work, improve resilience or quality, or deliberately evolve its operating model. The current operation may be healthy and valuable.

Improvement therefore never means, by itself:

- that a failure, incident, defect, or bad performance exists;
- that Root Cause has been established or is required;
- that a corrective action is necessary;
- that the proposal is justified, beneficial, approved, or safe;
- that implementation or a favorable Outcome will follow.

An improvement remains optional until an accountable human Decision establishes otherwise. A Decision that does not proceed is a valid result.

## Product role inside the Living Organizational Model

The Living Organizational Model is the persistent human-facing workspace and projection over the canonical Operational Operating Model. Improvement operates within that workspace; it does not create another organizational model, enterprise graph, or source of organizational truth.

Improvement may start from an authorized reference to:

- the organization;
- a department;
- a process;
- a system;
- an SOP;
- a role;
- an interaction;
- a recurring meeting;
- a report;
- an explicitly identified opportunity.

The starting reference supplies inherited organizational context. It does not automatically become Evidence, proof of benefit, proof of a problem, or an accepted premise. Material context preserves source, scope, freshness, sensitivity, and correction path. A decision-relevant historical snapshot is explicit and does not transfer source authority.

## Progressive work model

Improvement coordinates the following revisable concerns:

```text
Opportunity / Intent
  -> Current State
  -> Desired State
  -> Change Scope
  -> Affected Organizational Elements
  -> Dependencies
  -> Risks and Constraints
  -> Proposed Change
  -> Alternatives, where relevant
  -> Implementation Plan
  -> Accountable Human Decision
  -> Implementation / Intervention
  -> Monitoring
  -> Stabilization
  -> Operational Memory
  -> reviewed Operating Model update
  -> continued operational control
```

This is a completeness map, not a mandatory order, stage gate, maturity score, or linear wizard. Work may begin from any useful concern, move backward or forward, preserve unknowns, branch into alternatives, revise scope, pause, stop without change, or return after implementation learning. Proportionality applies: a low-risk reversible change may need a small record, while broad or hard-to-reverse change requires deeper reasoning and control.

### Opportunity / Intent

Preserves why change is being considered, the value worth protecting, the initiating human wording, timing, and whether the opportunity is exploratory or time-bound. Intent is not a diagnosis or Decision.

### Current State

References governed current context and records what is known, assumed, disputed, stale, or missing. A healthy current state and existing value remain visible. Improvement does not own or rewrite the Operating Model source.

### Desired State

Describes the intended operating condition and observable expectations without claiming that the proposed change will cause them. It may include conditions that should remain unchanged.

### Change Scope and affected elements

Bounds what may change and what is explicitly outside scope. Affected elements are typed references to source-owned departments, processes, systems, SOPs, roles, interactions, meetings, reports, controls, measures, or other governed records. Identification of an affected element creates no mutation authority.

### Dependencies, risks, and constraints

Preserves enabling conditions, sequencing dependencies, uncertainty, existing value at risk, unintended consequences, constraints, monitoring needs, and proportionate rollback or recovery considerations. A “better idea” does not justify change without an inspectable basis.

### Proposed Change and alternatives

Separates the proposed direction from the current state and from an authoritative Decision. Alternatives are included where meaningful, including defer, preserve the current state, run a bounded experiment, narrow scope, or do nothing. Decision Laboratory may provide non-authoritative modeled comparisons; its branches are not Evidence, Decisions, or authorization.

### Implementation Plan

Coordinates planning references, ownership, dependencies, sequencing, guardrails, validation, recovery, and monitoring conditions. Detailed execution records and mutations remain with their source capabilities. A plan is not execution authority.

### Accountable Human Decision

Only the accountable human may approve, reject, defer, narrow, condition, or request revision of the proposed change. The Decision preserves rationale, accepted uncertainty, alternatives considered, intended operating condition, guardrails, monitoring obligations, and whether implementation is authorized through the applicable source owners.

### Implementation / Intervention

After an executable Decision, the existing Operational Transition contract governs continuity. Operational Impact Review evaluates how the approved Decision should be absorbed; owner-approved adaptations hand off to Execution Planning; source-owned capabilities perform their own changes. Improvement coordinates references and continuity but does not become an execution system or acquire another capability’s write authority.

### Monitoring and Stabilization

Monitoring compares observable behavior with the Decision’s intended operating condition, guardrails, measures, and adverse-effect conditions. It does not convert temporal sequence or improvement into causal proof. Stabilization Assessment remains an Outcome contribution and may be stable, partially stable, unstable, indeterminate, or not yet assessable. Findings may lead to further approved action, Decision reconsideration, a linked Investigation, or stopping the change.

### Operational Memory, model review, and continued control

After proportionate Stabilization, the completed Improvement episode contributes an inspectable historical chain to Operational Memory:

- original intent and preserved prior value;
- accountable Decision and decision-time context;
- assumptions and unresolved uncertainty;
- approved implementation and source-owned execution references;
- monitoring results and measure definitions;
- observed side effects and stabilization disposition;
- lessons, contradictions, and follow-up conditions.

History is appended and versioned, never silently rewritten. Operational Memory is supporting historical context, not current truth, current Evidence, causal proof, or authority to repeat a change.

The stabilized result may inform a separately reviewed update to the canonical Operational Operating Model. Only the Operating Model’s governed correction/update path may change its records. The update preserves prior versions, effective time, source attribution, and the relationship to the Decision and observed result. Continued operational control then uses the reviewed current model while retaining the Improvement episode as history.

## Investigation boundary

**Investigation asks:**

> I need to understand what is happening and why.

**Improvement asks:**

> I understand enough to consider a deliberate change.

Investigation remains internally unchanged. It continues to own bounded inquiry episodes, uncertainty reduction, Evidence evaluation, competing explanations, process-understanding projections, and Decision Readiness concerning an Operational Matter. Improvement does not absorb Investigation, redefine its stages, weaken its Evidence rules, or turn its context into proof.

Improvement owns Product continuity for deliberate-change framing from opportunity through monitored stabilization. It does not own Matter identity or macro-state, authoritative Decisions, Operational Impact Review findings, Decision Laboratory branches, Execution Planning, source records, execution, Outcomes, Stabilization Assessment, Operational Memory, or Operating Model mutation.

## Transition contracts

Transitions create linked work; they never silently convert, relabel, merge, or delete a mode.

### Improvement to Investigation

Use when responsible change consideration exposes insufficient understanding, a material contradiction, an unknown dependency, unexplained behavior, or a premise that requires Evidence.

The attributable transition record preserves:

- originating Improvement identity, version, intent, scope, and owner;
- exact uncertainty or contradiction requiring Investigation;
- relevant context references, clearly not accepted as Evidence merely by transfer;
- assumptions and work paused, still possible, or invalidated;
- linked Investigation identity and attributable human reason.

Improvement may remain open, pause, narrow, or stop. Investigation findings return through an explicit human-reviewed link. They do not automatically approve, resume, or rewrite the Improvement.

### Investigation to Improvement

Use when sufficient understanding exists to consider an optional deliberate change and the outcome is not merely correction compelled by an established failure.

The attributable transition record preserves:

- originating Matter and Investigation identity and version;
- accepted findings, supporting and contradictory Evidence, and unresolved uncertainty;
- why optional change consideration is appropriate;
- proposed opportunity framing without claiming benefit or necessity;
- linked Improvement identity and attributable human reason.

The Investigation retains its identity, history, Evidence, reasoning, and disposition. Improvement references those records; it does not copy or reinterpret them as authority.

### Lineage and authority invariants

- Matter retains its canonical identity, macro-state, lifecycle, and linked Decision/Outcome references.
- Each Investigation and Improvement retains a distinct identity, state, history, accountable owner, and closure reason.
- Transition does not establish Root Cause, causal proof, Decision Readiness, change approval, or execution authority.
- New contradictory or material Evidence follows the existing Matter transitions and may return work to Decision Ready or Under Investigation.
- No-action, defer, preserve-current-state, and stop dispositions remain first-class outcomes.

## Change-safety contract

At every point the workspace preserves:

- uncertainty and assumptions separately from observed facts;
- optionality, including not proceeding;
- existing value and conditions that must not be damaged;
- dependencies and affected owners;
- unintended consequences and secondary risks;
- proportionate rollback, recovery, stop, or containment conditions where supported;
- monitoring ownership, measures, baselines, duration, and review conditions;
- uncertainty about benefit and causal mechanism before and after monitoring.

Implementation completion is not Outcome. Favorable Outcome is not causal proof. Monitoring may show mixed, delayed, adverse, or indeterminate effects. Stabilization does not erase residual uncertainty.

## Future Watson support boundary

Future separately authorized Watson support may:

- surface referenced dependencies and potentially affected processes;
- highlight conflicting SOPs or current-context gaps;
- retrieve repeated past change attempts and similar prior Improvements;
- locate relevant historical Decisions with source lineage;
- identify missing monitoring, rollback, recovery, or stop conditions;
- ask what could break or what current value could be lost;
- distinguish known, assumed, modeled, observed, and unresolved content.

Watson must not choose, approve, reject, prioritize, implement, or declare success for a change; infer causality without Evidence; convert similarity into applicability; override the accountable human; or mutate source-owned records. No AI behavior or implementation is authorized by this definition.

## Human Experience boundary

Improvement should feel like **designing deliberate change**; Investigation should continue to feel like **understanding uncertainty**. They remain siblings in one Product universe rather than separate products.

They may reuse Current State, Working Set, Inspector, organizational context, relationships, timeline/history, source traceability, progressive disclosure, and explicit human-judgment boundaries. Shared primitives retain one meaning. Mode-specific emphasis, language, work composition, and status may differ, but no Interaction design is adopted here.
