# Living Organization review-correction supplement

## State

Proposal-stage correction only. This supplement creates no canonical, Product or implementation authority.

## Responsibility reconciliation matrix

| Current owner | Proposed composition use | Overlap to control | Prohibited ownership | Amendment need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operational Operating Model | Sole governed organizational representation projected through the Living Organizational Model workspace | Organization structure, roles, processes, systems, measures, flows | No parallel enterprise graph/model or workspace-owned truth | Clarification only if Product name is adopted |
| Operational System | Bounded real-world organizational object/reference inside the Operating Model | System boundary versus organization-wide workspace | Cannot become organization-wide master model | None |
| Operational System Map | Source-governed map projection usable as one Organization Map layer | Topology/relationship visualization | Cannot imply causality, truth or universal graph semantics | Interaction reconciliation |
| Matter | Persistent envelope for a bounded operational concern | Persistence across Investigation/Improvement | Cannot become the organization identity or complete model | None |
| Investigation | Sibling inquiry mode opened with model context | Unknown/symptom understanding | Context is not material/Evidence; no topology ownership | Entry/transition clarification |
| Operational Impact Review | Assesses affected operational scope/consequences | Improvement risk and affected-elements analysis | Does not own Improvement intent, proposal or Decision | Product boundary required |
| Decision Laboratory | Non-authoritative alternative projection | Improvement option comparison | Cannot decide, authorize Change or overwrite history | Product boundary required |
| Adaptation/change planning | Human-approved adaptation and execution preparation | Proposed Change/implementation plan | Improvement cannot absorb source-owned execution authority | Product boundary required |
| Operational Memory | Historical chain and retrieval | Feedback into later model review | Not primary navigation, current truth or automatic mutation | No semantic expansion |
| P1–P6 | Existing responsibility views | Cross-view composition | No seventh layer | None |

Improvement owns deliberate-change opportunity framing and continuity from intent through monitored stabilization. It consumes Impact Review and Laboratory projections and hands off to Decision/change owners; it does not duplicate them.

## Department Workbench completion

Optional, source-classified and privacy-governed projections may include strengths, development areas, satisfaction, engagement, workload/capacity, employment and role history, people/department relationships, recurring reports, meetings, knowledge contributors, involved Investigations/Improvements, discussed systems/processes/SOPs/interactions, unresolved questions and conflicting process accounts.

None is a required completeness field. Subjective observations remain labeled observations with author, purpose, time and limitation; they never become established facts. Satisfaction, engagement and development data are excluded from any first slice unless Serhii explicitly approves their purpose, visibility, retention and correction policy.

## Privacy Decision gate

Before canonical Product adoption or implementation readiness, accountable humans must decide:

- permitted data classes and whether sensitive people observations are stored at all;
- who may see person identity, contribution content and Investigation/Improvement participation in Map, Department, Person and inquiry contexts;
- aggregation, suppression and redaction rules for broad views;
- protected/sensitive data handling and lawful/organizational purpose;
- retention, deletion where permissible, correction, dispute and historical audit;
- secondary-use and purpose-change prohibition/approval;
- export, analytics, logs, support tooling and backup leakage controls;
- whether person contributions survive identity deactivation and how access changes;
- incident response and accountable privacy owner.

Until that policy exists, privacy-sensitive fields and person-level participation projections are not Interaction- or Engineering-ready.

## Temporal and identity integrity contract

`InformationContribution` must distinguish:

- observation/effective time of the information;
- communication/provision time;
- Management OS recording time;
- role effective interval;
- department-membership effective interval;
- source snapshot version;
- later correction/dispute time and recorder;
- current versus historical identity resolution.

Historical capacity is immutable as a version, not uncorrectable. A correction appends a superseding version with attributable reason while preserving the original. Person merges, deactivation, deletion restrictions, inaccessible profiles, disputed identity and role corrections preserve the contribution relationship and apply policy-governed display/redaction; current profile changes never rewrite contribution-time context.

## First-slice boundary

The smallest plausible future slice is organization identity + one department + known/unknown state + one non-sensitive operational relationship, using local representative data only. It excludes Person-as-Source, sensitive people observations, Improvement execution, integrations, AI and production persistence until their separate contracts and authority exist.
