# 3. Selection Posture

A future accountable human should consider the following dimensions
qualitatively and non-compensatorily:

- source authority;
- chronology reconstructability;
- Decision visibility;
- Decision-rationale visibility;
- Change visibility;
- Outcome observability;
- Replay integrity;
- manageable scope;
- uncertainty preservation;
- risk of hindsight distortion;
- usefulness of negative or indeterminate Learning; and
- source durability and reuse constraints.

A strength in one dimension cannot offset a material weakness in another.
Descriptions such as `well-supported`, `partial`, `fragmented`, `disputed`,
`unknown`, and `requires tighter episode boundary` do not combine into a result.
This package uses no numeric score, weight, total, ordering, or ranking.

The accountable human must assess the exact bounded episode, not the fame,
severity, institutional importance, or volume of material associated with the
broader case.

## Candidate 1

### Candidate identity

**Knight Capital: dormant code activation and uncontrolled market orders**

### Domain and operational category

- **Domain:** electronic securities trading.
- **Operational category:** Operational execution; Engineering; Incident
  Response; Organizational Learning.

### Proposed bounded episode

Preparation and deployment of Retail Liquidity Program code in July 2012,
pre-open system messages on 1 August, the erroneous-order incident and immediate
containment, ending with the first bounded control response. Later merger history
and long-horizon corporate performance remain outside this episode.

### Strongest public-source support

The SEC administrative order provides a compact chronology of deployment,
control failures, generated messages, trading activity, response, loss, and
mandated remediation. Contemporaneous Knight/KCG SEC filings provide attributable
organizational reporting of the incident and financial consequence.

### Main source limitations

The SEC record is regulator-framed and settlement-shaped. Complete source code,
deployment scripts, logs, incident communications, consultant work product, and
individual reasoning are not public. Later control-effectiveness Evidence is
partial.

### Decision visibility

Deployment and response actions are visible, but a singular accountable Decision
owner and full Decision rationale are not completely exposed.

### Change visibility

Regulatory undertakings, independent review, and stated control investment are
visible. Actual implementation and fidelity are materially incomplete.

### Outcome visibility

Erroneous orders, unwanted positions, immediate market effects, financial loss,
and regulatory sanction are publicly observable. Longer-term remediation Outcome
is less visible.

### Authority complexity

Technology, trading operations, risk, compliance, incident response, executives,
and the regulator hold distinct responsibilities. They must not be collapsed into
one owner.

### Replay plausibility

A short staged sequence is plausible if pre-open messages and market-open behavior
can be presented without later findings. Replay integrity depends on identifying
one Decision moment and preserving unknown internal rationale.

### Likely H3 Learning opportunity

Structured re-engagement might expose missed signal semantics, control placement,
deployment authority, containment assumptions, and differences between a generated
message and an operational alert. This is a possible Learning opportunity, not a
performed Replay or H3 result.

### Risk of hindsight or narrative simplification

The large known loss can make weakly classified pre-open messages appear obviously
decisive. A “missed server” or single-operator story would erase systemic controls,
authority, and response conditions.

### Unresolved preparation questions

- Which exact deployment or containment Decision would anchor the episode?
- Which messages and controls were visible to which accountable roles and when?
- Can the source record support Decision rationale without inference?
- What remediation Evidence can establish Actual Change rather than obligation?

### Candidate-specific pause conditions

Pause if no attributable Decision moment can be bounded; raw chronology cannot be
verified; the walkthrough would require invented incident rationale; or longer-term
Change fidelity is required but remains unavailable. A pause is not permanent
rejection.

### What selection would authorize

Selection could authorize recording Candidate 1 and this bounded episode, then
preparing a source manifest, chronology and Evidence-gap assessment, and a separate
walkthrough-readiness consideration.

### What selection would not authorize

Selection would not authorize source ingestion beyond ordinary public research,
walkthrough creation, Replay, historical-truth claims, invention of rationale,
validation, H3 confirmation, operational Change, architecture, implementation, or
repository transition.

# 6. H3-Specific Selection Considerations

A first published walkthrough should plausibly support:

- separation of what was known then from what became known later;
- reconstruction of historical reasoning without inventing rationale;
- separate historical and Replay branches;
- meaningful alternative reasoning grounded only in information available at the
  declared moment;
- preservation of missingness, dispute, and uncertainty; and
- possible Additional Learning beyond a standard case summary.

Additional Learning may be negative or indeterminate. A walkthrough could reveal
that public material cannot support a different interpretation, that a familiar
signal was not actionable at the time, or that attribution remains unresolved.
Such an outcome would not make the exercise unsuccessful by definition.

```text
Famous case ≠ suitable first walkthrough
Large source corpus ≠ coherent Evidence
Clear failure ≠ meaningful H3 Replay
New insight ≠ historical truth
```

Case severity, narrative force, and ease of teaching are not substitutes for
Evidence provenance, Decision visibility, branch integrity, or manageable scope.

# 7. Candidate-Specific Pause Conditions

Each card records candidate-specific pause conditions. Across all candidates,
selection consideration should pause when:

- the bounded episode remains unclear;
- a critical primary source is inaccessible;
- the Decision identity cannot be reconstructed;
- public material shows Outcome but not reasoning;
- the case is too broad for one walkthrough;
- source accounts materially conflict and cannot remain attributable;
- Replay would rely mainly on hindsight;
- internal rationale would need to be invented; or
- copyright or source durability prevents attributable reconstruction.

Pause conditions are preparation findings, not permanent rejection. They do not
remove a candidate from the fixed pool or create a new disposition automatically.

# 12. Advisory Human Questions

The accountable human may consider:

- Which candidate offers the clearest bounded episode?
- Which case preserves the clearest distinction between knowledge then and
  knowledge later?
- Which case permits meaningful Replay without inventing missing human rationale?
- Which candidate can produce useful Learning even if the result is indeterminate?
- Which case is least likely to collapse into a familiar moral narrative?
- Which case is small enough for one walkthrough but rich enough for H3?
- What source limitation would most likely invalidate the choice?
- What additional Evidence would change the selection?

These are descriptive prompts. They are not governance gates, scores, acceptance
criteria, mandatory answers, or authority to select.
