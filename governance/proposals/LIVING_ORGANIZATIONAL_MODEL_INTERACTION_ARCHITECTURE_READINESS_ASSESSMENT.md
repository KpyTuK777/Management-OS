## Authority baseline

The proposal is bounded by the adopted Living Organizational Model, Improvement, and People & Privacy Product Decisions. It preserves:

- Operational Operating Model as sole governed organizational representation;
- Investigation’s existing internal Workbench and semantics;
- Improvement as a distinct deliberate-change mode;
- Matter, Decision, Evidence, Working Set, source-record, Monitoring, Stabilization, and Operational Memory ownership;
- InformationContribution distinctions and historical capacity;
- People & Privacy minimization, protected absence, anti-dossier, anti-surveillance, anti-scoring, and non-judgmental Watson constraints;
- P1–P6 responsibilities and current non-implementation state.

## Existing interaction support reused

The proposal reuses established interaction contracts from Perceptual Architecture, Operational Workbench, Operational Situation Board, Guided Investigation, Human-first Investigation, Design System, Natural Capture, and Mobile Experience:

- one dominant action and supporting context hierarchy;
- primary workspace ownership of the useful viewport;
- stable context during focused inspection and focus restoration on close;
- progressive construction rather than empty future placeholders;
- explicit selection distinct from navigation;
- human-first labels with technical metadata in Inspector;
- non-color state, visible focus, usable targets, reflow, and reduced-motion obligations;
- mobile role preservation without shrinking desktop composition;
- Watson as contextual, non-blocking, and absent when quiet;
- degraded non-AI operation.

## Interaction versus Engineering boundary

The proposal specifies behavior, hierarchy, state meaning, disclosure, navigation, focus, responsive capability allocation, and human-facing consequences. It deliberately leaves these to future Engineering Architecture:

- entity/state schemas and identity resolution;
- persistence, versioning, synchronization, caching, and conflict algorithms;
- relationship/graph/query technology;
- authorization enforcement and policy evaluation;
- search indexing and ranking;
- audit, encryption, redaction, retention/deletion propagation, logs, exports, and backups;
- cross-device state and responsive implementation;
- AI runtime, retrieval, model, prompt, tool, and evaluation design.

## Risks requiring independent challenge

1. Whether one-layer-at-a-time Map behavior could conceal cross-layer dependencies needed for safe judgment.
2. Whether “arrange hierarchy” and view arrangement are sufficiently distinguishable to prevent accidental organizational mutation.
3. Whether mobile read-only degradation preserves correction/dispute access for people-related data.
4. Whether contextual Investigation/Improvement entry previews expose restricted person or work-context existence.
5. Whether Person-as-Source historical-role defaulting is safe when communication time is approximate.
6. Whether short empty-state labels can distinguish unknown, absent, not applicable, restricted, stale, and disputed without excessive explanation.
7. Whether Watson’s contextual placement is discoverable without becoming persistent chat chrome.
8. Whether History/Memory comparison can avoid presenting historical topology as current or comparable without qualification.
