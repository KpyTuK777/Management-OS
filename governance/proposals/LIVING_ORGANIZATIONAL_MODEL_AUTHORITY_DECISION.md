## Attributable human Decision

I adopt the Living Organizational Model as the persistent human-facing Product workspace through which an operational manager progressively understands, navigates, observes, and works with the organization.

The Living Organizational Model does not create a second canonical organizational model. The existing Operational Operating Model retains sole governed representational authority for the bounded organization. The Living Organizational Model is a Product composition and projection over that canonical model.

It may provide:

- Organization Map;
- Department Workbenches;
- progressive organization construction;
- process, system, SOP, KPI and interaction views;
- organizational navigation;
- incomplete and progressively refined organizational understanding;
- contextual entry into Investigation;
- contextual entry into future Improvement;
- Operational Memory projections;
- reviewed organizational-model updates after change and stabilization.

Investigation remains internally unchanged. Historical Walkthrough Reader remains a supporting module.

## Human rationale

The repository evidence supports the Living Organizational Model as the missing persistent Product workspace above Investigation without requiring a duplicate canonical enterprise model.

The operational manager needs a continuously evolving representation of the organization as the context for daily work, Investigation, future Improvement, Decision, Change, Monitoring, Stabilization and Operational Memory.

Using the existing Operational Operating Model as the governed semantic authority preserves architectural coherence while allowing the product to expose that model through a human-readable, progressively constructed operational workspace.

This structure also prevents each Investigation from recreating organizational context from scratch and provides the future context required for Watson to reason across departments, people, processes, systems, SOPs, interactions and historical organizational knowledge.
