- how the Working Set is formed, retained, inspected, and distinguished from Evidence;
- how Missing Evidence, hindsight, attribution, and authority boundaries remain legible;
- how attributable response and explicit deferral are presented; and

These are not deficiencies in the authority-consideration package. They must not
be silently converted into screen design or technical decisions during this
assessment.

- Engineering Architecture; and
- prototype, coding, testing, deployment, and delivery choices.

Their deferral is required boundary preservation, not evidence that Product
Realization authority consideration is blocked.
