# 2. Readiness Method

The assessment applies the eleven required qualitative, non-compensatory checks.
A check passes only when structural preparation preserves its scope and all
applicable Evidence, provenance, Decision, Change, Outcome, attribution, Learning,
and authority boundaries.

The only possible readiness dispositions are:

- **Ready for authority consideration:** all required preparation inputs are
  present, structurally complete, neutral, and bounded enough to receive direct
  human-owned inputs; or
- **Not ready due to missing preparation inputs:** at least one required
  structural input is absent or materially deficient.

Readiness does not mean a candidate should be selected. Intentionally empty
human-owned fields are expected at this stage and are not structural deficiencies.
They prevent the shell from becoming a Decision until completed directly by an
accountable human.
