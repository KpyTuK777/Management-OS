## Attributable human Decision

I adopt Improvement / Вдосконалення as a distinct Product mode for deliberate, optional organizational change when sufficient understanding already exists.

Improvement is a sibling to Investigation.

Investigation remains:

> I need to understand what is happening and why.

Improvement becomes:

> I understand enough to consider a deliberate change.

Improvement may be used when the current system is functioning acceptably and the manager wants to:

- automate a process;
- simplify an interaction;
- improve quality or resilience;
- prepare for scaling;
- redesign an SOP;
- redistribute responsibility;
- replace or introduce an operational system;
- reduce unnecessary manual work;
- deliberately evolve the organization.

Improvement does not imply:

- failure;
- incident;
- defect;
- Root Cause;
- mandatory correction;
- proven benefit;
- approval;
- successful outcome.

The current state may be healthy.

Improvement and Investigation remain separate but may create explicit linked work in either direction. No silent conversion is permitted.

The adopted lifecycle preserves:

```text
Human Decision
  -> Impact Review
  -> source-owned planning and execution
  -> Monitoring
  -> Stabilization
  -> Operational Memory
  -> reviewed Operating Model update
  -> continued operational control
```

## Human rationale

A living operational system must support both reactive and proactive change.

Investigation is appropriate when understanding is insufficient and the manager needs to determine what is happening and why.

It is not appropriate to force every deliberate organizational change through an Investigation when no negative symptom or unexplained failure exists.

Improvement provides the missing proactive path while preserving existing Decision, implementation, Monitoring, Stabilization, Operational Memory, and Operating Model authority boundaries.

## Adopted Product boundary

The adoption is bounded by the fixed Product definition, correction supplement, transition contracts, authority boundaries, and explicit non-effects established by the reviewed consideration package.

The adopted Product sequence is progressive and revisable, not a mandatory linear wizard. Change remains optional. Inherited Living Organizational Model context does not automatically become Evidence or proof of benefit. Improvement does not absorb Investigation, Matter identity, Decision authority, Impact Review, source-owned planning or execution, Outcome, Stabilization, Operational Memory, or Operating Model authority.

The accountable human retains Decision authority. Source-owned implementation responsibilities remain source-owned. Monitoring does not establish causal proof. Completed Improvement history remains inspectable and may inform only a reviewed Operating Model update through the existing governed authority path.
