# 1. Protocol Identity and Purpose

```text
Knight Capital Published Historical Walkthrough 01
Shape B — Bounded operational-control walkthrough
```

This protocol defines a bounded, source-disciplined method for a possible future
walkthrough of the Knight Capital case in support of inquiry into **H3 — Experience
Amplification**. It prevents the walkthrough from inventing a managerial Decision
narrative, actor-specific knowledge, historical rationale, causal certainty, or
organizational Learning.

The protocol describes how a future walkthrough would be conducted if separately
authorized. It does not authorize or execute any walkthrough phase.

# 3. Permitted Operational Sequence

A future authorized walkthrough may traverse only this bounded descriptive
sequence:

1. preparation context;
2. deployment context;
3. publicly reconstructable production state;
4. operational-control visibility;
5. incident emergence;
6. observable alerts and detection signals;
7. attributable response actions;
8. containment attempts and uncertainty;
9. immediate observable Outcome; and
10. later attributable official findings.

This ordering is an inquiry sequence, not a causal chain, canonical lifecycle, or
proof that all relevant events are known. A later source may describe an earlier
event without becoming information available to historical actors.

# 4. Walkthrough Phases

## Phase 1 — Source-bound orientation

Fix the source identity, owner, authority class, publication date, bounded episode,
and known limitations before substantive traversal. No source is silently added or
treated as complete.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** source-boundary statement and fixed-source
index.

**Must remain unresolved:** source completeness and complete historical truth.

## Phase 2 — Pre-incident operational context

Present only attributable context needed to understand RLP preparation, SMARS, and
the operational-control setting. Later policy material remains later context.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** qualified context observations.

**Must remain unresolved:** unsupported organizational pressure, culture, actor
intent, and common knowledge.

## Phase 3 — Deployment reconstruction

Traverse preparation and staged deployment claims using their exact source and
retrospective status. Separate intended, prepared, and deployed Change.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** source-attributed deployment observations
and explicit missing deployment Evidence.

**Must remain unresolved:** technician identity, complete commands, verification,
approval, and rationale unless directly supported.

## Phase 4 — Production-state reconstruction

Describe only the production state publicly reconstructable from fixed sources.
Separate deployment statement from verified production configuration and activated
behavior.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** qualified later official findings about
server and system state.

**Must remain unresolved:** raw code, binary identity, configuration, runtime trace,
and independent forensic verification.

## Phase 5 — Incident emergence

Present source-supported order, execution, position, and duration observations
without treating sequence as full causality.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** attributable event and aggregate trading
observations.

**Must remain unresolved:** complete order-level chronology and unsupported causal
allocation.

## Phase 6 — Detection and visibility

Separate signal generation, delivery, viewing, recognition, interpretation,
escalation, and shared awareness. Do not call the BNET messages recognized alerts
where the source does not support recognition.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** observable signal and monitoring claims.

**Must remain unresolved:** who saw, understood, or could act on specific
information.

## Phase 7 — Response and containment

Present attributable response actions without converting them into one Decision or
complete response narrative. Separate response action, intended effect, Actual
Change, order cessation, position management, and verified containment.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** qualified response-action and containment
observations.

**Must remain unresolved:** Decision owner, rationale, authority, exact action
sequence, and complete containment verification.

## Phase 8 — Immediate Outcome

Present each attributable Outcome dimension and measurement scope separately.
Preserve different loss figures with their source dates and boundaries.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** aggregate trading, financial, market,
operational, organizational, and regulatory observations within the bounded period.

**Must remain unresolved:** complete multidimensional Outcome, participant-level
effects, and unsupported causal attribution.

## Phase 9 — Later official findings

Present later regulatory findings as attributed later findings. Do not insert them
into the historical actor information state or treat them as complete truth.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** explicitly attributed official findings,
sanctions, and undertakings.

**Must remain unresolved:** complete investigative basis, every actor's knowledge,
and complete historical truth.

## Phase 10 — Evidence-gap review

Review missing Evidence already established by the fixed Evidence-gap assessment.
Do not repair it with plausible events, assumptions, or synthesis.

**Permitted output if later authorized:** gap ledger and unresolved-claim statement.

**Must remain unresolved:** every gap not resolved by fixed attributable material.

## Phase 11 — Experience Amplification reflection preparation

Prepare human reflection prompts concerning additional insight, uncertainty
recognition, distinctions among Decision, Change, and Outcome, and identification of
missing knowledge.

This phase does not itself conduct reflection, validate H3, establish Product Value,
or claim organizational Learning. Original Learning remains materially absent.

# 5. Required Separation

Every future phase output must keep the following semantically separate:

| Material class | Required boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Historical source record | What a fixed source states, with owner and locator |
| Chronology | Supported temporal placement without automatic causality |
| Attributed official finding | Finding owned by its named authority and publication date |
| Analyst synthesis | Explicitly labelled combination that adds no historical fact |
| Uncertainty | Bounded proposition not established with current material |
| Missing Evidence | Identified absent material, not evidence of non-occurrence |
| Hindsight observation | Later information unavailable or not shown available then |
| Future Replay branch | Separately authorized simulation, never historical record |
| Human reflection | Attributable later reflection, not original organizational Learning |
| H3 validation | Separate authority, method, and result not created here |

No output may silently move from one class to another.

# 7. Phase Entry and Exit Discipline

Before entering a phase, a future authorized operator must have:

- fixed claim identifiers;
- fixed source identities and locators;
- applicable state labels;
- relevant chronology and Evidence-gap references; and
- explicit unresolved fields.

A phase may exit only with:

- source-attributed observations separated from synthesis;
- missing Evidence still visible;
- hindsight status recorded;
- prohibited inferences absent; and
- no unauthorized Replay, validation, or implementation material.

Failure to satisfy these conditions does not license completion by assumption. The
phase remains unresolved or stops.

# 8. Stop Conditions

The future walkthrough must stop at the affected claim or keep it unresolved where
continuing would require:

- invention of a Decision owner;
- invention of human rationale;
- invention of actor-specific knowledge;
- assumed recognition or interpretation of alerts;
- assumed authority or ability to act;
- causal certainty unsupported by sources;
- confident counterfactual Outcome;
- reconstruction of original organizational Learning;
- conversion of remediation statements into verified Learning; or
- treatment of regulatory findings as complete historical truth.

A local stop need not end every future phase, but the unresolved claim cannot be
carried forward as fact, premise, actor knowledge, or Replay input.
