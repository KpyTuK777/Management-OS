# 1. Purpose and Scope

This artifact defines historical inferences prohibited in any future Knight Capital
Published Historical Walkthrough 01 under **Shape B — Bounded operational-control
walkthrough**. It preserves the fixed gaps in Decision, actor information, Change
fidelity, Outcome, causality, and organizational Learning.

A prohibition prevents a claim from entering the historical branch. It does not
prove the opposite proposition, resolve the missing Evidence, or create a canonical
governance rule.

# 3. Decision-Narrative Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. an invented single managerial narrative;
2. an invented accountable Decision owner;
3. an invented operational rationale;
4. a Decision inferred solely from an observed action, omission, role, title, or
   sequence;
5. alternatives presented as historically considered without direct support;
6. one coherent organizational rationale assembled from different actors or later
   sources; or
7. a response action presented as a complete attributable Decision.

Where action is visible but Decision identity or rationale is absent, the action may
remain an Observation and the Decision fields remain unknown.

# 4. Actor-Knowledge and Alert Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. actor-specific knowledge inferred without direct support;
2. assumed alert awareness or recognition;
3. assumed interpretation of an alert;
4. assumed ability to act;
5. assumed authority to stop or reverse activity;
6. shared organizational awareness inferred from system generation, delivery,
   position display, or group distribution;
7. later regulatory detail assigned to a historical actor's information state; or
8. “should have known” language used as a substitute for Evidence of what was
   available, perceived, and understood then.

Signal generation, delivery, viewing, recognition, interpretation, escalation,
authority, and action remain separate propositions.

# 5. Causality and Outcome Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. an assumed causal chain based only on temporal order;
2. conversion of correlation into causation;
3. Root Cause or sole-cause language unsupported by the fixed source boundary;
4. causal certainty stronger than the attributed source wording;
5. a confident counterfactual Outcome;
6. a claim that one alternative action would certainly have prevented the Outcome;
7. a simulated consequence presented as probable historical reality;
8. one aggregate loss measure presented as the complete Outcome; or
9. later organizational consequences attributed wholly to the incident without
   bounded support.

Exact authoritative causal wording may be quoted or paraphrased with attribution
and qualification; it may not be generalized into independent causal proof.

# 6. Hindsight Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. later findings treated as knowledge available to historical actors;
2. later publication time collapsed into event time;
3. an official retrospective reconstruction represented as a contemporaneous
   shared view;
4. later-known consequences used to infer historical foresight;
5. present-day operational practice presented as the applicable historical norm
   without fixed support; or
6. retrospective clarity used to erase uncertainty during deployment or response.

Later material may appear only with its source date, attribution, historical-
knowledge status, and `HINDSIGHT-ONLY` or equivalent boundary where applicable.

# 7. Official-Finding and Source Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. regulatory findings treated as complete historical truth;
2. a settled administrative finding presented without its procedural boundary;
3. company disclosure silently converted into regulator-owned Evidence;
4. a regulatory press release treated as independent corroboration of its order;
5. a dynamic disclosure index treated as a separate incident investigation;
6. a secondary source silently substituted for stronger primary or official
   material;
7. disagreement reconciled by unstated source preference; or
8. source silence treated as proof that an event did not occur.

# 8. Change and Remediation Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. intended Change treated as prepared Change;
2. prepared Change treated as deployed Change;
3. a deployment statement treated as verified production state;
4. deployed Change treated as activated behavior without source support;
5. a response statement treated as verified containment;
6. software removal, order cessation, trade-out, financing, and remediation
   collapsed into one containment state;
7. remediation treated as proof of prior Root Cause;
8. remediation statements treated as verified organizational Learning;
9. an undertaking treated as completed implementation; or
10. an announced or required Change treated as sustained adoption or effectiveness.

# 9. Human Characterization Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. retrospective moral characterization not grounded in sources;
2. attribution of negligence, competence, incompetence, intent, awareness,
   motivation, recklessness, panic, or confidence without sufficient Evidence;
3. responsibility assigned merely from job title, access, seniority, signature, or
   participation;
4. multiple operational actors collapsed into one fictional Decision-maker; or
5. institutional action restated as the private belief of a named human.

This boundary does not erase attributable legal or regulatory wording. Such wording
must remain owned by its source and not be independently amplified.

# 10. Chronology and Synthesis Prohibitions

The historical branch must not contain:

1. chronology gaps filled with plausible but unsupported events;
2. unknown timestamps converted into exact times;
3. arithmetic time inference presented as an observed timestamp;
4. uncertain ordering silently resolved;
5. analyst synthesis presented as historical fact;
6. synthesis that adds unknown facts or strengthens source certainty;
7. a seamless omniscient narrative assembled across publication times; or
8. missing Evidence hidden because the narrative remains readable without it.

# 11. Replay and Validation Prohibitions

The historical branch and any future Replay branch must not contain:

1. future Replay output presented as historical reconstruction;
2. an alternative Decision presented as the historical Decision;
3. declared Replay assumptions converted into Evidence;
4. a simulated action used as proof of what historical actors could or should have
   done;
5. Replay output used as proof of Root Cause or causal certainty;
6. case observations used to validate H3 before separate validation authority
   exists;
7. walkthrough insight labelled as confirmed Product Value; or
8. negative or indeterminate reflection treated as formal H3 validation.

Replay is not currently authorized.

# 12. Required Boundary Rules

```text
Plausibility ≠ Evidence

Temporal sequence ≠ Causal proof

Later knowledge ≠ Historical actor knowledge

Remediation ≠ Verified Learning

Walkthrough insight ≠ H3 validation
```

Additionally:

```text
Observed action ≠ attributable Decision
Attributable Decision ≠ known rationale
Signal generation ≠ alert recognition
Response activity ≠ verified containment
Alternative Outcome ≠ historical probability
```

# 13. Handling a Prohibited Inference

If a future candidate claim depends on a prohibited inference, it must be:

1. removed from the historical branch;
2. recorded as `MISSING-EVIDENCE`, `UNCERTAIN`, `HINDSIGHT-ONLY`, or `EXCLUDED` as
   appropriate;
3. linked to the relevant Evidence-gap reference;
4. prevented from becoming a premise for later historical claims; and
5. prevented from entering a Replay branch unless restated as a declared assumption
   under separate Replay authorization.

Plausibility, narrative usefulness, managerial relevance, or Replay convenience do
not override the prohibition.

# 14. Explicit Non-Effects

This artifact does not:

- decide the truth or falsity of a prohibited proposition;
- create substantive historical claims;
- execute or authorize a walkthrough or Replay;
- establish Root Cause, causal proof, negligence, intent, or Learning;
- validate H3;
- create architecture, canonical objects, Engineering Architecture, metrics, KPIs,
  experiments, or implementation authority; or
- alter ROADMAP or BACKLOG.

The prohibitions remain `PREPARED FOR CONSIDERATION` with authority effect `NONE`.
