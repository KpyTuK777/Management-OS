## Decision object

The accountable human is deciding whether to adopt this bounded Product policy:

> Management OS may represent people only for a declared, necessary operational purpose, through source-governed, classed, effective-time, purpose-filtered, correctable relationships that preserve human dignity, historical integrity, least disclosure, and explicit human authority.

Adoption would establish what people information may mean in Product. It would not authorize collecting, storing, displaying, integrating, analyzing, or sending any people information.

## Information classes under Option A

1. **Organizational facts** — authoritative, effective-dated identity/role/department/responsibility/authority facts; never proof of behavior or performance.
2. **Attributable statements** — a person’s contextual words or faithful paraphrase; not automatic fact, Evidence, approval, or responsibility.
3. **Manager observations** — exceptional, attributable, purpose-bound, time-bound, restricted and disputable observations; not objective traits or employment judgments.
4. **System-derived records** — permitted source records with identity, meaning, time, limits, authorization, and correction; surveillance-grade telemetry and behavioral inference prohibited.
5. **Investigation material and Evidence relationships** — retained under Case Evidence Set authority; Person-as-Source never grants Evidence status.
6. **Sensitive people information** — excluded, separately gated, or prohibited by class; no speculative collection.

Every field must pass purpose, necessity, sensitivity, attribution, visibility, retention, correction, and secondary-use tests. Failure means exclusion.

## Person-as-Source under Option A

An **InformationContribution** would be adopted as Product meaning for the typed relationship that an identified person supplied specific information/material in a supported context at a particular contribution-time role and department.

It preserves person, contribution-time role/department, content reference, communication time, recording time and recorder, purpose/context, direct/indirect/unknown basis, uncertainty/limits, visibility, and later support/contradiction/correction/supersession/dispute/withdrawal/redaction/deletion status.

It remains distinct from authorship, ownership, responsibility, subject, recorder, approval, Decision, Evidence, and credibility. Zero, one, or multiple subjects must be explicit; none is inferred from source identity or free text.

Adoption does not decide whether InformationContribution needs a new canonical named relationship. A future canonical clarification must first test whether existing typed relationships are sufficient. It must not create a second Person or organizational model.

## Historical integrity under Option A

Current organization changes cannot rewrite contribution-time capacity. Role, department, information-effective, communication, recording, source-snapshot, correction, dispute, supersession, withdrawal, redaction, and deletion times remain distinct.

Corrections can update ordinary truth while a narrowly justified restricted audit version preserves accountability. Historical integrity is not blanket permission for indefinite harmful retention.

## Workbench boundaries under Option A

### Person / Role Workbench

A purpose-filtered operational surface may project minimal identity, role/department, responsibility/authority, relevant process/system/SOP/interaction relationships, effective-dated history, authorized contributions, and narrow work participation.

It must not become a universal dossier or expose sensitive data, broad manager notes, subjective fields, unrelated work participation, communications/activity timelines, inferred networks or traits, scores, ranks, ratios, or expired data without a current purpose.

### Department Workbench

It may show role/department operating context and named people only where necessary. Restricted Person content, protected-source identity, sensitive/subjective data, disputes, unrelated work, and restricted relationship existence do not roll up. Role and minimized aggregate views are preferred.

## Access, correction, retention, and anti-surveillance obligations under Option A

- declared purpose and least disclosure precede access;
- source authorization follows every Person, Department, Search, Memory, export, support, and Watson projection;
- unauthorized existence is not leaked through counts, labels, suggestions, or summaries;
- people may inspect and challenge data about them to the maximum safe extent;
- protected sources require recorded human restriction, minimum safe disclosure, correction/dispute response, review/expiry, and no secret automated adverse use;
- factual, attribution, source, historical, supersession, withdrawal, dispute, redaction, and deletion semantics remain distinct;
- retention is class/purpose-bound and separates ordinary display from narrowly justified audit history;
- correction/restriction/deletion propagates to projections and future AI context;
- secondary HR, employment, analytics, training, benchmarking, surveillance, and external-sharing use requires new explicit authority;
- keystroke/screen/device monitoring, location tracking, private-content mining, activity-based productivity/engagement/performance inference, covert monitoring, and cross-context behavioral profiles are prohibited within this scope.

## Contribution history and Watson under Option A

Contribution history may index source-linked reports, explanations, clarifications, exceptions, corrections, and narrow work participation. Support or contradiction qualifies information in context, never the person.

Credibility, reliability, trust, competence, performance, behavior, influence, employee-value, promotion, or termination scores—including proxy counts, ratios, rankings, centrality, visual scoring, and Watson scoring—are prohibited.

Future separately authorized Watson may surface traceable conflicting accounts, role-level information concentration, missing responsibility, inconsistent formal assignments, or documented person dependency when identity is necessary. It must show sources, class, time, uncertainty, and access limits; it cannot judge or profile people, reveal restricted context, infer sensitive states, or make employment Decisions. Option A authorizes no Watson execution.

## People-free path

Regardless of the People & Privacy choice, a separately authorized people-free Living Model path remains viable using departments, unnamed roles, processes, systems, SOPs, KPIs, interactions, meetings by role/department, reports/artifacts, and known/unknown state.

It excludes named identities, Person-as-Source, Person Workbench, named participation/history, manager observations, subjective/sensitive data, person-level Watson, and latent identity inference. This package does not authorize its implementation.

## Decision criteria

Select Option A only if all of these are intended:

1. named people have legitimate but strictly minimized operational representation;
2. facts, statements, observations, system records, Evidence relations, and sensitive data retain distinct meanings;
3. Person-as-Source is a relationship, not Evidence, credibility, responsibility, or a profile judgment;
4. historical role/department context is preserved without requiring indefinite harmful visibility;
5. Person and Department Workbenches remain purpose-filtered operational surfaces, not dossiers;
6. subjective data is excluded or exceptional and never transformed into fact;
7. inspection, correction, dispute, redaction, deletion, retention, and protected-source review are Product obligations;
8. surveillance, behavioral profiling, scoring, and automated employment judgment are prohibited;
9. Watson remains minimized, source-linked, uncertain, non-judgmental, and separately authorized;
10. a useful people-free path remains independently possible;
11. no new canonical aggregate, P1–P6 view, Interaction, Engineering, implementation, data collection, or roadmap authority is created.
