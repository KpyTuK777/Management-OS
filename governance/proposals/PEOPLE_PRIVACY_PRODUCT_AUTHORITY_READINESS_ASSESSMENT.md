## Risks for independent review

The review should particularly challenge:

1. whether “manager observation” is bounded enough to prevent a shadow personnel file;
2. whether the contribution subject should be modeled without exposing third-party sensitive information;
3. whether correction/deletion rules distinguish historical accountability from indefinite harmful retention;
4. whether participation and contribution links can leak restricted Matter existence through broad views;
5. whether “knowledge holder,” skills, experience, workload, or capacity can become proxy ranking;
6. whether protected-source identity and employee inspection rights require a sharper Product-level conflict rule;
7. whether Watson’s allowed “repeated dependency” and “knowledge concentration” can become person-risk inference;
8. whether the people-free path truly excludes latent identity from metadata and retrieval.

## Authority effect

This assessment only opens independent critical review. It does not adopt People & Privacy policy, authorize a canonical relationship, approve sensitive data, authorize Person-as-Source, define technical access, implement workbenches, enable Watson, modify canonical/ROADMAP/BACKLOG/active implementation files, or authorize integrations, deployment, Replay, H3, Root Cause, or causal proof.
