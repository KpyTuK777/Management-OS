## Constitutional and Organizational Validation

- Direction, Creation, Judgment, and Transition own distinct gates.
- No lifecycle state or activity can authorize its own progression.
- Evidence remains distinct from judgment and transition.
- Judgment approves a bounded transition but does not perform it.
- Transition establishes only the state authorized by judgment.
- Semantic ownership remains source-based through every iteration.
- Delegation preserves scope, separation, attribution, and human accountability.
- Correction cannot transfer approval to changed substance without re-judgment.
- Record custody reports state but does not create it.
- Legitimacy takes precedence over progression or convenience.

## Exclusion Validation

- No autonomous execution behavior is defined.
- No AI-agent coordination is defined.
- No automation permission is granted.
- No retry, scheduling, queueing, or orchestration logic is defined.
- No repository or tooling workflow is defined.
- No implementation methodology is selected.
- No M7.5–M7.7 work is activated.
