## Materialized Experience

- a stable dark Management OS frame preserves case and authority context;
- a warm editorial orientation establishes purpose and bounded case identity;
- Current Situation provides the dominant phase focus and adjacent navigation;
- the eleven-phase sequence is compact, keyboard-operable, and text-labelled;
- the first claim in each phase receives deliberate initial focus;
- claim excerpts support scanning while full fixed wording remains in inspection;
- provenance and public-source actions precede long claim text;
- uncertainty remains adjacent and complete traceability is deliberately disclosed;
- Working Set is persistent in the workbench composition, visually separate from
  Evidence, and provides explicit add/remove feedback without semantic promotion;
- Reflection is presented as a human-owned destination whose disabled finalization
  is explained as a governance boundary, not a broken feature;
- desktop, intermediate, and narrow layouts preserve reading order and meaning;
- visible focus, native controls, semantic labels, non-color state text, minimum
  targets, and reduced-motion handling establish an accessible baseline.

upstream contract.
