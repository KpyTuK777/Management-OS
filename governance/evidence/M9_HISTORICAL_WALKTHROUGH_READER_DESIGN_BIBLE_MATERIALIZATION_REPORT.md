## Design Bible realization

- **Operational office:** the global frame is now a quiet cabinet around the
  active case rather than an unrelated application sidebar.
- **Thinking Table:** the walkthrough workspace is one continuous dark-wood
  field with a dominant warm-paper historical record.
- **Material honesty:** dark structural surfaces frame work; warm paper is
  reserved for the immutable claim under inspection; brass identifies
  orientation and controls.
- **Editorial hierarchy:** the active case and current phase lead; navigation,
  repository status, Working Set, and authority boundaries recede until needed.
- **Visual gravity:** the evidence record is the strongest working object;
  claims and the Working Set support it with deliberately unequal weight.
- **Calm atmosphere:** spacing, localized warm light, restrained borders, and
  three elevation roles replace uniform card emphasis.
- **One visual language:** sidebar, navigation, buttons, status strips, phase
  controls, lists, evidence surfaces, and reflection boundaries share one
  material and typographic system.

## Sidebar redesign

The sidebar now contains a product identity, named operational spaces, a clear
active state, and a case docket. The irrelevant mode selector was removed from
the Historical Reader presentation. On compact widths the cabinet becomes a
quiet horizontal dock without changing navigation meaning.

## Ukrainian terminology

The remaining interface use of `випадок` was replaced by `кейс`, including
`Обмежений операційний кейс`, `Картка кейсу`, and the `Кейс` docket label.

English is intentionally preserved only for:

- `Management OS` and `Knight Capital` as proper identities;
- `Shape B` as the fixed inquiry-shape identity;
- `Root Cause` and `Learning` where the Ukrainian explanation remains adjacent;
- immutable claim text, source identities, source titles, IDs, commits, blobs,
  and artifact paths.

Terms to refine through later Product Owner use rather than a premature
canonical terminology guide are `кейс`, `Shape B`, `Root Cause`, and `Learning`.
