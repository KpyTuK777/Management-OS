## Canonical Dependency Validation

- M7.1 artifact classes remain separate: the definition is normative, this
  record is evidence, and neither records a verdict.
- M7.2 accountable human authority, bounded authority, separation,
  non-self-approval, evidence integrity, traceability, and implementation
  independence remain controlling.
- M7.3 Direction, Creation, Judgment, Transition, Semantic Stewardship, and
  Governance Record Custody are preserved as the only organizational functions.
- M7.4's eight stages and six gates remain unchanged; protocol conditions cannot
  cross a lifecycle gate or create authority state.
- Autonomous participants inherit constraints through delegation and never
  become authority sources.
- Handoff transfers accepted responsibility only, not implicit authority.
- Review participation cannot collapse creation, evidence, Judgment, and
  Transition.
- Suspension and recovery stop at the last valid authority condition and cannot
  create permission through urgency or retry.

## Exclusion Validation

- No constitutional authority is changed.
- No organizational function or lifecycle stage is added.
- No repository or deployment mechanics are defined.
- No scheduling, prioritization, retry, queueing, or timeout algorithm is
  selected.
- No infrastructure or runtime orchestration architecture is defined.
- No product, vendor, model, agent, tool, platform, or implementation method is
  selected.
- No governance operations or retrospective conclusions are defined.
- No M7.6–M7.7 work is activated.
