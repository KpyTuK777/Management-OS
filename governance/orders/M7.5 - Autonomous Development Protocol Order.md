## Status


## Ordering Authority

```text
Product Owner
```

## Authority

This repository-native order opens M7.5 — Autonomous Development Protocol as
the single active architecture stage after canonical closure of M7.4.

## Ordered Work

Prepare one M7.5 Autonomous Development Protocol candidate in conformance with
the canonical M7.1 Governance Structure, M7.2 Constitution, M7.3 Organizational
Model, and M7.4 Development Lifecycle.

## Exclusions

This opening order does not:

- define autonomous behavior, agent identity, coordination, handoff,
  supervision, permissions, capability grants, stop conditions, retry,
  scheduling, queueing, orchestration, timeout, or recovery rules;
- grant an automated system independent authority or remove accountable human
  authority;
- alter any M7.4 lifecycle stage, gate, transition, invariant, or authority
  meaning;
- introduce implementation, platform, tooling, repository, or deployment
  mechanisms;
- authorize M7.6–M7.7 work;
- approve or canonicalize the future M7.5 output.

## Completion Boundary

M7.5 remains Active until a separately prepared candidate completes its
required authority lifecycle. This order performs only the opening transition
and supplies no substantive Autonomous Development Protocol definition.
