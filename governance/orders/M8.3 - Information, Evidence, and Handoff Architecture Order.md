# M8.3 — Information, Evidence, and Handoff Architecture Order

## Status

```text
Effective / Scope bounded
```

## Direction Authority

```text
Architect Council
```

## Authority

This repository-native order opens M8.3 — Information, Evidence, and Handoff
Architecture as the sole active M8 stage after the separate canonicalization
and closure of M8.2 Capability and Ownership Coherence.

It establishes the M8.3 objective and dependency boundary only. It does not
define information movement, evidence movement, authority movement, handoffs,
interaction contracts, or any substantive M8.3 architecture.

## Stage Objective

Evaluate how information, evidence, and handoff responsibilities relate across
Management OS capability boundaries within the canonical M8.1 architectural
identity and canonical M8.2 capability and ownership model while preserving
semantic ownership, source authority, human authority, and external governance.

## Exclusions

This order does not authorize or define:

- an information, evidence, authority, interaction, or handoff architecture;
- a message, payload, event, command, state transition, sequence, failure rule,
  orchestration mechanism, or protocol;
- a runtime, API, data model, schema, persistence, storage, component, service,
  infrastructure, deployment, repository, UI, or implementation topology;
- a lifecycle, governance amendment, source-authority change, ownership
  reassignment, product redesign, delivery plan, release, or staffing decision;
- modification of canonical M8.1 or M8.2 meaning, their review records, source
  authority conditions, or the Product Purpose / System Identity watchpoint;
- Architecture Approval, Canonical Review, canonicalization, M8.3 closure,
  M8.4 opening, or any later-stage or successor transition.

## Completion Boundary

This opening order is satisfied only when one implementation-independent M8.3
candidate is prepared under the active Architecture Backlog task and submitted
to its separately required independent Architecture Review gate. This order
does not approve that future candidate, predetermine its findings, or supply any
part of its substantive architecture.
