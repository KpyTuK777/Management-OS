## Authority

This repository-native order opens M8.2 — Capability and Ownership Coherence as
the sole active M8 stage after the separate canonicalization and closure of
M8.1 Architectural Identity and Baseline Mapping.

It establishes the M8.2 objective and dependency boundary only. It does not
define a capability model, assign or change capability ownership, reach a
coherence conclusion, or supply substantive M8.2 architecture.

## Stage Objective

Evaluate how Management OS capabilities compose within the canonical M8.1
architectural identity, product-layer model, responsibility ownership, and
source-authority baseline.

## Completion Boundary

This opening order is satisfied only when one implementation-independent M8.2
candidate is prepared under the active Architecture Backlog task and submitted
to its separately required independent review gate. This order does not approve
that future candidate or predetermine its conclusions.

The candidate subsequently completed independent Architecture and Canonical
Review. A later, separately authorized repository transition canonicalized and
closed M8.2. This order is therefore scope fulfilled and grants no continuing
M8.2, M8.3, implementation, or successor authority.
