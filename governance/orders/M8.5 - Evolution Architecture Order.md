## Authority

This repository-native order opens M8.5 — Evolution Architecture as the sole
active M8 stage after the separate canonicalization and closure of M8.4 Gap and
Risk Assessment.

It establishes the M8.5 objective and dependency boundary only. It does not
define an evolution increment, target architecture, correction, treatment,
priority, portfolio, sequence, or any substantive M8.5 architecture.

## Stage Objective

Define the bounded architectural basis by which separately authorized future
evolution may respond to assessed architectural conditions while preserving
canonical M8.1–M8.4 meaning, unique semantic ownership, authority-preserving
handoffs, explicit residual exposure, source authority, human authority, and
external governance.

## Completion Boundary

This opening order is satisfied only when one implementation-independent M8.5
candidate is prepared under the active Architecture Backlog task and submitted
to its separately required independent Architecture Review gate. This order
does not approve that future candidate, predetermine its findings, adopt an M8.4
Recommendation, or supply any part of substantive Evolution Architecture.

The candidate subsequently completed independent Architecture and Canonical
Review. A later, separately authorized repository transition canonicalized and
closed M8.5. This order is therefore scope fulfilled and grants no continuing
M8.5, M8.6, implementation, or successor authority.
