## Status

```text
Effective / Scope bounded
```

## Direction Authority

```text
Architect Council
```

## Authority

This repository-native order opens M8.4 — Gap and Risk Assessment as the sole
active M8 stage after the separate canonicalization and closure of M8.3
Information, Evidence, and Handoff Architecture.

It establishes the M8.4 objective and dependency boundary only. It does not
identify, classify, assess, rank, accept, resolve, or assign any gap or risk and
supplies no substantive M8.4 architecture.

## Stage Objective

Evaluate the architectural gaps, unresolved questions, and risks evidenced by
the canonical M8.1–M8.3 baseline while preserving architectural identity,
capability ownership, information and handoff semantics, source authority,
human authority, and external governance.

## Exclusions

This order does not authorize or define:

- a gap inventory, risk inventory, classification, assessment, likelihood,
  impact, severity, priority, treatment, acceptance, mitigation, ownership,
  recommendation, disposition, or correction;
- implementation, runtime, API, data model, persistence, storage, event,
  messaging, infrastructure, deployment, repository, UI, workflow, lifecycle,
  protocol, product redesign, delivery, release, or staffing mechanics;
- governance amendment, source-authority change, source consolidation,
  capability ownership reassignment, or new organizational or human authority;
- modification of canonical M8.1, M8.2, or M8.3 meaning, their review records,
  unresolved findings, authority conditions, or framing watchpoints;
- Architecture Approval, Canonical Review, canonicalization, M8.4 closure,
  M8.5 opening, or any later-stage or successor transition.

## Completion Boundary

This opening order is satisfied only when one implementation-independent M8.4
candidate is prepared under the active Architecture Backlog task and submitted
to its separately required independent Architecture Review gate. This order
does not approve that future candidate, predetermine its findings, or supply any
part of its substantive architecture.
