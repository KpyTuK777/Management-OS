## Authority

This repository-native order opens M8.6 — Validation and Readiness as the sole
active M8 stage after the separate canonicalization and closure of M8.5
Evolution Architecture.

It establishes the M8.6 objective and dependency boundary only. It does not
define validation criteria, a validation framework, a readiness conclusion, or
any substantive M8.6 architecture.

## Stage Objective

Define the bounded, implementation-independent architectural basis by which the
assembled M8.1–M8.5 architecture may later be evaluated for validation and
readiness while preserving canonical meaning, semantic ownership, authority,
residual exposure, and external governance.

## Completion Boundary

This opening order is satisfied only when one implementation-independent M8.6
candidate is prepared under the active Architecture Backlog task and submitted
to its separately required independent Architecture Review gate. This order
does not approve that future candidate, predetermine its validation or readiness
meaning, or supply any part of substantive Validation and Readiness
architecture.
