## Findings

### 1. Product-wide information-flow model is coherent and complete

**Pass.** The candidate defines an architectural flow unit, eleven
ownership-preserving relation classes, non-linear product-wide direction, all
19 canonical M8.2 major capability boundary roles, universal treatment of
routine source-owned capabilities, seven cross-capability dependency classes,
and an architectural information graph. Flow-unit completeness and handoff
completeness expose source, kind, subject, owner, provenance, uncertainty,
authority, receiving responsibility, non-transfer boundary, and interruption
without becoming a schema, payload, protocol, workflow, or implementation.

### 2. Semantic kinds remain explicitly distinct

**Pass.** Information, Evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, recommendation,
decision, execution intent, execution outcome, learning, and Knowledge each
have a distinct definition, primary semantic owner, and explicit promotion
condition. The model states that these kinds form no automatic ladder. Evidence
admission, interpretation, human decision, outcome verification, learning, and
Knowledge promotion remain separate attributable semantic acts.

### 3. Semantic ownership is preserved across capability handoffs

**Pass.** Every handoff relates separately owned responsibilities. The origin
retains the meaning it produces; the receiver owns only new meaning explicitly
produced within its canonical M8.2 contract. Acceptance begins only an existing
receiver-owned responsibility. Presentation, orchestration, automation,
composition, reference, derivation, correction, and return create no universal
owner, co-owner, or ownership transfer.

### 4. Movement transfers no semantic or authority ownership

**Pass.** Reference, admission, interpretation, contextualization,
recommendation, decision, preparation, responsibility acceptance, outcome
return, historical relation, and promotion candidacy all identify their
ownership result and prohibited inference. Availability, technical receipt,
visibility, silence, elapsed time, confidence, recurrence, automation, and
continued use cannot grant truth, approval, acceptance, permission, source
authority, human authority, or governance authority.

### 5. Provenance, uncertainty, traceability, and authority are consistent

**Pass.** Derivation adds provenance without replacing origin; correction
changes present reliance without erasing historical use. Upstream uncertainty
remains visible while receivers may add only attributable refinements within
their scope. Traceability reconstructs typed semantic relations rather than
creating a universal record. Authority must be explicit and scope-matched and
is expressly non-transitive through capability dependency.

### 6. Product Purpose and System Identity remain framing concerns

**Pass.** The controlling-baseline section, vocabulary, graph node classes,
graph view, conformance conditions, and review-readiness criteria keep Product
Purpose and System Identity at architectural-object level. Their P1 and P2
mappings remain lookup aids; they are not flow nodes, handoff participants,
ordinary capabilities, or locally owned semantics.

### 7. Governance remains external

**Pass.** Canonical M7 remains the external Governance and Evolution constraint
plane for architectural direction, evidence, review, judgment, and authority
transition. It is not a product graph node, capability, product flow, handoff
participant, runtime function, or source of implicit product authority. No
governance amendment is introduced.

### 8. Implementation and prohibited mechanics are absent

**Pass.** The candidate defines semantic relations and architectural
responsibilities only. It introduces no runtime architecture, API, interface,
schema, data model, persistence, storage, event, message, command, queue,
service, component, infrastructure, deployment, repository topology, UI design,
workflow, process automation, scheduling, retry, compensation, escalation,
notification, synchronization, lifecycle change, protocol change,
implementation plan, release, staffing, or delivery mechanics. Terms associated
with these mechanics appear only to disclaim their definition or prevent
misinterpretation.

### 9. M8.4 architecture is absent

**Pass.** Ten gaps and unresolved questions remain evidence-backed,
non-authorizing findings. The candidate neither assesses or ranks them as a Gap
and Risk Assessment nor decides correction, priority, risk treatment, portfolio,
validation, delivery, or successor action. M8.4 remains expressly excluded and
unopened.

### 10. Canonical M8.1 and M8.2 consistency is preserved

**Pass.** M8.1 continues to own architectural identity, boundaries, six product
responsibility views, the cross-cutting product plane, the external governance
plane, responsibility mapping, and source-authority conditions. M8.2 continues
to own capability identity, unique semantic ownership, composition,
dependencies, overlaps, and ownership findings. M8.3 applies those baselines to
cross-capability meaning without modifying, consolidating, upgrading, or
transferring any owned scope.

No blocking Architecture Review deficiency remains unresolved.

## Advisory Observations

1. Any later realization must not reinterpret the architectural handoff
   conditions `prepared`, `accepted`, `qualified`, `declined`, `interrupted`,
   and `returned` as an approved runtime state machine or protocol.
2. The product-wide graph arrows must remain semantic dependencies and must not
   be treated as a workflow, transport topology, mandatory sequence, or
   completeness claim about technical integration.
3. Routine capability acceptance, refusal, outcome, and correction semantics
   remain source-local or unresolved under G2 and G5; implementation or later
   architecture must not normalize them silently.
4. Evidence correction and downstream reliance remain traceably related under
   G3, but exact correction behavior is not supplied by M8.3 where a source does
   not already own it.
5. Decision ownership remains partitioned among reasoning truth separation,
   Matter-relative continuity, C14 preservation, and external accountable human
   authority; handoff convenience must not collapse that partition.

These observations are non-blocking and require no correction to the reviewed
candidate.
