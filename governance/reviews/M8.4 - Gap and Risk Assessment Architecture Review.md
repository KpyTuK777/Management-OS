## AR-M8.4-01 Resolution Findings

**Pass.** Section 2.1 now defines seven distinct assessment objects: Finding,
Gap, Risk, Observation, Recommendation, Conclusion, and Decision. Observation
and Conclusion each have explicit purpose, evidence relationship, authority

### 2. Evidence relationships are distinct

**Pass.** Observation must derive from and remain attributable to
repository-visible evidence but is explanatory rather than evidentiary and
cannot substitute for evidence. Conclusion must be traceable to Findings, Gaps,
Risks, and supporting Observations and may summarize only what those objects

**Pass.** Observation is observational only and may clarify relationships among

Decision authority remains outside M8.4, and neither object gains product,
source, human, organizational, governance, approval, or transition authority.

### 4. Non-authority boundaries are explicit

**Pass.** Observation cannot become a Finding, promote evidence, imply severity,
Recommendation, or Decision, accept or treat Risk, resolve Gap, or authorize
work. Conclusion cannot approve, correct, accept Risk, resolve Gap, authorize
implementation, governance change, backlog work, or stage transition, or become
Architecture Approval or Canonical authority.

### 5. Inter-object relationships are complete and non-overlapping

**Pass.** Each of the seven rows defines its relationship to the other
assessment objects. Finding owns evidence-backed current condition; Gap owns
classified incompleteness; Risk owns possible adverse consequence; Observation
owns explanatory context only; Recommendation owns advisory future direction;
Conclusion owns bounded assessment synthesis; Decision remains an external
authorized choice. No object silently acquires another object's meaning or
authority.

### 6. Observation constraints are complete

**Pass.** The candidate and correction evidence state expressly that an
Observation cannot become a Finding, promote evidence, imply severity, imply a
Recommendation or Decision, or authorize work. They additionally prevent
Observation from accepting or treating Risk, resolving Gap, or changing another
assessment object.

### 7. Conclusion constraints are complete

**Pass.** The candidate and correction evidence state expressly that a
Conclusion cannot approve, correct, accept Risk, resolve Gap, authorize
