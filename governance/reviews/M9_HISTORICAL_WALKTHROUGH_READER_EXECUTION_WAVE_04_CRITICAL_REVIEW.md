# Historical Walkthrough Reader — Execution Wave 4 Critical Review

## Disposition

PASS — NO BLOCKING FINDINGS

## Review basis

The review compares the current-run baseline and final screenshots at 1440×900
and 1920×1080 and executes the strengthened browser contract. It evaluates
viewport use, reading priority, progressive disclosure, navigation clarity,
visual composition, Ukrainian language, accessibility risks visible from the
captured states, and preservation of authority boundaries.

## Corrected findings

1. **Blocking — English dominated the central reading surface.**
   Corrected with a complete Ukrainian-first presentation layer and an explicit
   original-language disclosure.
2. **Blocking — source metadata consumed first-scan attention.**
   Corrected by collapsing source, commit, blob, attribution, evidence metadata,
   uncertainty, and traceability behind named controls.
3. **Blocking — the workbench began too low at 1440×900.**
   Corrected by compacting the oriented header, situation band, status line, and
   phase rail while retaining context.
4. **Blocking — sidebar navigation did not answer operational questions.**
   Corrected with open-case identity, current phase, visited count, attention
   count, current claim, and read-only state.
5. **Non-blocking — orientation retained excessive presentation scale.**
   Corrected by reducing the editorial headline and balancing it with the case
   docket as a working-desk composition.

## Remaining non-blocking risks

- Ukrainian translations are implementation-owned reading aids and have not been
  separately attested as canonical translations. The fixed English wording
  remains immediately available and authoritative for verification.
- Some long claims require vertical continuation at 1440×900. This is content
  length rather than decorative scrolling.
- The phase list and claim list use one intentional horizontal or vertical
  bounded navigation region where the complete collection cannot fit.
- Screenshot review does not establish full screen-reader or contrast compliance.

## Boundary review

No canonical, Product, Interaction Architecture, Engineering Architecture,
historical, source, Evidence, governance, Replay, H3, Root Cause, causal-proof,
or deployment boundary changed. ROADMAP and BACKLOG remain untouched.

## Final judgment

Wave 4 shifts the interface from presentation toward use: the Product Owner can
now concentrate on the historical situation and investigation experience rather
than first decoding English source prose or navigating repository metadata.
