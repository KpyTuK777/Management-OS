## Architecture Review Findings

### 1. AR-M8.6-01 — Deterministic readiness classification

**Resolved — Pass.** Section 11.2 fixes the subject, scope, purpose, and evidence
boundary before classification and applies one explicit stop-on-first-match
sequence: R0 for blockers or an unbounded assessment; R2 for a claim whose
support depends on unresolved architectural conditions; R3 exclusively for an
unconditional complete-package M8.7-input purpose; R4 exclusively for an
unconditional later-engineering-architecture purpose; and R1 as the remaining
bounded-purpose outcome. Each table row expressly excludes every other class.

The procedure is exhaustive because an ambiguous assessment reaches R0, any
Fail or non-excludable Indeterminate is an architectural blocker, and an
otherwise supported conclusion reaches R2, R3, R4, or the R1 fallback. It is
mutually exclusive because evaluation stops at the first satisfied rule. R3
states that M8.7 has neither opened nor passed. R4 states that no Engineering
Architecture approval or authorization, implementation approval, backlog
authority, or implementation work follows.

### 2. AR-M8.6-02 — Governance eligibility separated from blockers

**Resolved — Pass.** Sections 12.2 and 12.3 restrict blockers to fixed and
attributable subjects, evidence sufficiency, semantic ownership, provenance,
uncertainty, human and source-authority boundaries, dependency integrity,
traceability, contradictions, residual-exposure visibility, and architectural
completeness. No external approval, transition, canonicalization, or permission
state determines the readiness class.

Section 12.4 separately records governance eligibility as an external,
non-architectural determination. It expressly states that eligibility cannot
change a readiness class or cure an architectural deficiency and that readiness
cannot grant eligibility or authorization. M8.6 owns architectural-readiness
assessment only.
