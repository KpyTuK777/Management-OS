## Information-class disposition

- Organizational facts: permitted when authoritative, effective-dated, necessary, and correctable.
- Attributable statements: permitted as statements with historical capacity and limitations, never automatic facts.
- Manager observations: exceptional, bounded, restricted, attributable, time-bound, disputable, and unavailable as general employment judgments.
- System-derived records: source/limitation-bound; surveillance and behavioral inference prohibited.
- Investigation material/Evidence: retained under Evidence authority, never flattened into profile facts.
- Sensitive people information: excluded, separately gated, or prohibited according to class.
