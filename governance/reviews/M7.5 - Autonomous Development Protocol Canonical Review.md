## Canonical Findings

### 1. Constitutional consistency

**Pass.** The protocol preserves accountable human authority, bounded power,
separation, non-self-approval, evidence integrity, traceability, semantic
ownership, implementation independence, and legitimacy over autonomous
completion or convenience.

### 2. Organizational consistency

**Pass.** Protocol roles allocate execution responsibility only and remain
subordinate to Direction, Creation, Judgment, Transition, Semantic Stewardship,
and Governance Record Custody. No seventh organizational function or hidden
decision authority is introduced.

### 3. Protocol identity boundary

**Pass.** Protocol identity is matter-, function-, delegation-, sponsor-, and
work-unit-specific. It is explicitly distinct from human identity,
organizational office, lifecycle identity, credential, service account,
session, and tool identity and cannot become a governance authority source.

### 4. Delegation integrity

**Pass.** Delegation envelopes inherit constraints, remain narrower than source
authority, preserve accountable human sponsorship, grant nothing through
silence, and end future action upon revocation or expiry. Redelegation cannot
enlarge or detach authority from its source.

### 5. Lifecycle fidelity

**Pass.** Protocol conditions describe execution posture only. They cannot
enter, exit, replace, or bypass any M7.4 lifecycle stage or gate. Each lifecycle
stage retains its canonical authority meaning and owning function.

### 6. Initiation and handoff integrity

**Pass.** Offer is not permission; acceptance requires independent validation;
refusal is mandatory when authority or conformance is deficient; and handoff
transfers only explicitly accepted responsibility. Communication delivery,
assignment, access, silence, timeout, or tool status transfers no authority.

### 7. Evidence and review separation

**Pass.** Evidence distinguishes source, action, observation, derivation,
validation, negative result, and limitation. It cannot issue judgment.
Autonomous review support preserves fixed subjects, participation disclosure,
independent Judgment, finding ownership, re-judgment, and separate Transition
Authority.

### 8. Interruption and recovery integrity

**Pass.** Interruption and suspension stop autonomous action when authority,
scope, evidence, identity, provenance, dependency, or risk becomes invalid or
unknowable. Recovery requires action by the owning authority and revalidation
of the envelope; retry, urgency, alternate identity, or degraded capability
cannot create permission.

### 9. Implementation independence

**Pass.** The protocol defines enduring meaning without selecting repositories,
schemas, products, agents, models, vendors, tools, algorithms, infrastructure,
runtime topology, orchestration, scheduling, retry policy, queue discipline, or
implementation methodology.

### 10. Stable M7.6 foundation

**Pass.** Governance Operations may later realize inspectable execution,
delegation, handoff, evidence, suspension, and recovery controls while remaining
constrained by the protocol. M7.5 neither defines Governance Operations nor
authorizes M7.6 work.
