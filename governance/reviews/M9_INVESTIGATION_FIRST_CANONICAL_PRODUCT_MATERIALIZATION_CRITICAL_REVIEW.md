## Adversarial challenges

### Existing Investigation code could be mistaken for reviewed Product realization

The repository already contains a large Investigation-oriented `index.html`, `natural-capture.js`, model modules, local-state behavior, and tests. This is useful feasibility evidence but includes demo collection and generated-hypothesis behavior that may exceed the proposed authority if accepted wholesale.

Disposition: NON-BLOCKING. The inventory and plan require selective reconciliation, traceability per behavior, disabled or clearly demo-only AI/external behaviors, and an explicit ban on legacy code defining Product meaning.

### “Maximum materialization” could silently become complete-lifecycle scope

Monitoring, Stabilization, Closure, and Reopening are canonically defined but not sufficiently realized as one bounded Product/Interaction/Engineering slice for this MVP.

Disposition: NON-BLOCKING. They are excluded and classified `NOT READY — PRODUCT MEANING INCOMPLETE` for materialization purposes.

### Timeline semantics are stronger than timeline interaction readiness

Event time, record time, uncertain time, and source linkage are supported, but interactive ordering and authoring are not fully contracted.

Disposition: NON-BLOCKING. The plan permits only a labeled static/read-only timeline and makes interactive authoring a stop condition.

### Browser-local persistence may be overread as operational continuity

Local state can demonstrate continuity but cannot establish durable identity-bound, cross-device, or production persistence.

Disposition: NON-BLOCKING. Inventory and plan constrain it to session/browser-local demonstration.

### Shared primitive consolidation can create semantic erasure

Technical reuse could merge Claim, Evidence, Artifact, and Hypothesis or create a universal graph abstraction.

Disposition: NON-BLOCKING. Both abstractions are explicitly rejected; shared Product meaning must be proven independently of reusable code.
