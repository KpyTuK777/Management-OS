## Architecture Review Findings

### 1. AR-M8.7-01 — Modeled-output boundary

**Resolved — Pass.** Section 5 places `Modeled / Scenario Output` as a distinct
semantic position after Interpretation / Reasoning and before Human Judgment.
Its trace row states explicitly that it is not Evidence, Observation, Fact,
Prediction, Recommendation, or Decision and cannot gain authority through
presentation, confidence, reuse, or similarity. The separate Decision and
Observed Outcome positions prevent modeled content from being read as either a
human decision or an observed consequence.

Section 5.1 permits exploration of alternatives and possible consequences while
requiring a scenario to remain a model. Reuse cannot turn it into Evidence, high
confidence cannot change its semantic kind, and presentation, confidence,
reuse, or similarity cannot promote it to Fact, Observation, Prediction,
Recommendation, Decision, or authority.

Section 9 preserves the same boundary in MI-06, prevents Recommendation from
inheriting modeled-output authority and retains explicit human Decision in
MI-08, prohibits automated promotion in MI-09, and requires attributable
observation or source-owned Evidence plus owned verification for outcome in
MI-17. Section 14 includes a dedicated review-readiness criterion covering
Evidence, Observation, Fact, Prediction, Recommendation, Decision, and outcome.
