# 2. Primary Uncertainty

Section 4 states the primary uncertainty as whether Management OS creates an
observable, useful contribution in real operational contexts. Its explanatory
text connects the question to context, inspectable reasoning, Decision-to-Change
fidelity, material Outcomes, bounded attribution, and Learning.

The proposal explicitly denies reduction to satisfaction, feature usage,
implementation progress, shipping, task completion, artifact volume, or
activity counts. Section 8 excludes general market validation, and Section 14
separates Learning success from adoption, revenue, and product-market fit.

This matches the post-M8 finding that the fundamental uncertainty is empirical
product value rather than further broad document production. It does not turn
usability, adoption, market demand, or implementation maturity into substitutes
for that uncertainty.

**Finding:** Pass. The dominant uncertainty is stated at the correct product-
value level and retains its real-context boundary.

# 3. Milestone Purpose Quality

The proposed purpose is bounded by the phrase “bounded, inspectable empirical
learning” and identifies the value subject, operational manager context, and
protected Evidence, human authority, source ownership, Change, Outcome, and
Learning boundaries.

The proposal defines Learning as a supportable change in what is known and
expressly permits negative, mixed, null, contrary, adverse, unexpected, and
indeterminate findings. It says that producing Learning does not mean obtaining
a positive result. Section 14 reinforces this by allowing credible rejection or
indeterminate disposition to satisfy the Learning identity.

**Finding:** Pass. The purpose is bounded, empirical, inspectable, product-value
oriented, authority-compatible, and not outcome-seeking by definition.

## Advisory Observations

### M9P-01 — Compact stage sequences omit visible authority gates

**Exact proposal evidence:** Model A includes “Bounded empirical work”; Model B
includes “Hypothesis selection” and “Bounded empirical work”; Model C includes
“Candidate case identification” and “Bounded empirical work.”

**Source comparison:** Canonical M7 preserves attributable stage and transition
authority; canonical M8.6 and the proposal's own Section 10 keep subject
selection, preparation, and validation authorization separate.

**Observation:** The complete Section 9 says the sequences explain internal
logic only and create no stages or work. An isolated list could omit that
qualification and appear to be an approved lifecycle.

**Consequence:** Later reuse could treat proposal model labels as automatic
stage or validation authority.

**Bounded future clarification:** If a later human requests proposal refinement,
repeat “illustrative / not authorized” within or immediately under each compact
model list. No correction, model selection, or opening is required by this
review.

### M9P-02 — Product Constitution classification is preserved globally but not local to its citation

**Exact proposal evidence:** The source basis lists
`MANAGEMENT_OS_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION_CANDIDATE.md` and
`PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION_NON_CANONICAL_REFERENCE_ROLE_DECISION.md`; the final source
rule preserves each source's own authority and denies elevation.

**Source comparison:** The role Decision recognizes only a fixed candidate blob
as a non-canonical auxiliary product-philosophy reference with no canonical,
milestone, validation, implementation, or requirements authority.

**Observation:** The proposal uses no Constitution claim as authority, but a
reader consulting only the source list does not see the classification beside
the candidate name.

**Consequence:** Out-of-context source-list reuse could overstate the candidate's
role.

**Bounded future clarification:** If the proposal is revised for approval
consideration, label the candidate locally as the fixed non-canonical auxiliary
product-philosophy reference. No current correction is required because the
substantive proposal and global source rule preserve the role.

### M9P-03 — Option A title is stronger when detached from its qualifying text

**Exact proposal evidence:** Section 18 titles option A “Approve bounded M9
opening based on this proposal,” followed by text requiring a separate
attributable human Decision and authority transition that fixes identity, scope,
boundaries, stages, effects, and non-effects.

**Source comparison:** The recorded preparation Decision authorizes only
proposal creation; canonical M7 and current control state require a separate
legitimate transition for opening.

**Observation:** The qualification is sufficient in context, but the title alone
could be quoted as if this proposal already supplied an opening recommendation
or complete opening definition.

**Consequence:** Isolated reuse could obscure that open scope and stage-model
questions must be resolved in the later human Decision.

**Bounded future clarification:** If refined, keep the separate-Decision
qualification in the option label or immediately adjacent text. No correction
or readiness claim follows from this observation.
