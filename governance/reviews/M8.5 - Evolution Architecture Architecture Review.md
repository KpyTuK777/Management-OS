## Architecture Review Findings

### 1. Implementation-independent evolution objectives

**Pass.** Section 5 defines coherence, ownership, dependency, alternative,
provenance, source-authority, fragmentation, and review objectives. Sections 1,
4, 6, 13, and 19 exclude technical realization, delivery selection, product
commitment, and work authorization. No objective depends on an implementation
form.

### 2. Internal coherence of evolution principles

**Pass.** EP-01 through EP-10 form a consistent chain: attributable baseline,
responsibility-first change, unique ownership, authority before normative
dependence, evidence before claim, preservation by default, genuine
alternatives, visible residual exposure, smallest coherent delta, and
implementation independence. No principle grants an exception to another.

### 3. Capability evolution and canonical ownership

**Pass.** Sections 7.1–7.3 require one primary semantic owner, preserved
non-ownership, explicit source relationships, and an unresolved prerequisite
where genuinely new ownership is not yet established. Clarification,
refinement, extension, composition, emergence, constraint evolution, and
retirement cannot silently absorb, duplicate, transfer, or omit canonical M8.2
meaning.

### 4. Dependency-respecting increments

**Pass.** Section 8 defines an architectural increment as a bounded semantic
delta rather than a release or work package. Identity, responsibility,
capability, semantic, handoff, trust, source, governance, and assessment
dependencies are explicit, and Section 8.3 requires unsatisfied prerequisites
to remain open rather than be filled by assumption.

### 5. Alternative paths and product priority

**Pass.** Section 9 presents six non-ranked routes: preserve and clarify,
refine, evolve composition, establish a future capability option, evolve a
cross-cutting constraint, and retain the current state with explicit residual
exposure. It expressly states that routes express compatibility and
prerequisites only and are not priorities, recommendations, commitments, or
plans. No route is selected or preferred.

### 6. Architectural sequencing boundary

**Pass.** Section 10 defines logical precedence for coherent meaning and
explicitly distinguishes it from delivery sequencing. Its baseline, ownership,
semantic, evidence, dependency, trust, governance, and canonical precedence
constraints impose no dates, releases, team assignments, execution order, or
work order.

### 7. Evolution invariants and canonical M8.1–M8.4

**Pass.** EI-01 through EI-16 preserve the controlling baseline, framing
concerns, external governance, unique ownership, semantic distinctions, source
authority, human authority, trust attributes, non-authority of future options,
and unchanged M8.4 disposition. They are consistent with the responsibility,
capability, handoff, and assessment models in canonical M8.1–M8.4.

### 8. Architectural decision boundaries

**Pass.** Section 13 distinguishes what architecture may determine from actions
requiring governance and from product-planning and implementation authority.
The candidate defines the model for future determinations but makes no decision
about a particular future option.

### 9. External Governance boundary

**Pass.** Sections 3.2, 8.2, 10, and 13 keep canonical M7 as an external
Governance and Evolution constraint plane. Governance supplies review,
authorization, and authority transition; it is not a product layer, capability,
or internal product mechanism. No governance meaning is amended.

### 10. Product Purpose and System Identity

**Pass.** Section 3.1 preserves Product Purpose and System Identity as
architectural-object-level framing constraints. Their M8.1 mapping to P1 and P2
remains a lookup aid and does not become capability responsibility or locally
evolvable ownership.

### 11. M8.4 Gap preservation

**Pass.** Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, 13, 14, 16, 17, and 19 prohibit implicit Gap
resolution. Section 14 retains GAP-01 through GAP-15 as possible evidence,
constraints, or residual exposure and expressly prevents resolution, narrowing,
reclassification, or work assignment.

### 12. M8.4 Risk preservation

**Pass.** The same boundaries preserve RISK-01 through RISK-15 without
acceptance, treatment, reduction, reclassification, or ownership assignment.
Route F's no-change possibility explicitly states that retained residual
exposure does not constitute Risk acceptance.

### 13. Advisory Recommendation preservation

**Pass.** REC-01 through REC-06 remain attributable advisory inputs only.
Sections 4 and 14 prevent a Recommendation from becoming a decision, approval,
treatment, ranking, work item, or adopted direction through comparison or
repetition.

### 14. Absence of implementation architecture

**Pass.** Candidate meaning is expressed in responsibilities, semantic deltas,
ownership, dependencies, prerequisites, authority, and coherence tests. It does
not define a technical realization or make one the basis of architectural
meaning.

### 15. Prohibited technical, planning, and delivery substance

**Pass.** No runtime, API, schema, data model, service, event, storage,
infrastructure, deployment, UI, prompt, workflow, protocol, lifecycle change,
roadmap priority, delivery plan, backlog item, or technical topology is defined.
References to those concepts occur only to distinguish or exclude them from
architectural meaning.

### 16. M8.6 and M8.7 exclusion

**Pass.** Section 19 excludes M8.6 Validation and Readiness and M8.7 Integrated
Architecture Review substance. Section 20 states candidate-level Architecture
Review readiness only; it does not define a validation framework, integrated
review, later stage, or successor milestone.

### 17. Source-authority preservation

**Pass.** Sections 2, 7, 8, 12, 13, 16, and 17 require recorded source scope and
authority condition to remain attributable and unchanged. Mapping, dependency,
reuse, comparison, or convenience cannot grant, normalize, consolidate, or
upgrade authority.

### 18. Canonical baseline integrity

**Pass.** The reviewed commit adds the M8.5 definition and permitted live-status
metadata only. Canonical M8.1–M8.4, canonical M7, their review evidence, and the
M8.5 opening order remain unchanged. The candidate claims no Architecture
Approval or canonical authority and performs no closure or transition.

No blocking Architecture Review finding exists.

## Advisory Observations

1. The M8 Vision label `Capability Evolution Portfolio` must continue to carry
   the candidate's Section 2 non-planning meaning; later use must not turn the
   option set into priorities, investment allocation, or delivery commitments.
2. A genuinely new capability may be explored with ownership unresolved only
   as an incomplete evolution subject. It cannot become a coherent increment or
   normative dependency until unique ownership is established through separate
   authority.
3. Route F preserves visible residual exposure but is not Risk acceptance. Any
   later record relying on no architectural change must keep that distinction
   explicit.
4. Candidate Section 20 review-readiness checks are local Architecture Review
   criteria and must not be reinterpreted as the M8.6 Validation and Readiness
   architecture.

These observations are non-blocking and require no correction to the reviewed
candidate.
