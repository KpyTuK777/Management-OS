## Critical findings resolved in the reviewed preparation

### 1. Phase 1 must not imply that historical reconstruction has begun

The suggested label “Що сталося” would describe historical content that Phase 1 explicitly does not contain. The reviewed system correctly uses “Як читати цей кейс”, while retaining the protocol identity as secondary metadata.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 2. Phase 11 must not pre-empt attributable reflection or H3 learning

The suggested label “Що це змінює у нашому розумінні” would state a learning outcome before the human-owned reflection has occurred. The reviewed system correctly uses “Що варто осмислити”.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 3. Detection language must not invent actor recognition

“Як її помітили” could imply that a particular actor recognized a signal. The reviewed system uses “Які сигнали були видимі”, which preserves the distinction between publicly reconstructable signals and actor-specific knowledge.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 4. Response language must not imply successful containment

A generic response label could silently imply that the incident was brought under control. “Як реагували і що відомо про зупинку” preserves containment uncertainty.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 5. A source excerpt is not interchangeable with fixed claim text

The specification distinguishes the exact fixed claim text from a bounded verbatim source excerpt. A source excerpt may be shown only when verified against the fixed locator; otherwise the inspection surface must state that the excerpt is unavailable rather than synthesize one.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 6. Missing temporal precision must remain missing

The Level 2 contract requires event time and publication time to remain separate. Missing precision must be presented as unknown and may not be inferred merely to complete the interface.

Status: RESOLVED.

### 7. Plain language can accidentally strengthen a claim

The specification controls this risk through direct claim/source traceability, clause-level review, preserved qualifiers, a prohibition on aggregated attribution, a default one-to-one mapping, and review of all 48 fixed claims rather than a sample. A passage that fails the transformation contract must be withheld.

Status: RESOLVED IN CONTRACT; EXECUTION VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
