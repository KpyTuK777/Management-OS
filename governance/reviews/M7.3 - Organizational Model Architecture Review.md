## Scope

This record preserves the Architect Council's completed independent
Architecture Review of the M7.3 Organizational Model against the canonical
M7.1 Governance Structure, canonical M7.2 Constitution, repository-native M7.3
order, and attributable definition evidence.

## Findings

1. The six governance functions have explicit, non-overlapping responsibility
   domains and authority boundaries.
2. Direction, creation, judgment, and transition remain separately
   attributable, and no function can authorize its own output.
3. Semantic stewardship preserves the unique ownership of the authoritative
   source rather than creating parallel semantic authority.
4. Delegation remains bounded by source authority and cannot erase identifiable
   human accountability.
5. Governance bodies are optional and cannot acquire authority through custom,
   composition, tooling, or repeated practice.
6. Decision classes assign one owning authority and prohibit cross-class
   substitution.
7. The model remains independent of individuals, titles, AI systems, tools,
   repositories, and implementation methods.
8. The logical interaction of powers introduces no workflow, review procedure,
   lifecycle, autonomous protocol, or M7.4–M7.7 substance.

No blocking or advisory Architecture Review finding remains unresolved.
