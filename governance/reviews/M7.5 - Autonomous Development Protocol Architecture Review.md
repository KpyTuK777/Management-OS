## Findings

1. Protocol identities are attributable execution identities and cannot become
   human, organizational, lifecycle, semantic, review, or transition authority.
2. Protocol roles allocate bounded execution responsibility without adding to
   or replacing the six canonical organizational functions.
3. Delegation envelopes inherit constraints, remain narrower than source
   authority, preserve identifiable human accountability, and cannot transfer
   or enlarge authority.
4. Offered, Accepted, Executing, Handoff Pending, Suspended, Completed, and
   Released are protocol conditions rather than M7.4 lifecycle stages or gates.
5. Work offer, validation, acceptance, refusal, execution, and handoff preserve
   explicit scope, semantic ownership, decision-class ownership, evidence, and
   receiver responsibility.
6. Evidence production preserves provenance, negative and contradictory
   results, uncertainty, and the boundary between observation and judgment.
7. Review interaction preserves fixed subjects, participation disclosure,
   independent Judgment, finding ownership, correction re-entry, and separate
   Transition Authority.
8. Interruption, suspension, recovery, release, and termination preserve the
   last valid authority condition and cannot create permission through urgency,
   retry, degraded capability, or alternate identity.
9. The protocol remains independent of tools, repositories, platforms,
   products, vendors, algorithms, infrastructure, runtime topology, and
   implementation methodology.
10. Governance operations, governance effectiveness assessment, retrospective
    conclusions, and M7.6–M7.7 authority remain excluded.

No blocking Architecture Review finding remains unresolved.

## Advisory Observations

- M7.6 Governance Operations must treat protocol records and conditions as
  representations of established responsibility and execution posture, never
  as independent authority or lifecycle state.
- Any later implementation must make delegation, stop, evidence, handoff, and
  recovery controls inspectable without weakening the protocol's
  implementation-independent meaning.
- Conformance review of an implementation should test identity separation,
  revocation effectiveness, fixed-subject review behavior, negative-evidence
  preservation, and recovery from partial effects.

These observations preserve downstream review concerns and require no M7.5
correction.
