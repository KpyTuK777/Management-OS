interaction, and dependency models; major and routine capability coverage;

responsibilities; bounded contexts; ownership-preserving cooperation;
composition principles; ownership invariants; overlap determinations;

## Findings

### 1. Capability coverage is complete and bounded

**Pass.** The candidate accounts for all product systems recorded in
`docs/SYSTEMS.md` and the major cross-capability architectures mapped by
canonical M8.1. It identifies 19 major semantic capabilities and 13 routine
source-owned operational capabilities. Home and situation experiences are
classified as composition; identity, trust, platform allocation, and governance
are classified as cross-cutting or allocation responsibilities; Operational
Learning is classified as a bounded context reusing Investigation. These
classifications prevent modules, projections, constraints, and contexts from
being mislabeled as duplicate capabilities.

### 2. Semantic ownership is unique and internally coherent

**Pass.** Each capability names one identifiable primary semantic owner or an
explicitly partitioned source set whose members own non-overlapping parts of a
composite contract. Detailed presentation sources remain scoped composition
owners rather than co-owners of domain semantics. Routine systems retain
`docs/SYSTEMS.md` ownership where no more specific source governs their purpose
and boundary. No universal capability owner or hidden duplicate semantic owner
is introduced.

### 3. Composition transfers no ownership or authority

**Pass.** Reference, context use, projection, preparation, constraint,
historical relevance, and candidate formation are defined as ownership-
preserving relations. Matter centrality, Investigation work, orchestration,
Transition coordination, Memory relevance, experience presentation, and AI
assistance do not absorb source meaning, state, validation, mutation, retention,
human judgment, or authority. Dependency is expressly non-transitive for
authority.

### 4. Product Purpose and System Identity remain framing concerns

**Pass.** The controlling-baseline section, identity tests, framing diagram,
ownership invariants, conformance conditions, and review-readiness criteria all
preserve Product Purpose and System Identity as architectural-object-level
concerns. Their P1 and P2 mappings remain lookup aids and do not become ordinary
capability ownership local to either layer.

### 5. Governance remains external

**Pass.** Canonical M7 governs direction, evidence, review, and authority
transition as the external Governance and Evolution constraint plane. The
candidate distinguishes governance authority from semantic, source,
organizational, human, runtime, and implementation responsibility and creates no
product governance capability or runtime governance function.

### 6. M8.3 architecture is excluded

**Pass.** The interaction model defines abstract semantic relationship classes,
and the dependency model defines interpretive dependency direction only. The
candidate expressly excludes messages, payloads, events, commands, state
transitions, sequencing, failure behavior, orchestration mechanics, exact
information movement, evidence movement, authority movement, and handoff
contracts. It introduces no M8.3 Information, Evidence, and Handoff
Architecture.

### 7. Implementation and later-stage leakage is absent

**Pass.** No runtime architecture, provider behavior, API, schema, data model,
storage, persistence, event system, service, component, process, queue,
infrastructure, deployment, repository topology, UI behavior, interaction
specification, lifecycle, protocol, governance amendment, implementation plan,
product priority, release scope, staffing, or delivery sequence is defined.
Implementation status appears only as a non-authorizing ownership distinction or
source condition.

### 8. Overlaps, boundaries, and unresolved findings are evidence-backed

**Pass.** The eight overlap determinations refine the six canonical M8.1 overlap
areas and two directly evidenced composition questions: Transition versus
source-owned execution, and Natural Capture versus Notes and Home. Each
determination preserves the boundary recorded in its owning sources. The six
unresolved findings trace to canonical M8.1 unknowns, `docs/SYSTEMS.md`, the
Documentation Map, or cited capability sources and explicitly authorize no
correction, consolidation, ownership reassignment, capability creation, or
later-stage work.

### 9. Canonical M8.1 consistency is preserved

**Pass.** The candidate retains the M8.1 system identity, real-system boundary,
six product responsibility views, cross-cutting product plane, external
governance plane, primary responsibility ownership, authority conditions,
assumptions, invariants, and exclusions. M8.1 is used as the controlling
baseline and is not reinterpreted, amended, or replaced.

### 10. Source authority conditions and ownership remain unchanged

**Pass.** The candidate uses the authority conditions recorded by canonical
M8.1 and `docs/DOCUMENTATION_MAP.md`. It grants, normalizes, upgrades,
consolidates, or transfers no source authority and modifies no existing source.
Composite ownership is partitioned explicitly rather than converted into joint
universal authority.

### 11. Candidate authority posture is correct

**Pass.** Both status blocks state `Ready for Architecture Review` and `No
approval or canonical authority claimed`. The authority condition and explicit
exclusions disclaim Architecture Approval, Canonical Review, canonicalization,
M8.2 closure, M8.3 opening, repository transition, implementation, and
later-stage authority.

No blocking Architecture Review deficiency remains unresolved.

## Advisory Observations

1. M8.3 must not reinterpret the M8.2 composition relations or semantic
   dependencies as already approved messages, information paths, handoff
   contracts, sequencing rules, or authority transitions.
2. If a routine system later receives a dedicated authoritative architecture,
   that source change should be evaluated explicitly; it must not silently
   rewrite the reviewed M8.2 ownership map.
3. The partitioned ownership of Owner Decision preservation should remain
   visible: reasoning owns truth separation and traceability, Matter owns the
   macro-state relationship, and human authority remains external.
4. Recommendations and coaching remain bounded behaviors or future directions
   under existing sources until separate authority establishes any independent
   capability identity.

These observations are non-blocking and require no correction to the reviewed
candidate.
