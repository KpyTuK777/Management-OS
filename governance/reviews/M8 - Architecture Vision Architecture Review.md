## Review Independence

The Architect Council conducted this Architecture Review independently from the
creation of the reviewed M8 Architecture Vision. The review evaluates only the
fixed revision and content identity named above. It does not author or modify
the Vision, open M8, activate a proposed stage, create a backlog task, approve
implementation, perform canonicalization, or transition the repository roadmap.

## Scope

This review evaluates whether the M8 Architecture Vision provides a coherent,
complete, internally consistent, and bounded architectural basis that may be
considered for a future, separately authorized Milestone M8 opening. It evaluates
the objective, scope, deliverables, decomposition, dependency graph, success
criteria, exclusions, proposed sequence, risks, assumptions, authority boundary,
and review readiness against the completed canonical M7 Governance Architecture.

## Findings

1. **Architectural direction is coherent.** The proposed Operational
   Intelligence Evolution Architecture gives M8 one intelligible purpose:
   integrate the existing capability architecture into a reviewable evolution
   baseline without replacing source-owned meaning or human judgment.
2. **Scope is complete and internally consistent.** The scope spans owner
   experience, operational reasoning, evidence, decision, transition, learning,
   bounded AI assistance, knowledge contexts, capability ownership, handoffs,
   readiness, and M7 conformance. Its scope posture consistently remains at the
   architectural level.
3. **Deliverables cover the objective.** Corpus mapping, integrated capability
   architecture, authority and handoff mapping, gap assessment, evolution
   portfolio, validation framework, and integrated review collectively address
   baseline identity, coherence, uncertainty, dependency, future direction, and
   independent judgment.
4. **Decomposition is logical and non-overlapping.** M8.1 establishes the source
   baseline; M8.2 owns capability and responsibility coherence; M8.3 owns
   information, evidence, authority, and handoff relationships; M8.4 evaluates
   gaps and risks; M8.5 organizes architectural evolution choices; M8.6 defines
   validation and readiness; and M8.7 performs integrated architectural review.
   Each proposed stage has a distinct question and candidate output.
5. **Dependencies are acyclic and justified.** The proposed graph is a directed
   linear sequence from attributable inputs through baseline, coherence,
   handoffs, gaps, evolution, validation, and integrated review. Every later
   output depends on information established earlier; no dependency returns
   authority or completion to an upstream stage.
6. **Success criteria are measurable architecturally.** Each criterion can be
   judged from attributable artifacts and evidence: inventory coverage,
   responsibility uniqueness, traceability, governance conformance, preserved
   human authority, implementation independence, evolution clarity, explicit
   risk, and review readiness.
7. **Exclusions prevent premature work.** The Vision explicitly excludes
   governance amendment, normative definitions, lifecycle or protocol creation,
   M8 opening, stage activation, backlog work, implementation design, repository
   transition, delivery planning, product priority, authority-status changes,
   successor work, and adoption of M7.6 recommendations.
8. **No normative authority is introduced.** The Vision identifies itself as an
   exploratory draft and disclaims order, roadmap, backlog, normative,
   lifecycle, protocol, review-disposition, approval, dependency, and canonical
   effects.
9. **Canonical M7 governance remains fixed.** M7 is used solely as the governing
   constraint and review baseline. The Vision neither modifies nor reinterprets
   the Constitution, Organizational Model, Development Lifecycle, Autonomous
   Development Protocol, Governance Structure, Retrospective, or Governance
   Review.
10. **No M8 stage is implicitly opened.** Proposed identifiers are expressly
    planning labels rather than opened or reserved stage identities. Both status
    blocks state `Milestone M8 — Not Open`, and the roadmap and backlog remain in
    the completed M7 state.
11. **No out-of-scope substance is introduced.** The document contains no active
    task, implementation work, repository mechanics, new lifecycle, new
    protocol, runtime orchestration, product interaction design, data model, API,
    component, service, algorithm, infrastructure, release, or delivery plan.
12. **Review-to-opening separation is explicit.** Architecture Review is treated
    as judgment only. A positive disposition may establish an architectural
    basis for considering M8 opening but cannot itself perform the opening or
    authorize a proposed stage.

No blocking Architecture Review deficiency remains unresolved.

## Deficiency Classification

### Advisory observations

1. A future M8.1 definition should establish explicit corpus inclusion and
   authority-classification criteria before attempting completeness claims.
2. Any future milestone-opening order should confirm or revise the proposed
   stage names and boundaries rather than treating planning labels in the Vision
   as canonical reservations.
3. Future review should test whether the linear dependency proposal needs
   bounded feedback for correction while preserving the canonical M7 lifecycle
   and avoiding a second lifecycle definition.
4. The future evolution portfolio should keep architectural dependency separate
   from product priority, delivery sequence, and implementation readiness.

These observations are non-blocking and require no correction to the reviewed
Vision.
