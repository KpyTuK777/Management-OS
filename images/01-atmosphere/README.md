# Atmosphere

## Purpose

Define the environmental feeling that surrounds operational work.

## Questions answered

- How do light, quiet, depth, and warmth affect concentration?
- How should the product feel inhabited without looking decorated?
- What creates trust during prolonged thinking?

## Supports

Cognitive calm, orientation, seriousness, warmth, and private professional focus.

## Belongs here

References about lighting, environmental depth, studio quiet, working patina, and restrained human presence.

## Avoid

Cinematic drama, literal detective sets, luxury signaling, dark surfaces without contrast, and atmosphere that competes with evidence.
