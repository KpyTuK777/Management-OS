# Textures

## Purpose

Define subtle surface variation that prevents sterile digital flatness without reducing clarity.

## Questions answered

- What level of grain or imperfection feels natural?
- Which texture supports depth at interface scale?
- When should texture disappear?

## Supports

Warmth, quiet tactility, continuity, and reduced digital sterility.

## Belongs here

Low-contrast grain, paper fibers, restrained patina, and annotated scale or opacity studies.

## Avoid

Seam visibility, noise behind small text, grunge, themed patterns, high-frequency detail, and texture used to hide weak hierarchy.
