# Watson

## Purpose

Define the visual presence of quiet, optional, risk-aware assistance.

## Questions answered

- How can Watson remain available without becoming a colleague avatar or permanent panel?
- How do proposals differ from owner-approved material?
- When may risk interrupt the owner’s flow?

## Supports

Trust, correction, restraint, clarity, and owner authority.

## Belongs here

Compact assistant states, semantic review, proposed relationships, risk interruptions, and calm acknowledgments.

## Avoid

Chat-first layouts, anthropomorphic spectacle, constant prompts, glowing AI decoration, conversational blocking, and ambiguous authority.
