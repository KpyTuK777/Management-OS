# Moodboard

## Purpose

Explore the broad emotional territory of Management OS before selecting a specific interface treatment.

## Questions answered

- What should the product feel like before its function is recognized?
- Which references suggest calm, judgment, maturity, and operational focus?
- Which directions feel distinctive without becoming theatrical?

## Supports

Warmth, professional confidence, intellectual depth, restraint, and desire to remain in the workspace.

## Belongs here

Early cross-category references and annotated visual hypotheses that have not earned canonical status.

## Avoid

Trend collections, unannotated inspiration, generic SaaS screens, detective fiction, and images selected only for surface beauty.
