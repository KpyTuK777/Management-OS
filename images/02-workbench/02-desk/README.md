# Workbench Desk

## Purpose

Define the immediate working surface and the relationship among map, notebook, tools, and supporting material.

## Questions answered

- What remains permanently within reach?
- What belongs beside rather than over the investigation?
- How does material depth clarify role?

## Supports

Readiness, tactility, ownership, and uninterrupted contribution.

## Belongs here

Drafting desks, working notebooks, restrained stationery, document arrangements, and practical tool placement.

## Avoid

Decorative props, messy realism, floating overlays, ornamental stationery, and objects without an operational role.
