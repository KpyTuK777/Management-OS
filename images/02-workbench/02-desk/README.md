# Workbench Desk

## Purpose

Define the immediate working surface and the relationship among map, notebook, tools, and supporting material.

## Questions answered

- What remains permanently within reach?
- What belongs beside rather than over the investigation?
- How does material depth clarify role?

## Supports

Readiness, tactility, ownership, and uninterrupted contribution.

## Belongs here

Drafting desks, working notebooks, restrained stationery, document arrangements, and practical tool placement.

## Avoid

Decorative props, messy realism, floating overlays, ornamental stationery, and objects without an operational role.

## Accepted references

### [`workbench-desk-thinking-table-01.jpg`](workbench-desk-thinking-table-01.jpg)

**Status:** Accepted

#### Purpose

This reference defines the character of the Thinking Table. Its value lies in demonstrating how operational reasoning naturally unfolds on a spacious, calm working surface where digital tools and handwritten thinking coexist.

#### What it teaches

- Large uninterrupted surfaces encourage structured reasoning.
- Paper and digital tools naturally complement each other.
- Open space is more valuable than additional technology.
- Warm natural materials improve psychological comfort during long sessions.

#### What should influence Management OS

- A spacious Thinking Table.
- Calm organization.
- Warm wooden materials.
- Minimal visual clutter.
- A large reasoning surface.

#### What should not be copied

- Specific furniture.
- A particular monitor or keyboard.
- A domestic-office setup.
- Decorative objects.

Management OS should adopt the principles of the workspace rather than imitate its physical appearance.

#### Canonical candidacy

This reference is currently one of the strongest candidates for the canonical Thinking Table.

It represents the physical philosophy of the Workbench rather than its visual styling. Its Accepted status does not yet promote it to the intentionally small [`99-canonical/workbench`](../../99-canonical/workbench/README.md) library.

## Rejected references

### [`workbench-desk-thinking-table-03.jpg`](../../00-moodboard/workbench-desk-thinking-table-03.jpg)

**Status:** Rejected

This tidy workspace emphasizes document handling and routine office work rather than operational reasoning. It lacks a large uninterrupted thinking surface, evidence organization, visual reasoning, expansion space for ideas, and calm operational composition.

It represents task execution rather than investigation.

#### Lesson

A Thinking Table is not simply an organized desk. It is intentionally designed to support reasoning.

The rejected image remains in the Moodboard as review evidence. It must not be used as positive Desk direction or promoted into the canonical library.
