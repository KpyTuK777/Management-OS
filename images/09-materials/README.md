# Materials

## Purpose

Define how material character communicates operational role and depth.

## Questions answered

- Which surfaces feel appropriate for map, notebook, source, and control?
- How much tactility is useful?
- When should material recede?

## Supports

Role recognition, warmth, material honesty, and inhabited professionalism.

## Belongs here

Wood, metal, paper, glass, textile, and restrained composite references with stated functional meaning.

## Avoid

Luxury veneers, fake realism, material for decoration alone, illegible surfaces, and inconsistent metaphors.
