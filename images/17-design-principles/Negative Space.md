# Negative Space

Empty space is working capacity.

It separates operational roles, preserves hierarchy, accommodates growth, and gives the eye somewhere to rest. It is not leftover area and should not be filled to demonstrate feature completeness.

Healthy negative space surrounds Current Situation, meaningful clusters, and focused inspection. Wasteful space appears as margins or chrome that reduce the Workbench without improving orientation.

Preserve emptiness inside the operational composition; return unnecessary application space to the Workbench.
