# Visual Gravity

Visual weight must follow operational importance.

Current Situation receives the strongest gravity because it holds the working synthesis. Material risk and contradiction may temporarily challenge it. Inbox remains available but calm. Collections, navigation, Watson, metadata, and administration recede until needed.

Gravity is created through position, scale, contrast, space, and material—not by enlarging every urgent label. If several elements demand equal attention, the composition has failed to express judgment.

Ask: where does the eye land, what does it understand next, and which element can safely wait?
