# Watson Presence

Owner-driven. Watson-assisted. Risk-interruptible.

Watson quietly organizes natural contribution and appears when explicitly asked. It may interrupt only for material risk, serious contradiction, authority conflict, likely source failure, or a consequential decision conflicting with strong Evidence.

Watson has no permanent claim on Workbench space. Its interpretations look proposed, remain editable, and never impersonate owner judgment.

Presence should feel like a precise thought offered at the right moment—not a conversation the owner must maintain.
