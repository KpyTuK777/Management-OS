## 3. Controlled terminology

### Context

- **Professional context** — the meaning required to continue professional work.
- **Context Envelope** — the sole canonical continuity contract owned by the
  Workbench Canonical Domain Model.
- **Navigation context** — a professional destination plus continuity and return;
  never a second envelope.
- **Information space** — canonical residency/responsibility boundary; never a
  context container.
- **Practice context** — explicitly non-production context with separate identity,
  provenance, authority limits, and return.

### Owner

- **Domain owner** owns canonical identity and state contract.
- **Authority holder** may perform a bounded consequential act.
- **Source owner** owns source truth and its mutations.
- **IA steward** maintains residency rules without owning resident domain truth.
- **Responsible actor** is accountable for an obligation but does not thereby gain
  domain authority.

### Workspace scope

**Workspace Scope** is the organizational authorization and configuration context.
**Workbench** is the Matter-centered inquiry and professional work environment.
They are not synonyms.

### Monitoring

- **Monitoring activity** is professional observation over time.
- **Monitoring layer** is its navigation architecture context.
- **Monitoring information space** is the primary residency for its contract,
  observations, Outcome Assessment, and stabilization basis.
- **Observation contract** defines baseline, sources, interval, guardrails, and
  interpretation authority.
- **Monitoring lifecycle posture** is Product-owned Matter posture, not activity or
  location.

### Professional-cycle terms

**Symptom Detection**, **Understanding**, and **Implementation** are professional
cycle activities, not Matter lifecycle states. Implementation remains source-owned.

### Epistemic terms

- **Evidence** is attributable material used as a basis.
- **Confirmed claim** is a proposition whose required confirmation contract passed.
- **Fact** is an epistemic classification, not a new domain entity.
- **Observation** is an attributable noticed or measured condition.
- **Hypothesis** is a testable explanatory proposition.
- **Judgment** is an accountable professional interpretation.

### Qualified movement terminology

- **Matter lifecycle transition** changes Product-owned Matter lifecycle state.
- **Operational Transition** coordinates Decision intent with source-owned change.
- **Navigation transition** moves between professional contexts.
- **Presentation transition** changes representation only.
- **Transition Layer** is the canonical navigation layer whose dominant object is
  Operational Transition.

Unqualified wording must not substitute for any of these four meanings.

## 4. Source-owned implementation invariant

Management OS owns the Decision-to-Operational-Transition handoff, commitments,
dependencies, approved adaptations, guardrails, monitoring contract, Outcome
Assessment, and stabilization judgment. Source systems own task/process execution,
deployments, physical implementation, and source-owned completion.

> Source-reported completion does not establish Outcome, stabilization, or Matter closure.

## 5. Practice–production boundary

1. Simulated evidence carries explicit practice-context provenance.
2. Retrospective reconstruction is never represented as knowledge available at the
   historical time.
3. Practice artifacts never enter production automatically.
4. Admission into production is a separate governed interaction.
5. Attempt history remains attributable and is never rewritten.
6. Learning authority never implies production authority; returning from practice
   restores the prior production Context Envelope without merging envelopes.
