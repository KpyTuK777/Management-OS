# 2. Conformance Contract

## 2.1 Descriptor and ownership

A qualification binds one realization identity/revision to every applicable
exact approved source revision, scope, applicability ledger, evidence,
independence declaration, findings, verdict, validity conditions, publication
state, and immutable history. Scope cannot hide obligations, and unsupported
capability is degradation rather than inapplicability.

The framework owns qualification judgment only. M6.1–M6.14 retain every
qualified obligation and ID family. Qualification cannot amend a source, grant
canonical ownership, or approve source change.

**Result: Pass.**

## 2.2 Lifecycle and verdict

Scoped → Evidence Prepared → Under Independent Review yields Conformant only
after complete pass; otherwise Non-conformant. Source, realization, capability,
scope, or evidence changes expire or supersede reliance. Correction creates a
new attributable qualification and preserves prior verdicts.

Draft and Under Independent Review have no verdict. Partial, provisional,
averaged, or self-certified conformance is prohibited.

**Result: Pass.**

## 2.3 Evidence, failure, and requalification

Evidence is exact-revision-bound and covers applicability, positive and
negative paths, degradation, interruption, recovery, changed capability,
provenance, reproduction conditions, limitations, conflicts, retention, expiry,
and supersession. Realization producer, evidence producer, independent reviewer,
and canonical authority are separated.

Failures identify exact obligation, owner, revision, realization, scope,
divergence, risk, correction condition, and requalification evidence.
Requalification is mandatory after any invalidating change. Exceptions are
bounded and cannot make failed evidence pass, redefine Conformant, waive
mandatory meaning, or create precedent.

**Result: Pass.**

# 4. Governance Contract

## 4.1 Ownership and responsibilities

Governance owns admission, routing, gates, version relationships,
compatibility, deprecation, migration, withdrawal, exceptions, traceability,
and history. It owns no governed meaning. Only each canonical owner may approve
changes to its source.

Proposer, implementer/evidence producer, independent reviewer, canonical
authority, and governance custodian have explicit, non-overlapping authority.

**Result: Pass.**

## 4.2 Descriptor, lifecycle, and change

Every change records identity, exact sources/revisions, proposer, evidence,
rationale, class, ownership, impact, review, authority disposition, resulting
revision, transition, and history.

Proposal, scope validation, impact assessment, independent review, authority
disposition, publication, migration/requalification/deprecation, and terminal
supersession/withdrawal are separate gates. No gate implies a later gate.

Clarification, extension, incompatible change, deprecation, migration,
withdrawal, and correction are distinct. Compatibility is
predecessor-revision-specific; extensions cannot overload IDs; mixed-revision
publication is blocked; history is preserved.

**Result: Pass.**

## 4.3 Conflict escalation

Contradictory obligations, ownership disputes, incomplete cross-owner approval,
conflicting evidence, or meaning-changing compatibility failures return to
canonical authority. Governance records and blocks them; it cannot weaken,
average, silently prioritize, or reinterpret obligations.

**Result: Pass.**
