# Workbench Philosophy

The Workbench is a place, not a page.

It begins sparse and grows with accepted material. Current Situation remains central. Evidence, observations, hypotheses, conversations, contradictions, and questions occupy meaningful positions. Inbox rests beside or below the map. Watson remains optional and contextual.

The Workbench does not expose the entire data model. It reveals only what supports the present act of reasoning.

Reject dashboards, permanent columns, equal cards, document flows, and layouts that sacrifice spatial context for component regularity.
