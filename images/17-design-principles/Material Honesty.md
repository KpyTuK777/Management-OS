# Material Honesty

Material treatment must explain role.

Paper suggests authored, editable, or collected work. Dark structural surfaces frame the workspace. Metal or glass may identify tools or precise controls. Texture provides depth only when it remains subordinate to legibility.

Digital material is interpretive, not a simulation. A notebook may communicate continuous capture without reproducing every physical detail. A pinned note may communicate provisional placement without becoming theatrical.

If material exists only to decorate a container, remove it.
