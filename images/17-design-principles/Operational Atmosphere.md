# Operational Atmosphere

Management OS should feel like entering the office of an exceptional operational manager.

The atmosphere is warm, quiet, professional, intellectual, and inhabited by work. It uses restrained light, depth, material, and working traces to support prolonged thought. It never becomes a themed set.

Atmosphere succeeds when it lowers cognitive noise and increases the desire to study the situation. It fails when decoration becomes memorable before the operational material.

The emotional target is confidence without performance and seriousness without sterility.
