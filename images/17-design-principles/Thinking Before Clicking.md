# Thinking Before Clicking

Management OS optimizes for the quality of judgment, not the number of interactions completed.

The interface should make relationships, uncertainty, provenance, contradiction, and current understanding perceptible before asking for action. Controls appear at the moment they serve thought. They do not compete with the material they manipulate.

Efficiency is valuable when it removes clerical effort. It is harmful when it rushes interpretation, hides uncertainty, or turns investigation into task completion.

Ask whether an action clarifies thought or merely increases interface activity.
