# Operational Inbox

## Purpose

Define the owner’s permanently available place for natural contribution.

## Questions answered

- How should writing feel effortless and unclassified?
- How can text, files, screenshots, links, voice, and ideas coexist?
- How does Inbox remain available without covering the map?

## Supports

Flow, ownership, informality of capture, and confidence that anything relevant can be added.

## Belongs here

Working journals, professional notebooks, margin systems, composers, and integrated capture surfaces.

## Avoid

Modals, floating overlays, record-creation forms, mandatory classification, assistant-gated submission, and decorative notebook mimicry.
